model list [--project PROJECT]
  --project PROJECT: a project's name or number; optional. Only this project
Read the models your projects may use, by provider
Prints: `models:`, each project's providers and models with their windows, efforts, speeds and prices, then the accounts and `digest: models`
--json: the record models.
Key: none.
Use these speed names with --speed SPEED or --sub-agent-speed MODEL:SPEED
on chat start, goal start or task amend. A faster speed costs more.
Choose a listed speed or standard; leave it out to use the charter's
speed or the model's default.
Its digest is the models' read, not the accounts'; to wait on the
accounts, use the digest account list prints.
