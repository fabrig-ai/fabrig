task text TASK [--field specification|instructions] [--from OFFSET]
  TASK (task): a number; required. The task's number
  --field specification|instructions: one of specification or instructions; optional. Which source to read; specification when absent
  --from OFFSET: a number; optional. Byte offset printed by the previous chunk; zero when absent
Read a chunk of a task's specification or effective instructions
Prints: The source words, byte offsets and total size; then a continuation call or `no more`
--json: the record task-text.
Key: none.
Each chunk keeps UTF-8 characters whole. Follow the printed call
until no more. Instructions use the task's override, or its charter
at the task's recorded settings revision.
