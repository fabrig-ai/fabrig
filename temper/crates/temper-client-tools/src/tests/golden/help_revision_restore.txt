revision restore REVISION [REASON] --key KEY
  REVISION (revision): a number from 0 to 4294967295; required. The revision to restore
  REASON (reason): text, at most 1048576 bytes, or - to read stdin; optional. Why; restores revision R when absent
Propose the settings as REVISION left them, to the owners of its items
Prints: `proposed restoring revision R as proposal P to role K`, then each item left out
--json: the record restore.
Key: --key KEY, required.
A forgotten revision exits 2, naming the oldest kept. A revision
too large to restore exits 2; restore a later revision instead.
Read the proposal with proposal show PROPOSAL, then decide it
with proposal decide PROPOSAL accept --key KEY.
