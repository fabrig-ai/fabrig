- **Fates:** *Stays*, unchanged; *Shortened*, kept above with the rows or assertions named, the rest held below; *Folded*, its assertions carried by another test kept above; *Gone*, every assertion held below.
- **Holders:** *Added* marks a holder the goal's change adds, under the name given here; every other holder already ran at that commit. A change that places an added holder under another name or in another binary edits its row here. Binaries are named as `tools/tiers/layers.toml` names them, a library's own tests by its package's name. Line numbers are that commit's, in `temper/bin/temper-box/tests/suite/`.
