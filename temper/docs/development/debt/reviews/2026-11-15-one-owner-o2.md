# one-owner O2: the count

2026-11-15. Milestone O3 of `temper/docs/design/draft/one-owner.execution.md`: the figures `temper/docs/design/draft/one-owner.md`, 6 asks of the pilot, goal 3313, at its two endpoints: `59f00cd180`, the commit its goal started from, and `b948f40d6d`, its landing on main. Other goals changed 19 of hearth's files between them, none of them a file counted here.

Counted by one script at both endpoints, parsing the source read from git: it blanks comments and literals, follows `use`, re-exports, aliases and `Self`, and types a field read from parameters, annotations and constructors. It is not a compiler: it expands no macros or derives. The field reads it couldn't type, 41 at each endpoint, were checked by hand, as were seven sites at each endpoint. Lines are Rust lines that are neither blank nor comments; characters are characters other than whitespace.

## 1. In one page

- **What a change touches:** a change to `Component`, `DataChange` or `Collision` touched 29 production functions and items at the start and 19 at the landing. In tests, 52 and 50: the builder's copies' fixtures went, and the client's new pins came.
- **Copies:** the pairs of types the builder's translations linked fell from 191 to 30. The records' 151 pairs fell to 1, the outcome's envelope. Configuration's 40 fell to 29, its positioned documents and declarations, which the builder compiles into render's types. The translation's code fell from 2,784 lines to 1,006, and its `_back` functions from 177 to 41.
- **Quality:** the review finds the code clearer and cheaper to change. A data value's new field touches three places, not six, and a declaration's new field name two, not five. It found eleven things to improve and no bug. Two of them are repetitions the goal added, one a copy kept without its reason, and one a step moved into the vocabulary.
- **Size:** the area's production fell 3,073 lines (8.9%), with the new vocabulary's 1,562 counted. Its tests grew 936 lines: the client's printer gained 4,444 lines of byte-for-byte pins, and the builder lost 3,512 of the copies' tests. The design grew 2.1% in hearth, and the model 3.0%.
- **Cost:** 108 minutes, from 19:47 to its landing at 21:34. The goal agent spent 673.86 and its changes 942.30, in the record's unit: 1,616.16 in all.
- **The figure:** where design-economy's pilot (`temper/docs/development/debt/reviews/2026-11-14-design-economy-e2.md`) took 0.9% from render's production for 2,238.81 over two goals, one owner took 8.9% from the area's for 1,616.16 in one. The copies were where the lines were.

## 3. Copies

A pair is a render type and a records or configuration type that one of the builder's translation functions builds from the other (`temper/docs/design/draft/one-owner.md`, 6.2). Nested types count as pairs of their own, so a record with five fields of copied types makes six pairs.

| of them, tests calling an inverse, or other boundaries | 88 | 29 |

The pairs left, with the reasons the goal gave (`hearth/docs/development/debt/D013-records-three-times.md`, "The copies kept"; `hearth/docs/design/domain/render/README.md`, 5.1):

- **Configuration's documents and declarations (26 pairs),** from `Document` and `Value` to `Declaration`, `Field` and `Text`: they carry the byte offsets their decoder orders refusals by, and the builder compiles them into render's, expressions included, rather than copying them.
- **Configuration's faults (2):** `Fault` and `Shape` translated into render's `Decode`. The note gives no reason of their own (section 4, finding 6).
- **The position (1):** configuration's `Position`, the JSON tokenizer's byte offset, against render's line and column.
- **The outcome (1):** the client's `Outcome` adds `Unavailable`, an envelope of its own (`hearth/docs/design/client/README.md`, 3.2).

The projections the client's records carried, a schema fault's typed names, a filter's name and arity, an export map's absent entries, became the printer's, so they left no pair (`temper/docs/design/draft/one-owner.status.md`).

## 4. Quality

One code review of the goal's diff, by the kinds of `temper/docs/design/draft/design-economy.md`, 7.3.

**Clearer and cheaper, with examples:**

- **The printer** reads as free functions over the owner's types, not as a mirrored tree of types with methods. The builder's answer passes render's values through: a spec reaches the client as `Record::Spec(Box::new(spec))`, where the client had its own copies of the spec's types and five translation functions for them.
- **A new field of a data value** touched six places: render's type, the client's copy and its printer, the builder's translation, its `_back` inverse and a fixture. Now it touches three: the vocabulary's type, render's digest of it, and the printer.
- **A new declaration field name** touched five places: configuration's names and its field table, the builder's translation, render's three matches, and a `_back` inverse. Now it touches two: the vocabulary's three matches, and the field table's row.
- **A new error kind** no longer needs the client's copy, the builder's 600-line `translate/errors.rs` or its inverse tests. It still needs the vocabulary's kind, the printer, and two matches the goal added (finding 2).

## 6. Reading the figures

- **The copies were the cost.** The first pilot cut concepts inside render and bought 0.9% of its lines; this one cut the copies around it and bought 8.9% of the area's, and a third of the places a change to its records touches. The tests grew, but by pins of what the client prints, which the copies' inverse tests stood in for.
- **The vocabulary role held.** The machines that decode render's values name them from the vocabulary, the printer prints them, and no layer holds a copy but where the note says why. Finding 7 shows the role's edge needs watching: a step moved with its value.
- **What's left is small and named:** findings 1, 2, 3, 6 and 7, each a local change, and mmdebstrap's `tail::Class`, which repeats a decision of render's, outside this pilot. Adoption beyond the pilot is `temper/docs/design/draft/one-owner.md`, 8's open question.
