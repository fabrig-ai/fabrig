# Temper slow-test survey

A read-only survey of 2026-11-23 at main `ea29d3cbd0`, for `temper/docs/design/draft/slow-tests-down.md`. Its classes are a reading of `skein/docs/foundation/testing-strategy.md`, not measurements, and its line numbers describe that commit. Failure counts come from the after-landing records of 2026-11-16 to 11-19, 148 runs, each failing line cut at its end, so they are lower bounds.

**`process-temper` and `browser-temper` supply the automatic coverage in this scope.** `temper-box::suite` has no automatic runner in the supplied configuration, including its scratch-binary wiring smoke, native listener checks, and forge process checks. The highest-value reductions in the scheduled suites are to shorten the Forgejo and cluster stories to their process boundaries, and to give Chromium fixtures that start at the browser assertion. The highest-value recovery for the gate is to extract the replayable checks from `suite` and the managed GitHub support modules. The supplied records give run-level timestamps and truncated failure lists. They give no current per-test wall or CPU. A demonstrated duplicate can be retired with zero new gate test work. Every new lower scenario has an unmeasured cost. The failure-worlds budget report makes the same distinction between historical costs and present savings.
