# limits-raise: four budgets near their limit rise by half, for now

The request: `inputs/request.md`. The user, on 11-08: "if any limit is close to causing reds, just raise it 50% and will revisit". Four `cpu_suite_s` figures in `tools/tiers/budgets.toml` rise by half: `[gate.temper.fuzzy]` 120 to 180, `[browser-skein]` 145 to 217.5, `[browser-temper]` 85 to 127.5, `[process-temper]` 360 to 540. ## What was decided

- **One task, as the request asks,** effort medium (the request's "low"; a spec's scale starts at medium). Every new line is the cut's, word for word in the spec (E1 to E19), each old block checked by the cut to match its file exactly once: the implementer copies and checks, and decides nothing but the options the spec names.
- **The design text rides in the spec, not ahead of it.** The cut would otherwise push the new design lines first and leave **Not yet** entries for `budgets.toml`'s old figures, for the task to delete. The request asks that each document "change with" its figure; one commit keeps the file and every statement of it in step, with no transient gap to record. The departure from the implementer text's "Not yours: any `docs/design/`" is stated in `notes/common.md`, for the named lines only.
- **The header's line 1 is edited in place.** It gives the reason for all four slow-tier figures (x1.5 of 10-30's measurement; process-temper at its design aim). The request keeps that reasoning for the other entries (browser-jig) and says nothing is added to or removed from the file, so line 1 gains one sentence naming 217.5, 127.5 and 540 as 145, 85 and 360 raised by half, rather than a new comment line. Line 8 takes the request's own example word for word.
- **What changes in the documents,** as stating a figure as current: `tools/tiers/design.md`, 7.3 (Temper's fuzzy entry at 180; the strategy's figures by the word of 11-07 but for Skein's `default` and now Temper's `fuzzy`) and its status's 7.3 paragraph; temper's `domain/testing.md`, 1 (30 s and 180 s; within its 540 CPU s), 2.6 (the suite budget is 540, the 6-minute aim raised by half), 6.1 (the focused 30 s stands until the user sets it; the fuzzy suite's is 180 s) and 11's three items naming 360; `testing.status.md`'s **Not yet** 2.6 entries (within 540; the seven `test-speed` runs, 287.1 to 433.7 s, now all within it, four over the 360, the gap of an unmeasured tier standing) and **Departs** 6.1 (the fuzzy median 125.579032 against the strategy's 120, within the 180; the departure from the rule stands); `client/browser.md`, 5's default (127.5); `browser.status.md`'s **Not yet** 4.3 (127.5, and the rest of the tier's share 82.650100 s, 127.5 less the four reading stories' 44.849900); `STATUS.md`'s browser and fuzzy paragraphs, which summarize those two status entries.
- **What stays, on purpose:** the strategy's 30 and 120 (`skein/docs/foundation/testing-strategy.md`, 8: the rule this is an exception to); `testing.md`, 2.6's *6-minute aim* (the design's aim, the starting point the budget is raised from); `.config/nextest.toml`'s comment of 10-22 (a history, of each tree's provisional figures); the draft status `temper/docs/design/draft/bundle-loading.status.md` (*83.069911 of its 85 CPU s*, a measurement at `668f7cd3e5`); every `docs/development/` account (`browser-budget.md`, `playground-figures.md`, `limits-heavy.md`, `limits-fuzzy.md`, `limits-worlds.md`: dated measurements), the debt reviews and the plans; hearth's, smith's, skein's and jig's own figures. No design document states browser-skein's figure: it changes in `budgets.toml` alone.
- **No test pins an old figure** (request item 4): `tools/tiers/test_run.py` and `test_cpu.py` write their own budget files in temporary roots, and no test or boundary check reads the tracked file: only `tools/tiers/run` (CPU mode and `--verify-all`) and whatever measures a gate share (`tools/tiers/design.md`, 7.3) do. The task proves the file instead with its own reader, `cpu.py`'s `budgets()`, which takes floats (`217.5`, `127.5`); it builds nothing.
- **Runs:** no slow tier, no `tools/tiers/run` (it builds, `--verify-all` included); the raised budgets are read after landing. The task runs the reader, old-forms, the citations check and the outward boundary test (`fabrig-boundary`, `outward`), which reads every tracked file.
- **No question:** the figures and the wording are the user's, in the request.

No stored format, protocol or public interface changes; `budgets.toml`'s schema is unchanged.
