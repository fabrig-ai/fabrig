# The reference, beside the page

## Goal

The stock page "How temper builds temper" joins temper's compiled documentation, and "See how it works" opens it in the document pane, in Split, with no chat made. On a phone it is alone, as Draft, the composer under it.

**Departs from the implementer's text:** this task writes `temper/docs/help/`, by copying the note below and adding the index's lines.

Acceptance:

- **The page** is the row of `notes/contracts.md`, 1. `the_table_is_the_directory` and `each_page_keeps_its_head_template_links_and_voice` pass with it, and the kind's template refuses a `way` whose sections aren't numbered from 1.
- **The file** is byte for byte `temper/docs/plan/look-round-8/notes/how-temper-builds-temper.md`.
- **The figures are placeholders:** a test holds "- Merges: —", "- Agent runs: —", "- Workers: —" and "Figures to come." in the page, and no `://` anywhere in it.
- **The tree** is `notes/contracts.md`, 4, from *With `reference` set*, in both shells.
- **Modes:** `Act::Mode` at `Configure` follows `notes/contracts.md`, 3. "Chat" folds the pane and keeps the group; "Draft" gives the pane the main's `MODE_DRAFT`.
- **Nothing is made:** opening and changing mode push no `Request`, and `Chats.start` stays `None`.
- **A chat's document is unchanged:** `temper-client-view`'s document tests pass with no edit but a moved helper's path.
- **The bundle takes one page:** the view names `temper_docs::HOW_TEMPER_BUILDS_TEMPER`, never `PAGES` or `find`, and `heavy cargo clippy --target wasm32-unknown-unknown -p temper-client-view -- -D warnings` passes.
- **The gallery** draws `configure-reference` (Split) and `configure-reference-draft` (Draft), with `Holds` for each.
- **The rules** marked `pane` in `notes/contracts.md`, 5, and the picture's two, are in `temper.css` under `/* ==== ui: settings ==== */`.
