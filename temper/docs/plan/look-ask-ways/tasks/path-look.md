## Goal

Draw a path as the look request `ask-temper-ways` draws it: across with
the landing branch's bar at its end and a goal's branch dimmer in a
stop's place, down the page where the box is narrow, and, when pressed,
each stop a group carrying its act, the open one ringed with its card's
caret under it (`temper/docs/design/client/README.md`, 5.16, *Drawn*,
*Its layout* and *Pressed*). Replies keep drawing paths through it,
unpressed.

Acceptance:

- `paths::path` takes `Option<&Pressed<'_>>` and `paths::pressed_stop`
  exists, as `notes/contracts.md`, section 2 writes them; the drawing node
  carries `WAYS_PATH`; its data carries the flags byte and the open stop.
- At a box 720 pixels wide, the scene of the request's three paths (the
  tables already in `src/tests/paths.rs`) puts each mark, word and bar
  where the request's `01.html` and `03.html` draw them in their wide
  SVG, in whole pixels, rounded down: You approve's stops at 12, 92, …,
  652; main's bar at 712, "main" ending there at 88; Draft first's twelve
  places at 12 + 640·i/11, the goal branch's bar the seventh, in a
  `WAYS_LANDING` group, "goal branch" at 88 over it; marks and words at
  40, 22, 106 and 131 as 5.16 says; the line's parts above the dotted
  line `WAYS_RISE`, the rest `WAYS_BASE`, rising over 0.42 of a step.
- At a box 388 pixels wide (a phone's), the same paths run down the page
  as the request's narrow SVG draws them, offset to centre a 360-pixel
  column: the dotted line at 178 of the column, judgement at 206 with
  words after, code at 150 with words before, 40 pixels a stop from 16,
  main's bar 38 under the last, the scene's height its own (388 for You
  approve). A box from 560 wide is across where its stops stand at
  least 48 pixels apart, else down the page.
- Pressed with stop 3 of Agents review open (its review): each stop but
  the bar a `WAYS_STOP` group with its `WAYS_HIT` area carrying the act
  `[index]`; a `WAYS_HALO` ring of 15 behind the review's mark; its words
  with `WAYS_OPEN`; across, the scene 154 high with a `WAYS_CARET` point
  under the review; down the page, no caret. The fallback's items are
  buttons bound to their `stops` entries, the review's `Expanded`; a bar
  stays an item. `pressed_stop(&[3])` is `Some(3)`; empty or longer data,
  `None`.
- Unpressed, as in a reply, no group, hit, halo, caret or act, and the
  fallback stays a list of items.
- `look.rs` gains contracts section 2's tokens in order, after the last;
  `WAYS_BAR` becomes `WAYS_MAIN`, named `temper-ways-main`.
- `temper.css`: the request's path rules (`notes/common.md`, the look
  request's `overlay.css`, from `.temper-ways-path` to
  `.temper-ways-stop[aria-expanded=true] text`, and its phone part's
  path lines) land under `/* ==== ui: chats ==== */`, in place of today's
  `#app .temper-ways-*` rules there, as `notes/common.md` says of
  selectors skein draws otherwise: the expanded stop's words by
  `.temper-ways-open`; the halo shown by being drawn; the wide and narrow
  SVGs by the scene's layout; `.temper-ways-rise` the signal's faint
  tone, `.temper-ways-caret` the card's surface and line. The report
  lists each rule as landed, translated or dropped, with why.
- The view's focused tests pass, the bounds' tests among them (`DATA`,
  `MARKS`, `SCENE_BYTES` recomputed and still the limits' maxima in
  `src/limits.rs`).

## Steps

1. Read 5.16 and contracts section 2; read `paths.rs`, `layout.rs`,
   their tests and skein-ui's `scene.rs` (`group`, `act`).
2. Grow the data (flags, open stop) and `path`'s signature; `views.rs`
   passes `None`.
3. Lay out across and down the page by width; draw the bars as ends
   and goal branches; two tones of line; pressed groups, hits, halo,
   open words and caret.
4. Make the fallback's items buttons when pressed.
5. Tests: extend the existing tables with the request's coordinates
   across (720) and down (388), pressed and unpressed, and
   `pressed_stop`. Break a rule once by hand for each (the landing bar
   not dimmed, the caret drawn down the page) and see its row fail.
6. The rules in `temper.css`.
7. `heavy cargo nextest run -p temper-client-view --lib -E
   'test(/paths/) | test(/views/) | test(/limits/)'`; then the package's
   focused tests; `heavy cargo nextest run -p temper-client-gallery`
   and regenerate what it reports stale with its own tool.
8. `cargo fmt`; `python3 -I -B temper/scripts/old_forms.py`; `heavy cargo
   clippy -p temper-client-view -p temper-client-gallery --all-targets --
   -D warnings`. Commit with the trailer `Look-request: ask-temper-ways`
   and push.
