## Goal

Give the gallery the opening's states, so that the render check after
landing draws every state the request names, those its pages show and
those its message says in words (`temper/docs/design/client/browser.md`,
7.2: `ask-first-run` and the `ask-ways-*` rows;
`temper/docs/design/draft/look-requests.md`, 4, *States in words*).

Acceptance:

- `ask-first-run` delivers its project's `ways` (the first run's world at
  its step 7: Agents review current, To begin's first step left, the
  person an owner), and its `Holds` check the region "Ways of working",
  the three tabs in order with "now" on Agents review, the panel "Agents
  review" and "Keep it", in place of "Ways to begin".
- Each row of 7.2's `ask-ways-*` is a state, named as there, in
  `names.txt` (sorted) and `src/samples.rs`, with a `Holds` in
  `src/tests.rs` checking what its row says: `ask-ways-approve`,
  `ask-ways-draft`, `ask-ways-card`, `ask-ways-card-person`,
  `ask-ways-yours`, `ask-ways-refused`, `ask-ways-member`,
  `ask-ways-kept`. A state reached by a press (a tab, a stop, "Use this",
  "Keep it") presses it through the page's acts, as the gallery's other
  pressed states do, never by setting the domain's state by hand.
- The `ways` deliveries are hand-made `WaysPanel`s in the gallery's
  worlds, as `goals-solo`'s Goals deliveries are: the three stock ways'
  names and lines (`temper/docs/design/protocol/configuration.md`,
  13.10), their paths' words and actors as the request's three pages
  draw them (and Draft first's over a repository with CI and both tiers,
  `ask-ways-draft`), their cards as the engine's derivation words them
  (`temper/crates/temper-engine-domain-ways/src/path/cards.rs` and
  `derive.rs`), the review's model "Claude Opus 5.5 · high" as the
  request's `04.html` has it. A refusal is jig's `Judged` refusal with the
  forge's reason for too few approvers as the client world's ways story
  receives it.
- The gallery's focused tests pass within its budget: a state adds
  little; if the gallery's tests pass their budget (`browser.status.md`,
  7.3, records them over it today), the report gives the time before and
  after.

## Steps

1. Read 7.2's rows, the gallery's `ask-*` and `goals-*` states and how
   their worlds deliver documents.
2. Add the `ways` deliveries, `ask-first-run`'s change and the eight
   states; each `Holds` as its row says.
3. `heavy cargo nextest run -p temper-client-gallery`; regenerate what it
   reports stale with its own tool and run it again.
4. `cargo fmt`; `heavy cargo clippy -p temper-client-gallery --all-targets
   -- -D warnings`. Commit with the trailer `Look-request:
   ask-temper-ways` and push.
