# Keep asking, a person's fact

## Goal

A person's facts gain `KeepAsking { project, subject, at }`: Keep asking pressed on a need's reason line, for the item of that subject, so that the client stops offering Stop asking for it for 7 days. The fact crosses the wire and the store as `ToBegin` and `GoalFrom` do, and a second one of the same project and subject replaces the first. The client's use of it is `needs-why`'s.

Acceptance, by `temper/docs/design/domain/forge.md`, 5.5, and `temper/docs/design/client/README.md`, 4.6 (*A person's facts, the subject `dismissed`*):

1. **The shapes** of `temper/docs/plan/ui-reaches/notes/contracts.md`, section 5, exactly: the wire's `KeepAsking` and `PersonFact::keep_asking`; the store's same, its `Dismissed { party, what }` taking the variant; the forge's `PersonFact::KeepAsking { project, subject, at }`. Both schemas regenerated by the drift test (`notes/common.md`, *Generated files*; this task is the third schema writer).
2. **The kit's derives:** `Kind` and `Subject` gain `PartialOrd, Ord`, nothing else of the kit changing.
3. **The decision** (`dismissed.rs`, `dismiss`): a `KeepAsking` where the party holds one of that project and subject with another `at` erases that row and saves the new one, marking the subject `dismissed`, answered dismissed; one held already as it is: dismissed, nothing written; the bounds as they are, a replacement never counting as a new row. `erase_project` takes `KeepAsking`'s project.
4. **The store's key and record** (`keys.rs`, `records.rs`): the fact encodes and decodes as its siblings, its subject by the store's `ClassSubject`.
5. **The service** (`family.rs`): `Dismiss` of a `keep_asking` reaches the forge as `PersonFact::KeepAsking`, the subject through `temper_protocol_reaches::subject_from`; `Dismissals` answers it back through `subject_record`.
6. **The client's compile-forced arms:** a `match` on the wire's `PersonFact` in `temper-client-domain` or `temper-client-view` (its dismissals' readers: To begin and the goals started from chats) passes `keep_asking` by, as a fact neither reads; named in the report. Using it is `needs-why`'s.
7. **Rows,** no new test function: the forge's dismissal tests (`tests/dismissed.rs`), a `KeepAsking` saved, the same again writing nothing, one of another `at` replacing it (one erase, one save, one mark), another subject kept beside it, all erased with their project; the store's key and record round trips, a row each; the service's family test of `Dismiss` and `Dismissals`, a row; the codecs world's fuzzy store test draws the fact; the client world's `dismissed.rs` idempotence story (about line 208), a row of a replaced `KeepAsking` read back on a second device.
8. **Bite:** let a second `KeepAsking` of the same subject add a row, see the replacement row fail; restore.
9. Runs, counts in the report: `heavy cargo nextest run -p temper-protocol-reaches -p temper-engine-domain-forge -p temper-protocol-store -p temper-client-protocol-wire -p temper-service -p temper-codecs-world -p temper-client-domain -p temper-client-view -p temper-client-world`; the fuzzy profile for `-p temper-codecs-world -p temper-protocol-reaches`; the drift test without writing; clippy for every package changed.
10. Status: delete the **Not yet** entry naming `ui-reaches/keep-asking` in `temper/docs/design/domain/forge.status.md`.
