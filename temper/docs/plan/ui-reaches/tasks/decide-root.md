# Decided for any task: the caller's person, the tool's kinds, paths and `under`, its words

## Goal

Any task whose question would reach a person may call `decided`, and its asks are judged and written too, so that the question that follows says why it came. The tool takes the ten kinds, the paths a choice would change and the item it decides under; its Ask names the reason; the charters tell goals and chats to call it before asking, `Asks: always` included. The engine world holds the draft's stories of who decides and what reaches the person.

Acceptance, by `temper/docs/design/domain/forge.md`, 5.9 (*The caller's person*, *Nothing answers for anyone*) and 5.10, and `temper/docs/design/domain/agent.md`, 4.3 (`decided`):

1. **The caller's person** (`route/decided.rs`, `person`): no longer a tracked task alone. A task whose requester is a person: that person. A task whose requester is a task: the person of the chat serving it (`served_chat`), where that chat's requester is a person. Otherwise none, answered ask by the root with `reach: None`, nothing asked of the forge. `always` is whether the caller's words hold a line `Asks: always` (the four forms the function tests today); such a caller goes to the forge with `always: true`, no longer answered ask by the root.
2. **The tool** (`temper-engine-smith/src/calls.rs`): its description "Ask whether you may decide this for your person. Carry on only if decided; otherwise ask them with message, as a question."; its schema's `kind` the ten names of `temper_protocol_reaches::Kind::name`, `paths` an optional array of at most 16 strings, `under` an optional integer 1 to 20; decoded into `DecidedCall` (`notes/contracts.md`, section 6): a path empty, past 256 bytes or with a leading `/`, more than 16 paths, or `under` outside 1 to 20 refused at decoding, saying which, as its other bounds are.
3. **Its words** (`answers.rs`): "Decided: carry on with TOOK." as today; ask, "Ask: REASON. Ask with message, as a question; if your brief says [Requested by person N], with delegate, a person task addressed to person N.", REASON by the reach exactly as `agent.md`, 4.3 gives them: `Wait` "KIND always waits for your person"; `AskAway` "KIND asks your person, even when away" or "changes to path set N ask your person, even when away"; `Ask` with Away off "KIND asks your person while they are here" (or the set's form); `Always` "your words say Asks: always"; a reach of `Decide`, or `Ask` with Away on, answered ask "your person's decided list is full for today"; no reach "no person is asked through you: ask your requester". KIND is the kind's name.
4. **The charters:** `stock/chat.txt`'s goal paragraph and its chat paragraph, and both places of `stock/coordinator.md`: before asking the person anything, call `decided` with the question, its options, the one you'd take, why, its kind, the paths the choice would change, and `under` where an item of the project's classes with words covers it (`see settings project-classes`); on Decided carry on and ask no one; on Ask, ask as the answer says. The kinds in one sentence: `money` for funds and budgets, `irreversible` for rewriting history, deleting work that hasn't landed or a store's data, `rules` for a change to the project's classes, a landing rule or a standing role's rules, `other` for what fits none of the ten, which always wait too. `Asks: always` no longer tells a run never to call it: "If your words hold the line Asks: always, every answer is Ask." The stock brief's room check (`tests/stock_brief.rs`) passes; `tests/stock.rs`'s `a_goal_is_told_to_call_decided_before_it_asks` and `asks_always_is_told_never_to_call_it` (renamed for what it now checks) assert the new words.
