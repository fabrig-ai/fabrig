# Away without kinds; `decided` judged by the project's classes, for every row

## Goal

The forge judges `decided` by the project's classes (`temper/docs/design/domain/forge.md`, 5.10) with the person's Away as one input, writes a row for every call it judges, decided or asked, keeps rows 7 days under a limit of 1,024 a project that never erases a row inside its day, and answers both lists in the `decided` document. Away loses its kinds everywhere: the forge's row, the store, `SetAway`, the wire and the client's controls that set them. The root's caller rule and the tool's words are `decide-root`'s; this task keeps them as they are.

Acceptance, by `temper/docs/design/domain/forge.md`, 5.8 and 5.9, `temper/docs/design/protocol/configuration.md`, section 7, and `temper/docs/design/client/README.md`, 4.6 (`SetAway`, the subjects `away` and `decided`):

1. **The shapes** of `temper/docs/plan/ui-reaches/notes/contracts.md`, section 4, exactly: the wire's `SetAway`, `AwayRow`, `Decision`, `Asked` and `Decisions`; the store's `Away`, `ReachReason`, `Reach`, `Decided` and `CallDecided`; the forge's `Away`, `Event::SetAway`, `DecidedCall`, `Event::Decided` (with `always`), `Decided`, `Request::Decided`, `CallPayload::Decided`, `DecisionView`, `AskedView`, `DecisionsView` and the constants; `Limits::decided_per_project`; the root's `Application::SetAway` and `CallAnswer::Decided`. `DecisionKind` and `DECIDED_PER_PROJECT` go, every caller naming `temper_protocol_reaches::Kind` and the limit. Both schemas regenerated by the drift test (`notes/common.md`, *Generated files*); the wire's `SetAway` validation of `kinds` goes from `temper-client-protocol-wire/src/lib.rs`.
2. **Away** (`away.rs`, `set_away`): 5.8's cases with `until` alone; `on_at` as it is.
3. **The judgement** (`away.rs`, `decided`): `temper_protocol_reaches::judge` of `d.project_classes(project)`, an `Ask { kind, paths, under, away, always }` from the call and the event, `away` the party's row `on_at` the decision's wall. A `took` past the options: answered ask, `reach` none, no row. Otherwise a row (5.9, *Its row*): decided, `took` the index and the options and `why` kept; asked, `took` none, no options, `why` empty; both with `reach`. `Request::Decided` carries the taken words or none and the reach.
4. **Kept and bounded** (5.9, *Kept 7 days, bounded by a limit*): before it writes, the decision erases the project's rows whose `at` is more than `DECIDED_KEPT_NS` before its wall; at `decided_per_project` rows, it erases the project's oldest row whose `at` is more than `DECIDED_DAY_NS` before its wall; with none such, it writes nothing and answers ask, `reach` the judgement's. The family's capacity is `decided_per_project` times `judge_projects` (`Limits::decided`), and the restore's check (`restore.rs`) refuses a project past `decided_per_project` and the family past that product.
5. **The limit,** `forge.decided_per_project`: a field of the forge's `Limits`, 1,024 in both profiles of `temper/crates/temper-service/src/limits.rs`, named by the overrides as its siblings are (`configuration.md`, section 7, *Overrides by dotted name*).
6. **The document** (`decisions_view`): the asking party's rows of the project; `rows` the decided ones with the taken option's words, `asked` the asked ones, each the newest `DECISIONS_SHOWN` in number order; `cut` where either left a row out. `Overturn` of an asked row is refused unknown. The service encodes `Decisions` and `Aways` with the new fields (`temper-service/src/family.rs`), and translates `SetAway` without kinds.
7. **The call's kept answer:** `CallPayload::Decided { took, reach }` in the store's `CallDecided` (`records/entries.rs`); the root's `CallAnswer::Decided { took, reach }` (`store.rs` measures it, `translate.rs` maps it both ways); `route/forge_route.rs`'s arm for `forge::Request::Decided` passes `reach` on, one line; `route/decided.rs` hands `always: false` and answers its own asks with `reach: None`, its person rule as it stands (`decide-root` widens it); smith's `decided` decoding maps its five names to the kit's kinds with `paths` empty and `under` 0, and its answer's words stay as they are (`answers.rs`, the arm takes `{ took, .. }`).
8. **The client's compile-forced deletions,** nothing more of the client: `temper-client-domain`'s `AwayRow.kinds`, `DEFAULT_KINDS`, `forge::away::ask`'s `kinds` (`ask(project, on, until, limits)`), `pages::away::Act::Kinds` and its uses (`temper-client-view/src/frame.rs`); the view's Settings rows "Kinds of decision" and their checks, words (`words.rs`), uses of their styles and tests (`tests/settings.rs`: `defaults_with_no_row`, `a_kind_writes_through_set_away`, `locked_stays_unticked_and_disabled` and the bit table, cut to what remains: the heading and "Away until"); the client domain's away tests of kinds (`tests/away.rs`, `kinds_keep_on_as_it_reads`); the gallery's and the client world's away fixtures' `kinds` (`temper/tools/gallery/src/worlds.rs`, `temper/tests/client/src/front/away.rs`, `tests/suite/away.rs`'s `off_keeps_the_choices`, now of `until` alone); the terminal shell's family fixture (`temper-client-shell-terminal/src/tests/family.rs`); every fixture building a `v1::Decision` or `v1::Decisions` given a fixture `reach` and empty `asked`. Settings' *While you're away* keeps its heading and its "Away until" select; `away-client` redraws the rest.
9. **Rows,** no new test function, each existing test kept to what it checks and moved to the classes where it named bits:
   - the forge's `tests/away.rs`: the bit tests become one table over Agents review's stock (the project holds no kind 8): `wording` decided with Away off; `implementation` asked with Away off and decided with it on (`away` true in the reach); `money` and `other` asked, `Wait`, Away on; and, with a kind 8 table held in the forge's settings (as `classes-value`'s rows hold one), `dependency` at `AskAway` asked with Away on, and a `wording` call whose path touches a set at `AskAway` asked with reason `Set(1)`; each row asserting the answer, the row written (decided or asked, its reach) and the subject marked;
   - `the_sixty_fifth_erases_the_lowest_and_numbers_rise` becomes the bound's table, under a test's own `decided_per_project` of 4: a fifth call with the oldest row past its day erases it; with every row inside its day it writes nothing and answers ask; a row older than 7 days goes at the next write; numbers rise; another project's rows stay;
   - `an_option_past_the_list_answers_ask`: no row, no reach;
   - the overturn tests: an asked row's number refused unknown, as a row;
   - the service's family tests (`temper-service/src/tests/family.rs`): `SetAway` without kinds, `Aways` without kinds, `Decisions` with a decided row, an asked row and `cut`, beside their existing cases;
   - the store's tests: the round trips of `Away`, `Decided` (decided and asked) and `CallDecided` (with and without a reach), in their existing tables; the codecs world's fuzzy store test draws the new fields;
