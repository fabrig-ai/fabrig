## Decisions

- **Charter 11 and template 5 keep their numbers;** 3 stays unused. The client, the
  reference, `ask.txt`'s readers and the design already name 11 and 5, and a renumbering
  would move every one of them for nothing a person sees.
- **The settings family's tools stay,** for a charter of a deployment's own: `settings`,
  `read_docs` and `propose_settings`' configuring form, with its `note` source, are
  declared as today to a charter whose tools hold the settings family and not the asking
  bit. No stock charter holds them now. Removing them is beyond the request; their
  stories run a charter of the test world's own (`notes/contracts.md`).
- **The configuring acceptance's scripted runs move to charter 11** with what they check;
  a check `see` has no address for stays with the settings charter's story of the same
  subject.
- **The standard profile's brief stays 354,988 bytes:** it was charter 3's room less the
  titles' framing; charter 1's room, now the smallest, still holds it with a margin, so
  no limit or limits page moves.
- **Migrations first, the removal last:** every task but the stock's moves its readers to
  charter 11 while charter 3 still exists, so each lands green; the stock's removal needs
  them all.
