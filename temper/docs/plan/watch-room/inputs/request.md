# watch-room: two open tabs don't lock everyone else out

temper/qa's walk with a real model met `temper ctl: the engine at ADDRESS did not answer within 30 s` on `task list`, `inbox`, `proposal show` and `settings show`, with a browser holding two tabs. An investigation on fake boxes, 2026-11-06 at `3a034de1ca`, reproduced it in real Chromium and found the mechanism. Its scripts and evidence are outside this repository, so the words here are what you have. **Run, unless marked read.**

**What happens.** A new `temper ctl` must get its own `Me` watch before it sends the command it was asked for (`temper/crates/temper-client-shell-cli/src/host.rs`, `client_progress` and `root_requests`). The core's watch budget, 17, was full: `temper/crates/temper-engine-domain/src/limits.rs`, `VIEWS` and `core_limits`, install `watchers: 17` and `watchers_per_party: 17` for the whole deployment, sized from one client's bound. So that watch came back Busy, again and again (`jig/crates/jig-domain-core/src/routing/view/routes.rs`; `jig/crates/jig-domain-views/src/watch.rs`, `open`). `jig/crates/jig-client-domain/src/watches.rs`, `ended`, treats Busy on its own watch as retryable, marks it Behind and takes the link Offline, while the stream stays healthy. The client gives up at 30 s with the requested read never sent.

**The evidence:**

- Home at 1440 wide and a long chat at 420 wide, open or reopening: 10 timeouts in 144 probes, across all four reads and `me`, and five concurrent `chat start`s. With two settled tabs doing sends, accepts and rejects, 359 probes all succeeded, at most 0.82 s. **So it's the opening.**
- A snapshot during the wait: 17 core watches held; 8 free mounted-call slots; no waiting HTTP heads; 8 or 9 of 12 server connections; the root with room; the engine's main thread at 0.19 CPU s in 31 s, asleep.
- Closing the Home tab five seconds into a stuck read took the watches from 17 to 9, and the read finished 1.5 s later.
- With only those two constants at 64, in three repetitions of the same opening: 51 probes, no failures, at most 0.076 s. The core held up to 25 watches, more than the old budget. **64 was a test of the cause, not a sizing.**
- A JSON `views.watchers_per_party` didn't change the behaviour, since temper's root installs `VIEWS` directly.

So one person with two tabs can lock out their own third tab, `temper ctl`, a second person and every walk. **Read, not run:** a page refused this way may show its documents as absent: `temper-client-view/src/pages/home.rs`, `chats`, says "No chats yet" for absent rows, and `frame.rs`, `behind`, says Behind. The walk saw exactly that on Home. temper/ui owns those words and is fixing them apart.
