# look-round-20: what every task follows

- **The request binds:** `temper/docs/plan/look-round-20/inputs/request.md`, all of it, *For every task of this plan* first. It wins over a spec where they differ.
- **The look requests** are commits on `origin/look-requests`, found by their trailers, since the branch moves: `git log origin/look-requests --grep='Look-request: NAME'`. When cut: `night-mode` `3c5333485a`, `to-begin-two-steps` `4332a26265`. Read a request with `git show` alone, its whole message first. Read its pages and `overlay.css` from its own commit (`git show COMMIT:PATH`), never from the branch's tip: `to-begin-two-steps` redraws `night-mode`'s pages. Leave `origin/look-requests` itself alone. Both requests landed in `look-round-19`; this plan repairs what the render check after it found (`temper/docs/design/draft/look-requests.md`, 4 and 7).
- **The design this plan follows,** written by this cut: `temper/docs/design/client/README.md`, 3.5 (*One layout for every chat*; *Home*'s *Needs you* and *Chats* left out while To begin shows; *Ask temper's page*, its composer on a phone) and 5.3 (the night switch; the tab bar on a phone); `temper/docs/design/client/browser.md`, 2.5 (*The project's frame*; *Day and night*: *Shadows keep their colour* and *The switch*) and 7.2's `home-begin-with-needs` and `home-begin-settings-left` rows. Each task deletes the **Not yet** entries naming it and `look-round-20` (`README.status.md`, `browser.status.md`), and edits no other design text but what its spec names: that departs from the implementer's text, which closes `docs/design/`.
- **Trailers:** every commit carries its request's trailer, `Look-request: NAME`, one line directly above the other trailers (`temper/docs/design/draft/look-requests.md`, 3); a commit that serves two requests carries both lines.
- **The account:** each task's last commit carries a table `| Item | Result |` (`look-requests.md`, 5): one row per item its spec gives it, landed and where, differs and why, or deferred and to whom; its last row the render check's, which follows landing, owned by temper/ui, since compiling, prints and focused tests establish no rendered equality.
- **Runs:** focused and fuzzy alone, each build and test through `heavy`, one command at a time. No `tools/tiers/run`, no `<tier>-<tree>` profile, no `scripts/browser.sh`, no browser story run, no walk, no render check, no screenshot, no throwaway driver: temper/ui's render check follows landing (`look-requests.md`, 4). The browser tier keeps its twelve functions; no task here adds a browser story.
- **Prints:** the journeys' prints are the view's markup replayed from their recordings, and `the_committed_journeys_are_their_print` (`temper/tools/journeys/src/tests.rs`) compares them byte for byte. A task whose view change could move a print reprints every journey from its recording, `heavy cargo run -p temper-client-journeys` with no argument (`temper/docs/design/client/journeys.md`, 5.1), and commits what changed; no task here records a journey again (`again`), since no recording's act or read changes. The prints link `temper.css` by path, so a stylesheet change leaves them as they are; the gallery's print is made by its tests and never committed.
- **Tests stay cheap:** no new test function where a row or an assertion of an existing table test holds the case; a spec names the test. A stylesheet change takes no test of its own: its check is the render check after landing.
- **`temper.css`:** two tasks change it, in different places. `night-shadows` edits the night set alone (the block on `html:root[data-night]:has(#app)`, about lines 148 to 283). `tabs-and-switch` edits the switch's rules in the project's frame block (about lines 1408 to 1431), the chat layout's phone rule (about line 1793), the Split's folded rail rules (about lines 1946 to 1953 and 2007 to 2009), and adds its new phone rules under `/* ==== ui: phone ==== */` (about line 3084), never at the file's end. Each leaves the other's lines alone; replaced rules are removed where they stand.
- **Before pushing:** `cargo fmt`; `python3 -I -B temper/scripts/old_forms.py`; `python3 -I -B tools/citations/citations.py --base origin/main`; clippy as the implementer's text says, naming every package changed (none for a task that changes no Rust).
- **Leave alone:** `origin/look-requests`; the drafts (`temper/docs/design/draft/*.md` and their status files); `docs/help/`; `overlay.css` and every request's hand-made directory, which are not on main.
