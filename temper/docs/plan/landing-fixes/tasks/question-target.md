# A producer is told which task to ask

## Goal

The request's item 4. A producer's first question went to its own task, twice in two, and the answer was `Authority refused: task reference; `. The charter says "message your change", the brief says `[This task: N]`, and nothing joins them. Its target is the task its brief's `[Requested by task N]` line names. The refusal's bare `; ` isn't a reason lost: `authority_findings` writes `; ` after every finding, so every authority refusal ends that way. It is a separator with nothing after it, and it goes.

Acceptance:

- Authority refusals list their findings with `; ` between them and nothing after the last: `Authority refused: tool family control`.
- `change_hint`'s sentence follows the findings after `. `: `...(task authority.delegation.kinds). For a change to a repository, call change with what to change.`
- A `message` refused for `Finding::Reference` reads, whole: `Authority refused: task reference. Your brief's [Requested by ...] line names your requester; [This task: N] is you.` Other findings beside it stay in the list, before the sentence.
- `coding.txt` says, right after `message your change only in the question form.`: `Your change is the task your brief's [Requested by task N] line names, never [This task: N].`
- In a world, a producer messaging its own number gets the refusal, with no question asked and no hold; messaging the number in its `[Requested by task N]` line asks its change.
