# ignored-engine: rules every task keeps

- **The request** is `temper/docs/plan/ignored-engine/inputs/request.md`:
  twelve ignored temper tests brought back into the suite that selects
  them. Read it for the why; your spec and the design sections it names
  are what you build. Every one of the twelve is in the focused suite
  (`.config/nextest.toml`, `[profile.default]`: temper's packages, binary
  `suite`, no `fuzzy::` prefix).
- **Un-ignore first:** your first commit removes the `#[ignore]` lines
  your spec names and nothing else; run each test alone
  (`heavy cargo nextest run -p PKG --test suite -E 'test(=NAME)'`, checking
  the count is 1) and quote in the report the first failure each shows, or
  that it passed as it stands.
- **Each failing test, by its cause:** the product where the product is
  wrong against the design the reason cites; the world, joint or fake
  where the test lacks a capability. A test whose design has moved is
  rewritten to the design as it stands now, citing the section, never
  deleted and never weakened to pass (no assertion dropped that the
  design still asks).
- **Still ignored only for a missing feature:** if the cause is a
  feature that isn't built and is larger than your task, the test keeps
  its `#[ignore]`, its reason rewritten to name the missing design
  section (`#[ignore = "<doc>.md, <section>: <what is not built>"]`).
  Then write `temper/docs/plan/ignored-engine/notes/<your task>-ignored.md`
  (a file of your own, only in that case): the test, the section, what
  is missing and what you observed. Say so in your report's first lines.
- **No other ignored test is touched,** including jig's restore stories
  (`jig/tests/core/tests/suite/settings_restore.rs`) and
  `forge_routes/hints.rs`'s `two_change_hint_request_profile`.
- **No test moves to another suite,** and no budget, timeout, nextest
  filter or configured figure changes. A test you un-ignore costs what
  it does: report each one's time from nextest; one over its guide
  (`skein/docs/foundation/testing-strategy.md`, 8: 1 s for a focused world
  test) is examined for its cause (8.1, question 2) and named in the
  report, never moved or ignored again for its cost.
- **A capability added to a world, joint or fake** has a test of its own
  only where nothing else holds it; the un-ignored story is normally its
  first user. A new test must bite (the implementer's text).
- **No stored format and no protocol changes:** no wire schema, no store
  record, no journal entry changes shape. A fix that would need one is a
  `Question:`, never a quiet departure.
- **Fix in the lowest tier that shows it:** a jig or skein fault seen in a
  temper story is reproduced in jig's or skein's own tests (a row of an
  existing table or step test there), fixed there, and named in the
  report. jig never names temper, and skein names neither.
- **Only the focused and fuzzy suites run.** Never `tools/tiers/run`, a
  `<tier>-<tree>` profile, `scripts/browser.sh`, a browser, process or
  live tier story, nor a walk, render check or throwaway driver.
- **Decide, don't ask.** A small edit just outside your file list is
  made, and named in the report. A `Question:` is only for what truly
  blocks your acceptance.
- **Before calling a focused or fuzzy failure "outside the change",** run
  it once on the pristine base. If main passes and your branch fails, it
  is your branch's.
- **Not yours:** `docs/design/` but the status lines your spec names, the
  drafts, and `docs/help/` but a generated page your change makes stale,
  regenerated by its own tool.
