# parallel-faults: what eight producers at once met

From fabrig/qa's parallel replay of a real plan on a deployment, 2026-11-06 at `a37eb8e4c0`: 8 producers at once, the machine fine, 10 of 11 changes merged into the goal's branch, then stopped at a blocker. A rerun waits on item 1. Causes are from a driver's reading of source and logs, not reproduced: **reproduce each first in the lowest tier, and say plainly if one's wrong.** Ranked; 1 and 2 must land.

1. **Blocker: a stalled hold strands a finished producer.** A change held `its wait stalled at producing`: `waiting_on_agent` resets `since` only while asking, and `temper-service/src/limits.rs` gives 30 minutes (about 57 in; why not at 30 isn't traced, so trace it). Its run then finished `accepted` with a change form, its branch at a commit on the forge, and the change stays `head: null`, `producing`, for good. **A producer simply working for 30 minutes is normal,** and the other pipeline has no such limit. So a producer with a live run isn't stalled, by `temper/docs/design/domain/forge.md`, 8.4; and a result that arrives on a held change isn't lost: it's taken, or the hold says what to do. Reproduce with a small profile, whose `stall` is 30 s in `limits.rs`.
2. **That hold reaches nobody.** The inbox was empty, the goal wasn't woken, `task show` said "Waiting for news", and it sat idle for 1 h 43. A hold on a goal's change wakes the goal, or reaches a person, by the design's rule for a hold.
3. **A failure snapshot put a stray log in the goal's branch.** `.cursor-focused.log`, 8,373 lines, from the commit `Save unfinished work`. Inferred: it stages untracked files. A snapshot must not carry a file a change has no business with, by `temper/docs/design/protocol/git.md`: at the least, it's kept out of what lands, and it should honour what's ignored. Say what rule you took.
4. **Snapshots drop a run's local commits.** A parked run's local commits don't reach its next run, and a resume's words name them. Related, seen once: a producer parks with uncommitted files, the host commits "Save unfinished work"; on resume it runs `git reset --soft BASE`, commits and `deliver`s: the pushed head sits on the save, the fold is lost, and the pull request's `Head:` names it. Delivery should say what it pushed, and the words mustn't name a commit that isn't on the branch.
5. **`timed out during response idle (HTTP 200)`:** 19 failed runs, 73 minutes, on two tasks only, with requests of 456 to 801 KB, though a run at 823 KB worked. The largest cost in the run, and its cause is unproved. Read smith's idle bound against what a large request at high effort does before its first byte, and what a comparable client allows. The bound's a figure the user owns: if raising it's the answer, say by how much and why, and make the change in `smith/`, as the stock's limits go, with the measurement. **Don't guess: if it can't be settled from reading, say what evidence would, and leave it.**
6. **Tasks with `after` start detached or half-merged,** 3 of 3, from the runs' own words, not proved. See `landing-fixes`' early start. If it's the same fault, say so; if the words only, a word.
