# per-wake: the client's charter carries its parts and its LLM choice a later

## Goal

The client wire 19 and the client's domain carry a charter's parts and an LLM choice's
`later`, in place, and the engine's translation and the client's carry both whole, both
ways (`contracts.md`, section 8): a settings read shows a charter's parts, a settings change
sets them, and a `Create`, an `Amend`, a template and a task's document carry `later`.

Acceptance:

- `client-v19-part1.schema` and `client-v19.schema` carry the records and fields of
  `contracts.md`, section 8; the wire limit `charter_parts` (ceiling 8) bounds a charter's
  parts as `charter_documents` bounds its documents; the family's version is unchanged;
  generated code and goldens are regenerated by `jig/tests/codec-families.rs`'s drift
  writer (`SKEIN_CODEGEN_WRITE=1`, `jig-client-wire-world`), never by hand.
- `jig_client_domain` has `CharterPart`, `Charter.parts`, `LlmLater`, `LlmChoice.later` and
  `REASON_NAMES` as `contracts.md`, section 8 gives them, each citing
  `jig/docs/design/client/domain/contract.md`, 3.2 or 5.1 at type level.
- The client's translation (`jig-client-protocol-link/src/translate/settings.rs`,
  `wire_charter`/`restore_charter`; `translate/llm.rs`, `wire_choice`/`choice`) and the
  engine's (`jig-protocol-engine-clients/src/translate/settings.rs` and
  `settings/change.rs`, a charter both ways; `translate/llm.rs`, `choice`, `people` and
  `indices`, the people's and the tasks' choices both ways) carry parts and `later`
  whole. A part's `on` crosses as its bits, unchecked here: the core refuses an empty or
  unknown one (the parts-core task). A `later`'s model and effort names map to indices and
  back as the first choice's do, with the same refusals at the same kind of path.
- A test in `jig-protocol-engine-clients` pins `jig_client_domain::REASON_NAMES` to
  `jig_domain_tasks::REASON_NAMES`, name by name.
- Rows, each in the table that holds its kind today: a charter with two parts round-trips
  from the core through the wire and back (`src/tests/settings.rs`, beside
  `charter_documents_round_trip_from_core_through_wire` and
  `change_settings_keeps_the_charter_documents`); the contract's (`jig-client-protocol-link`,
  beside `charter_documents_round_trip_through_the_contract`); a choice with a `later`
  round-trips, and a `later` naming an unconfigured model is refused as the first choice's
  is (`jig/tests/engine-clients/tests/requests/llm.rs`'s table); the client-wire world's
  settings and LLM-choice records with the new fields populated.
- The scripted client engine and the client test records encode and build the new fields
  (`jig/testing/jig-client-scripted-engine/src/codec/values/`,
  `jig/testing/jig-client-test-records/src/settings.rs`).
- Delete from `jig/docs/design/client/domain/contract.status.md` the sentence "3.2, 5.1: the
  client's charter carries no parts and its LLM choice no `later` (...)." and nothing else
  (a departure from the implementer's text, which closes `docs/design/`: the status must say
  what is built when this lands). The 5.1 sentence after it is the later task's.
- `heavy cargo nextest run -p jig-client-protocol-wire -p jig-client-domain
  -p jig-client-protocol-link -p jig-protocol-engine-clients`, the client-wire world and the
  engine-clients world pass; `heavy cargo check --tests` is clean for every package that
  builds a client `Charter`, `CharterParts` or `LlmChoice`, temper's among them.
