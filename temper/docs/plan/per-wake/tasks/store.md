# per-wake: the store keeps a charter's parts, a later choice and a task's reasons

## Goal

Store what the surface added, in place in jig's settings, tasks and people families
(`contracts.md`, section 6): a charter's parts, an LLM choice's `later` in all three
families, and a live task's `reasons` and `offered`. Until now the codecs drop them
(the surface's forced defaults); after this task each round-trips.

Acceptance:

- `settings-v1.schema`, `tasks-v9.schema` and `people-v14.schema` carry the records and
  fields of `contracts.md`, section 6 (the live task's two `u32`s in `TaskRun`, beside
  `due_reason`); the families' versions are unchanged; generated code, layouts and goldens
  are regenerated by their tools (`jig/tests/codec-families.rs`'s drift writer with
  `SKEIN_CODEGEN_WRITE=1`, the store's layout drift writer), never by hand.
- The domain codecs carry them both ways: `encode_charter`/`decode_charter`
  (`src/codec/settings.rs`) the parts in order; the tasks family's `llm`/`read_llm`
  (`src/codec/tasks/value.rs`), the settings family's template choice and the people
  family's choice the `later`; the live task's encoder and decoder `reasons` and `offered`.
  The surface's forced defaults in those decoders are gone.
- A decoder refuses, as its family refuses any value out of shape: a `reasons`, `offered`
  or `on` with a bit outside `Reasons::ALL` (`!is_known()`), an empty `on`, and a
  `later` whose sub-agents pass `MODELS`.
- The store's round-trip table (`src/tests/settings.rs`, its `Record` rows) holds a
  charter with two parts; the tasks family's round trip holds a task whose choice has a
  `later` and whose `reasons`/`offered` are neither default; the people family's an ask
  with a `later`. One row each refuses an `on` of bit 14 and an empty `on`.
- `jig/docs/design/protocol/store.md` is read by `src/tests/words.rs`: those tests pass
  unchanged (this plan's edit is in section 5's opening, outside the words they read).
- Delete from `jig/docs/design/protocol/store.status.md` the sentence "5: the settings
  family's charter keeps no parts, no LLM choice keeps a `later`, and a live task keeps no
  reasons or offered kinds (`schema/settings-v1.schema`, `tasks-v9.schema`,
  `people-v14.schema`)." with its bullet, and nothing else (a departure from the
  implementer's text, which closes `docs/design/`: the status must say what is built
  when this lands).
- `heavy cargo nextest run -p jig-protocol-store`, the store-codecs world and the store
  world pass; `heavy cargo check --tests` is clean for every package that links
  `jig-protocol-store`'s changed records.
