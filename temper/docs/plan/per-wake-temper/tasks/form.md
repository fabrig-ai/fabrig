# per-wake-temper: temper's settings form carries a charter's parts and a later choice

## Goal

A settings file, `temper ctl settings apply` and `settings show … --json` carry a charter's
`parts` and a template's `llm.later` whole, both ways, through the form, the wire and the
client adapter, and the decoder refuses a malformed part at its path before anything is
sent. Today the decoder refuses both as unknown members and every round trip drops them
(`settings_form::wire`, `w::CharterParts.parts` empty and `later: None`;
`temper_client_tools::jig::settings_adapter`, the same).

Acceptance:

- `settings_form::view` has `CharterPart`, `Charter.parts`, `LlmLater`, `LlmChoice.later`,
  `REASON_NAMES` and `ALWAYS_OFFERED_NAMES` as `contracts.md`, section 1 gives them, citing
  `temper/docs/design/client/tools.md`, 5.3 at type level.
- The file decodes and encodes both members as `contracts.md`, section 2 gives them:
  optional, encoded only when present, so every value without them prints as it does
  today and no golden that holds such a value moves.
- Both carry whole through `wire.rs` (a form charter to `w::CharterParts.parts` and back; a
  form choice's `later` to the wire's and back) and `settings_adapter.rs` (the client's
  `c::Charter.parts` and `c::LlmChoice.later`, both ways). A part's `on` crosses as its bits.
- The decoder's refusals are `contracts.md`, section 2's table, each at its path and in
  its words.
- `FIELDS` (`src/fields/jig.rs`) gains the rows: a charter's `parts`; a part's `on` and
  `instructions`; an LLM choice's `later`, with `later`'s `main` and `subagents` resolved as
  the choice's own are (a shape that recurs has its rows once). The form's step tests
  (`src/fields/tests.rs`: the shape inventory, the generated decode, the required members)
  pass with an example of each, and `temper/docs/help/reference/settings-fields.md` is
  regenerated by `temper/crates/temper/src/tests/settings_fields.rs`
  (`the_page_is_what_fields_renders`, `SKEIN_CODEGEN_WRITE=1`), within its 32,768-byte bound.
- Rows in the existing tables of `temper/crates/temper-client-tools/src/tests/jig/settings.rs`,
  none new as a test of its own but the pin below:
  - the apply and propose fixture that carries `DOCUMENTS` through both wire paths
    (near line 102): a charter with two parts, one `on` `["first"]`, one `on`
    `["question", "question_owed"]`, carried whole;
  - the show-JSON-to-file-to-apply equality (near line 557): a charter with parts and a
    template whose choice has a `later` come back equal;
  - the malformed-members table (near line 281): one row per refusal of `contracts.md`,
    section 2's table, each naming its path and words;
  - the bound table (near line 217): parts past `charter_parts`, a part's instructions past
    their bound;
  - a test pinning `settings_form::view::REASON_NAMES` to `jig_client_domain::REASON_NAMES`
    and `ALWAYS_OFFERED_NAMES` to `jig_domain_tasks::ALWAYS_OFFERED_NAMES`, name by name.
- `reference_goal_template_resources_survive_apply_and_show` (the same file) still passes.
- Delete from `temper/docs/design/client/tools.status.md` the bullet "**Not yet:** 5.3: a
  settings file's charter takes no `parts` and a template's model choice no `later` ..."
  and nothing else (a departure from the implementer's text, which closes `docs/design/`:
  the status must say what is built when this lands). The next bullet, on `settings show`'s
  text, is the `show` task's.

- Owns the files in the header.
- Readers, each edited only by the new field's name and its empty value (`parts:
  List::with_capacity(0)`, `later: None`): any literal of the form's `Charter` or
  `LlmChoice` the compiler names elsewhere (the survey found none outside the header's
  files), and `temper/tools/stage-one`'s tests if a fixture of theirs must name a member
  to keep its meaning (none expected: both members are optional and printed only when
  present).
- Pre-approved: if `settings_file.rs` in `temper-client-tools` holds its own copy of a
  bound the form now checks, take the form's and say so. If the host's `propose_settings`
  (`temper-engine-smith`, which shares the decoder) has a test whose expected words a new
  refusal changes, edit that row's expected text only and say so; its other files are not
  yours.
- Not yours: `src/render.rs` and `src/lib.rs`'s refusal words (the `show` task's), the
  reference's `settings.json` (the `focused` and `reference` tasks'), any jig file.
