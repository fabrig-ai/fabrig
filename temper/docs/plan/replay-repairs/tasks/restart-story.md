# A restart walks settings across a model's setting

## Goal

The engine's domain world holds the restart of the request's item 1: a box whose settings hold an application's setting or a project's policy and a model's setting restarts, its startup walk paging across them, without *startup continuation advances in walk order* (`temper/crates/temper-engine-domain/src/loads.rs`). `setting-order` (merged before you start) made `Setting`'s derived order the store's key order, which is the fix; this task writes the story that would have caught it, below the real loop, and carries temper's readers of the new order.

This task departs from the implementer's text in one way: the story passes from the start, since the fix is merged; it bites by moving `Model` back before `Application` in `jig/crates/jig-domain-people/src/settings.rs` by hand, seeing the story fail with that panic, and restoring.

Acceptance:

1. In `temper/tests/engine/domain/tests/suite/loads.rs`, the real store seeded with two settings records in one subfamily, a project's policy (or an application's setting) and a model's setting, is walked over `Range::Core(root::CoreRange::Settings)` one row a page, on open and after `store.crash(91)`. Each page keeps the world's `page_rules`, and each page goes through `loads::begin` and `loads::loaded` under the startup waiter (`STARTUP`), the second with the first's `next` as its `after`, without a panic. Default shape: `sweep_repeats_the_paged_people_walk_on_open_and_after_crash` becomes a table over `(records, ranges)`, its people row as now and the settings row new, renamed for both (`paged_walks_repeat_on_open_and_after_crash`), with the `loads` step added to every row.
2. With `Model` moved back by hand, the settings row panics with *startup continuation advances in walk order*; the report quotes it.
3. temper's readers of the new order pass: an assertion of a settings order that put a model's setting before an application's setting or a project's policy follows the new order. Focused tests pass in `temper-engine-domain` (`--lib`), `temper-engine-domain-world`, `temper-service` (`--lib`), `temper-engine-smith` (`--lib`), `temper-client-tools` (`--lib`), `temper-client-world` and `temper-client-journeys`.
