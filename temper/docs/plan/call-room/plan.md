## Critical path

`jig-room` (120) → `reference-room` (180) → `room-close` (30): 330 implementer-minutes. `large-argument`, `smith-room` and `report-through` start at once beside `jig-room`.

## What the cut decided

- **The figure** is picked by test, in `reference-room`, from `smith-room`'s measurement. By reading, T at 131,072 and 98,500 is about 21 MB against a ceiling of 33.5 MB, so the larger is expected to stand.
- **`request.md`'s "20,000 for Opus"** is `StockModel.output`, a price per unit. A stock model's token output is the literal 8,192. Neither moves, and the mechanism needs neither to.
- **`hub.charter_bytes` alone doesn't hold a spec:** smith's own `run_bytes` capped a charter at 65,536. It now follows the payload, and the stock's stays at 65,536.
- **The batch stays in jig's `standard`,** whose `tasks.batch` is already 160; `jig-room` moves it to the reference's `limits.json` if a store check refuses it.
- **`limits.json` can't reach a worker,** since it merges objects and `workers` is an array: `room.json` holds that and the agent's figures.
- **No stored format or protocol changes:** every figure stays under its generated ceiling, so there's no question for an owner.

- `temper/tests/real-loop` and `temper/crates/**`, written by `limits-worlds`, whose trunk hasn't landed: no file is edited here but by what a test reads, and T at init is unmoved.
