## Goal

Put the records of a path, a project's stock ways and a view on the
client's wire, so that the service can encode what the root makes and the
client can watch `ways` and read `View`, each side built to this task.

Acceptance:

- `client-wire-v1.schema` holds every line of `notes/contracts.md`,
  section 3, verbatim, `Watch`'s `ways` and `Read`'s `view` last in their
  enums; `CLIENT_VERSION` stays 16.
- The generated code, its bounds and its goldens are regenerated by the
  codec's own generator, and `temper/tests/codecs`'s drift test
  (`client_wire_schema_matches_generated_code_and_goldens`) passes.
- `lib.rs` has `ways_panel`, `decode_ways_panel`, `view_answer` and
  `decode_view_answer`, as `changes_panel` and `decode_changes_panel` are;
  `src/tests.rs` round-trips the smallest and the full `WaysPanel` and
  `ViewAnswer` through the committed goldens, as
  `changes_panel_round_trips` does, and a `Watching` of `ways` and a
  `Reading` of `view`.
- The codecs' fuzzy decoder table
  (`temper/tests/codecs/tests/suite/fuzzy/client_wire.rs`) holds
  `decode_ways_panel` and `decode_view_answer`.
- Every package that matches `Watch` or `Read` exhaustively still builds,
  each new arm answering as its neighbours do for a subject or read it
  does not serve.
