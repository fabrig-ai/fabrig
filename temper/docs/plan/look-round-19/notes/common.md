# look-round-19: what every task follows

- **The request binds:** `temper/docs/plan/look-round-19/inputs/request.md`, all of it, *For every task of this plan* first. It wins over a spec where they differ.
- **The look requests** are commits on `origin/look-requests`, found by their trailers, since the branch moves: `git log origin/look-requests --grep='Look-request: NAME'`. When cut: `ask-temper-start` `e9da9f87a8`, `night-mode` `3c5333485a`, `to-begin-two-steps` `4332a26265`, in that order, oldest first. Read a request with `git show` alone, its whole message first: what its pages show, and the states in words they do not. Read its pages from its own commit (`git show COMMIT:PATH`), never from the branch's tip: a later request edits an earlier one's pages (`to-begin-two-steps` redraws `ask-temper-start/` and `night-mode/` to two steps). Leave `origin/look-requests` itself alone.
- **The design this plan follows,** written by this cut: `temper/docs/design/client/README.md`, 3.5, *To begin* (its card, its two steps, *The rail and the tab bar*), *Its look, kept per browser* (under *A chat's terminal*), and 5.3 (*While To begin shows*, the night switch); `temper/docs/design/client/browser.md`, 2.5, *Day and night*, 7.1 and 7.2's `home-begin-*` and `night-*` rows; `temper/docs/design/client/journeys.md`, 9, `project-landing`'s row and the paragraphs on the three requests. Each task deletes the **Not yet** entries this cut wrote for it, naming `look-round-19` and the task (`README.status.md`, `browser.status.md`, `journeys.status.md`; an entry two tasks share loses its task's half, the last one deletes it), and edits no other design text but what its spec names: that departs from the implementer's text, which closes `docs/design/`.
- **Trailers:** every commit carries its request's trailer, `Look-request: NAME`, one line directly above the other trailers (`temper/docs/design/draft/look-requests.md`, 3); a commit that serves two requests carries both lines.
- **The account:** each task's last commit carries a table `| Item | Result |` (`look-requests.md`, 5): one row per overlay rule, page change and state in words its spec gives it, landed and where, differs and why, or deferred and to whom; its last row the render check's, which follows landing, owned by temper/ui, since compiling, prints and focused tests establish no rendered equality.
- **Runs:** focused and fuzzy alone, each build and test through `heavy`, one command at a time. No `tools/tiers/run`, no `<tier>-<tree>` profile, no `scripts/browser.sh`, no browser story run, no walk, no render check, no screenshot, no throwaway driver: temper/ui's render check follows landing (`look-requests.md`, 4). A browser story a task changes is compiled, `heavy cargo check -p temper-client-browser-world --tests`, not run, and the report says so.
- **Prints:** the journeys' prints are the view's markup replayed from their recordings, and `the_committed_journeys_are_their_print` (`temper/tools/journeys/src/tests.rs`) compares them byte for byte. A task whose view change moves a print reprints every journey from its recording, `heavy cargo run -p temper-client-journeys` with no argument (`journeys.md`, 5.1), and commits what changed; no task here records a journey again (`again`), since no recording's act or read changes. The prints link `temper.css` by path, so a stylesheet change leaves them as they are; the gallery's print is made by its tests and never committed.
- **Tests stay cheap:** no new test function where a row or an assertion of an existing table test holds the case; a spec names the test. A stylesheet change takes no test of its own.
- **`temper.css`:** new rules go where their part's rules stand, or under the marker its spec names, never at the end of the file; replaced rules are removed where they stand. Two tasks of this plan change it, in different places: `night-tokens` its colours, token sets and the night's rules; `two-steps` one rule beside the tab bar's grid. Each leaves the other's lines alone.
- **Before pushing:** `cargo fmt`; `python3 -I -B temper/scripts/old_forms.py`; `python3 -I -B tools/citations/citations.py --base origin/main`; clippy as the implementer's text says, naming every package changed.
- **Leave alone:** `origin/look-requests`; the drafts (`temper/docs/design/draft/*.md`; their status files only where a spec says); `docs/help/`; `overlay.css` and every request's hand-made directory, which are not on main.
- **Beside this plan:** the plan `ui-ask-drop` renames names in `temper-client-view`, `temper-client-domain` and the gallery (`configure` becomes `ask_page`, `configuring` `asking`, the gallery's `configure*` states `ask-*`); `look-round-19`'s tasks change those crates too. A task here keeps the names it finds on its base and renames nothing of theirs; fab's catch-up merges the two. Where a gallery file or `names.txt` conflicts on a catch-up, it is regenerated, never hand-merged, and `names.txt` stays sorted.
