# look-round-6: what every task holds to

The procedure is `temper/docs/design/draft/look-requests.md`, sections 3 to 5. The request is the commit `3f707babb9` on `origin/look-requests` (`git fetch origin look-requests`, then `git show 3f707babb9`); `8ad410e969` above it is held, and isn't landed. Its pages (`journeys/chat-work/01.html` to `04.html`) and its `overlay.css` are read from that commit into a scratch directory: nothing of the request's own lands, and `overlay.css` on the branch stays empty (`temper/docs/design/client/journeys.md`, 7).

- **Every commit** carries the trailer `Look-request: chat-work`, directly above `Co-Authored-By:` where a commit has one, else last.
- **Decide, don't ask.** The user is asleep and takes fabrig/ui's judgement. A stored format, a protocol or a public interface may change freely, with no compatibility. A small edit a task needs just outside its list (a re-export, a `mod` line, a design line, a golden) is made and named in the report. `Question:` only for what truly blocks the task.
- **`temper.css`:** new rules go under `/* ==== ui: chats ==== */`, never at the file's end. A rule replaced is removed where it stands. `ui-final-walk` and `ui-rename-focus` are landing there too; `ui-rename-focus` adds `position: relative` to the conversation's scroller.
- **Goldens, prints, the gallery's `names.txt` and `tests.rs`:** regenerate them, never hand-merge; `names.txt` stays sorted. Journeys that print a chat are reprinted in the task whose change makes them stale, with `heavy cargo run -p temper-client-journeys`; none of this plan's changes the client's exchange, so a recording going stale is a surprise, and is re-recorded by `again NAME` (`temper/docs/design/client/journeys.md`, 8.1), up to three tries on a fresh fake box if a key mark catches a moving page, then that mark's wait widened and said.
- **Generated files** your change makes stale (`docs/help/reference/*`, prints, goldens, counted baselines) are yours in the same task. A `docs/help/` page pushed past its bound is shortened, losing no fact, never by raising the bound.
- **Departure from the implementer's text:** a task's `## Owns and may change` may name lines of `docs/design/` it edits (a status entry it closes, the account). Everything else there stays the lead's.
- **`chat-reading` is beside this plan, in the user's order:** (a) its sections 3 and 4, the whole live text and the turn budget, under way as `chat-reading-a`; (b) section 2's line, on top of (a) and this look; (c) section 5's window and scrolling; (d) section 6's numbered lists in skein's Markdown, under way as `chat-reading-d`. Don't build (a), (c) or (d). Where their commits arrive by catch-up, take them. Where the view has no fact yet, draw what can honestly be drawn in one function, and the task's report names it for the account as deferred to `chat-reading`.
- **The terminal's prints keep their meaning:** a heading the browser drops by style stays a heading; a change of words is a change of its goldens, read before regenerating.
- **Render proofs:** throwaway scripts stay untracked and go before the push. Chromium 147's new headless won't go below 500 px by `--window-size`: a 420 px proof uses CDP's `Emulation.setDeviceMetricsOverride`. Handle `Page.javascriptDialogOpening`, or a pending request's `onbeforeunload` hangs a CDP driver.
- **Reach for the mock:** `temper/docs/design/client/greenfield/`; where an old test walks a part the request removes, change it with the part.
- **Strict subset:** the step crates' rules hold (`AGENTS.md`, 2): no `_` in a match, no indexing, no unchecked arithmetic, no `as`, no `unwrap`. `chat.rs` is near its size: new work goes in new modules.
