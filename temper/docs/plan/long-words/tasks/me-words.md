# `me` says the most bytes a party's words may have

## Goal

`jig/docs/design/client/domain/contract.md`, 5.1 has `me` say this "so that a client says the bound before it sends", and the code never built it. Today a client learns a person's bound only from a refusal. Carry `people.words` on `Me`, as `words_bytes`.

Acceptance:

- `Frame`, `w::Me` and `c::Me` carry `words_bytes`, from `limits.people.words`, and it crosses the wire both ways. The family's goldens and generated code are regenerated by their tool and hold.
- Through the real engine and native bytes, in `jig/tests/client-protocol`: a signed-in client's `me` says the engine's configured `people.words`.
- `jig/docs/design/client/domain/contract.status.md`'s **Not yet** on 5.1 drops the party's words and keeps the rest, reading: `me` says no bound on an amendment's.
