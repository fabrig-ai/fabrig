# long-words: one long message can't stop a chat being read, and a person can write one

From a check at `chat-reading-c`'s trunk (`55ea17c1b4`), and temper/ui's walk finding M2 on main before it: real Chromium, a real temper, fake backends. Its scripts and output are outside this repository, so the words here are what you have. The chain below is from reading, not run: reproduce each in the lowest tier first, and say if one's wrong. **Run:** 66 ctl messages of 538 or 1149 bytes hang the read; at 492 bytes the same chat reads.

1. **A line's words past 512 bytes fail its whole page, and the chat never reads it.** `temper/crates/temper-client-shell-web/src/limits.rs`, `CONVERSATION_TEXT`, is the client's `text`, 512; so is the terminal's. `jig/crates/jig-client-protocol-link/src/translate/pages/activity.rs` decodes a line's words with `text`, and one line over makes the page too large. What the store takes is `tasks.message_bytes`, 10,660 on the stock (`temper/docs/design/protocol/configuration.md`, 13.8), so `temper ctl task message` reaches it. Check what else does: an agent's message to a task, a question's words, a reply. **The rule:** a line holding words the store took draws whole, in the browser and the terminal; never cut silently, as `jig/docs/design/client/domain/client.md` has it. `temper/docs/design/client/README.md`, 5.4, is what moves: say which bound you take (`long_text`, 131,072, is the one that exists) and what it does to the window's bytes and the view's prices.

2. **A read the client can't decode retries forever, and nobody's told.** In `jig/crates/jig-client-protocol-link/src/component.rs`, a turn read gets `ReadNone::Malformed`, and any other read goes to `failed`, which ends `Unreachable`. That's transient, so `temper-client-domain`'s `Asking` asks again, at 1, 2, 4, 8 and 16 s on what was seen. "Loading earlier messages…" stays up. "Couldn't load earlier messages" never shows. The composer's lines stay "Sent", and "Latest" sent no read within 3 s. **The rule:** an answer that came and can't be decoded isn't transient: the read ends for good, the page says "Couldn't load earlier messages" as it does for its other failures, and its other lines and acts still work. A lost exchange still retries. Say what this does to the other reads that share `failed`, and widen it no further than this needs.

3. **A person can't write a paragraph.** `temper/crates/temper-client-domain/src/pages/chat.rs`, `Act::MessageWords`, refuses an edit past `CUE_BYTES`, 256, a playground suggestion's bound, and keeps the old words: a test says so, `an oversized edit keeps the previous words`. `chats.rs` does the same, and `question.rs`. So a paste of a long paragraph is dropped with nothing said. Client 5.4 says `text`, the code says 256, and the store takes 10,660. **The rule, temper/ui's words:** a person can type or paste up to what a message can hold. Past that, nothing they typed is silently dropped or kept stale: the words stay as typed, Send is disabled, and a line says how far over they are ("That's 3,200 characters over what one message holds."). The same on `chats.rs`'s start and `question.rs`'s reply. Tests: paste 1 KB, and paste past the bound. This depends on 1: a long message must read back. Check what the request table's `ask_bytes` allows, and say what the client knows of the store's bound.

**Order:** 1, then 2, then 3. Failing tests first, in the lowest tier: the link's decode of a line at the store's bound; the domain's read ending; the composer's edits. No new browser story: the browser tier is over its budget, and budgets and timeouts are the user's (skein's `docs/foundation/testing-strategy.md`, 8.1). temper/forge proves the shape by hand after landing.

**This trunk stands on `chat-reading-c`'s,** which holds what's read and worded here (no "Older" on a chat, `Word::LoadingEarlierFailed`), and this plan can't be ready until it lands: take its reading as given. An agent's page keeps "Older", and the fix must hold there too. Keep hunks small. Formats change in place before v0.1.0, but a client protocol or wire bound that moves is named in your report, for temper/ui, who reviews the composer's words before this lands. `walk = false`: the user tries it.
