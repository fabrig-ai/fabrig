# Kind 11 declared, read and proposed: `template-goal`

## Goal

A goal template's work becomes a setting owners change and runs read and propose,
as kind 3 is: the stock declares it to jig, the client wire's entrances take it,
a settings file names it `template-goal`, and a run's `settings`, `see` and
`propose_settings` read and propose it. After this task an owner's change of kind
11 is kept and read back, and Ask temper can propose it.

Acceptance, by `temper/docs/design/protocol/configuration.md`, 13.2 (*A goal
template's work, kind 11*), `temper/docs/design/domain/agent.md`, 8.1 (*A goal
template's work*) and 8.4.1 (`settings template-goal N`), and
`temper/docs/design/client/tools.md`, 5.3 (*A goal template's work*):

1. **Declared** (`temper/crates/temper-service/src/stock/kinds.rs`), as
   `temper/docs/plan/ui-p3f-design/notes/contracts.md`, section 4, has it: kind
   11, `KindScope::Deployment`, its `value_bytes` the size of a `Value::Goal`
   with both bools set, `per_scope` `limits.templates`, `charters` 0.
2. **The wire's entrances:** `settings_kind`
   (`temper-client-protocol-wire/src/lib.rs`) takes 11.
3. **Smith's codec:** `kinds.rs`' `decode` of kind 11 reads a `v1::TemplateGoal`
   whole (trailing bytes refused) into `Value::Goal`; `encode` and `size` as
   `goal-value` left them.
4. **Its name:** smith's `settings.rs` takes `template-goal`, a `number` required
   and no `project`, as `forge-charter` is; its unknown-setting guidance names
   it; the setting-kind enums and the `number` descriptions of the schemas in
   `calls.rs` (`propose_settings`, the `settings` read, the asking
   `propose_settings`) gain it, the `number` described as a charter's or a
   template's where it says which; `see.rs`' addresses and inventory name
   `settings template-goal N`, as `temper/docs/design/domain/agent.md`, 8.4.1
   has it.
5. **The root's reads:** `route/see.rs` parses `settings template-goal N` into
   kind 11, scope 0, number N, as it does `forge-charter`; `route/host_route.rs`'
   newest-settings fallback answers kind 11 at scope 0 from
   `domain.forge.setting(11, 0, N)` where jig knows none, as it answers kind 2;
   where neither jig nor the forge holds one, the answer is the contracts' words,
   `template-goal N: none kept; its coordinator plans and makes no close-out.`,
   not the unknown-setting error, wherever the settings answer's text is made for
   it.
6. **The settings form** (`temper-settings-form`): the SETTING `{"kind":
   "template-goal", "number": N}` and its VALUE `{"design": BOOL, "close_out":
   BOOL}`, both members required, parsed, refused and rendered as kinds 3 and 7
   are, their shapes, choices, fields and lines in the form's tables; a member
   left out or unknown refused naming it. Smith's selectors and proposals reach
   it through the form, as kinds 3 and 7 do: no second parser in smith.
   `temper/docs/help/reference/settings-fields.md` regenerated by its test
   (`notes/common.md`).
7. **Rows,** no new test function:
   - smith: the round-trip values table and its size test (`tests/kinds.rs`), a
     kind 11 row; `tests/settings.rs`' typed proposal cases, a `template-goal`
     row, and `template-goal` with a `project` refused; `tests/see.rs`' address
     table, the address;
   - the service: the declarations table (`tests/stock.rs`, where kinds 3, 6 and
     7 are listed with their bounds), a kind 11 row; the typed boundary test in
     `clients/translation.rs`, a row;
   - the root: `tests/see.rs`' parse table, `settings template-goal 4`;
     `tests/kinds.rs`' read table, kind 11 read in the deployment's scope and
     refused in a project's;
   - the form: `fields/tests.rs`' shapes and rendered kinds;
     `settings_file_tests.rs`' whole-record round trip, selector rules and
     malformed values: `{"design": true, "close_out": false}` round trips; a
     value without `close_out`, one with a third member and a `template-goal`
     with a `project` refused;
   - the engine world's `configuring.rs`: the asking reads of each kind and the
     address inventory, kind 11's rows; and one row where `see settings
     template-goal 4` first answers none kept, then an owner's `ChangeSettings`
     sets template 4's work to design and close-out, and a member's run reads it
     back by the same address.
8. **Bites:** let the form take a value without `close_out`, see its row fail;
   drop kind 11 from the fallback, see the none-kept row fail; restore each.
9. **Status:** delete the bullets naming `goal-kind` in
   `temper/docs/design/protocol/configuration.status.md` (13.2) and
   `temper/docs/design/domain/agent.status.md` (8.1, 8.4.1) (`notes/common.md`,
