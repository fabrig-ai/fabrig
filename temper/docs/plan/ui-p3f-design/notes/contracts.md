# ui-p3f-design: the contracts

The run's binding calls, for every task of this plan. Each is built by the task
named, to these shapes, and read by the others as written here. Design:
`temper/docs/design/protocol/configuration.md`, 13.2 (*A goal template's work,
kind 11*), 13.4 (*Template 4's work*) and 13.9 (*template 4's work*);
`temper/docs/design/domain/forge.md`, 8.13 (the inputs, *A typical run*, steps 1
and 6, *The coordinator's cards*); `temper/docs/design/client/README.md`, 4.6
(`PathStep`); `temper/docs/design/protocol/store.md`, section 2 (*Family 20 also
keeps kind 11*); `temper/docs/design/domain/agent.md`, 8.1 and 8.4.1;
`temper/docs/design/client/tools.md`, 5.3.

## 1. Kind 11, a goal template's work

- **Scope** the deployment's (0) alone; **number** a template's, 1 to jig's
  `core.templates`, the root's `Limits::templates`; **value** a `TemplateGoal`,
  `design` and `close_out`, two bools.
- **With none:** `design` and `close_out` both false: the coordinator plans and
  makes no close-out.
- **Name** `template-goal`, a `number` required and no `project`, as
  `forge-charter` is; in a settings file its VALUE `{"design": BOOL,
  "close_out": BOOL}`, both members required.
- **The stock's:** template 4's, `{design: false, close_out: false}`; **the
  reference's** (`temper/docs/examples/reference/settings.json`): template 4's,
  `{design: true, close_out: true}`.
- Kinds 8, 9 and 10 are other plans' (`notes/common.md`, *Beside this plan*).

## 3. The forge's value (`goal-value`, `temper/crates/temper-engine-domain-forge`)

In `src/settings.rs`:

```rust
/// A goal template's work, kind 11 (temper/docs/design/protocol/configuration.md, 13.2):
/// whether its coordinator designs first and makes a close-out; neither with none.
#[derive(Clone, Copy, PartialEq, Eq, Hash, Debug, Default)]
pub struct TemplateGoal {
    pub design: bool,
    pub close_out: bool,
}
```

- `Kind::TemplateGoal`, numbered 11, after the last variant, in `number` and `of`.
- `Value::Goal(TemplateGoal)`, after the last variant.
- `Bounds` gains `pub templates: u32`, last; the root's
  `temper-engine-domain/src/route/kinds.rs` `bounds` fills it with
  `limits.templates`.
- `judge`: kind 11 at scope 0 alone (else `Refusal::Scope`), its number 1 to
  `bounds.templates` (else `Refusal::Number`), a `Value::Goal` or none (else
  `Refusal::Value`); a removal or a reset good.
- In `src/kinds.rs`, beside `project_questions`:

  ```rust
  /// A goal template's work as held: its change, else the stock's; none where removed
  /// or never given (temper/docs/design/protocol/configuration.md, 13.2).

  ```

- Not erased with a project (a deployment's kind); `stored_bytes` counts it as

- The store's codec: `Value::Goal` as `SettingValue::Goal` through the store's

- Smith's codec (`temper-engine-smith/src/kinds.rs`): `encode` and `size` write

## 5. The path (`goal-path`, `temper/crates/temper-engine-domain-ways`)

and `Step` gains `Design` and `CloseOut`, after `Branch`.

In `src/path/derive.rs`, by `temper/docs/design/domain/forge.md`, 8.13:

- **The coordinator's stop,** after "draft": with `design`, an agent's stop,
  `Step::Design`, words `design`, opening `Opens::Charter(charter)`, its card
  `who` `an agent · designs the goal, then cuts it into changes`, `name` `the
  coordinator`, the agent's facts (`agent_card`) and then the fact `Its order`,
  `the design is delivered first, as a change into the goal's branch; the
  changes start from it and merge after it`; without it, today's `plan` stop,
  unchanged.
- **The close-out,** with `close_out`, on the landing branch's segment after a
  goal's alone: right after that segment's merge (by queue) or turn (by turn),
  before its tiers, an agent's stop, `Step::CloseOut`, words `close-out`,
  opening `Opens::Charter(charter)`, not needed and with no small words over
  it; its card `who` `an agent · after the landing, brings what the goal leaves
  up to date`, `name` `the coordinator`, the agent's facts and then the fact
  `Lands`, `as one change into BRANCH, by BRANCH's rules`, BRANCH the landing
  branch (`inputs.branch`).
- **The service** (`temper-service/src/family/ways.rs`): `Step::Design` as
  `w::PathStep::Design`, `Step::CloseOut` as `w::PathStep::CloseOut`.
- **The root's construction** (`temper-engine-domain/src/route/ways.rs`,
  `start`) gives `design: false, close_out: false` until `goal-read`; every
  other reader matches `Start::Goal { charter, .. }`.
