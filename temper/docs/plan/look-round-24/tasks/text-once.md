Acceptance, by `skein/docs/design/ui-text.md`, 5, *Then every node in preorder* (the rule this cut wrote), and `temper/docs/plan/look-round-24/notes/contracts.md`, *The text shell*:

1. **The rule** in `render.rs`: a node whose line would be `NAME:` alone, above its children (a group's line with no disclosure description and no state words after it), where the nearest ancestor that printed such a line printed the same `NAME:` (the same name bytes), prints no line of its own and indents its children no further: its children print at the indent the ancestor's line gave them.
