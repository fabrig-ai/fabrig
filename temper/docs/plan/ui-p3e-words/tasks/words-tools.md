# `temper ctl settings show charter-words` and `project-words`, and the help

## Goal

A person reads a charter's words and a project's words with `temper ctl`, as they
read a project's ways: `settings show charter-words NUMBER` and `settings show
project-words PROJECT`, each with `--revision` and `--json`, and the help says
what the two settings are. ctl names them right wherever it names a setting.

Acceptance, by `temper/docs/design/client/tools.md`, 5.3 (the `settings show`
rows and *Settings FILE is JSON*):

1. **The commands** (`temper/crates/temper-client-tools/src/settings.rs`): the
   `settings show` forms gain `charter-words NUMBER [--revision REVISION]`,
   NUMBER a charter's, from 1, and `project-words PROJECT [--revision
   REVISION]`, read from the forge's `Setting { kind, scope, number }` as
   `project-ways` is; the value stored is printed whole, in text and with
   `--json` in the form `settings apply` takes; with none, a charter's words
   print the stock's marked so, or that none is kept, and a project's that none
   is kept (`notes/contracts.md`, section 4, for the forms).
2. **Named right:** `name` (`settings.rs`, about line 263) prints
   `charter-words N` for kind 9 and `project-words P` for kind 10, not
   `landing-rule`; so do the proposal's and revision's renderers under
   `src/jig/render/`, wherever they name an application kind by its number.
3. **The help:** `temper/docs/help/settings.md` lists the two settings where it
   lists `project-ways 2` and `project-questions 2` (about lines 13, 120 and
   133), in its own words: a charter's words, the line How work runs says the
   charter by; a project's words, How work runs' headline and lede where the
   project runs a way of its own; Ask temper writes them with its proposals.
   `temper/docs/help/project.md`, after its *Its ways* bullet (about line 24), a
   sentence on `project-words`. Each page within its bound (`notes/common.md`,
   *Help pages*); `docs/help/reference/ctl-settings.md` and any other reference
   page made stale regenerated by its test.
4. **Rows,** no new test function: the commands' parse and print tables in
   `src/tests/` where `project-ways` is (`rg -n 'project-ways'

   settings, stored and none; `name`'s table, kinds 9 and 10; the help's golden
   `help_settings_show.txt` regenerated; the client world's `settings_tools.rs`,
   if it shows each kind through ctl, a row each.
