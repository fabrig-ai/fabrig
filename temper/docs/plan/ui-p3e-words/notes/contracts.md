# ui-p3e-words: the contracts

The run's binding calls, for every task of this plan. Each is built by the task
named, to these shapes, and read by the others as written here. Design:
`temper/docs/design/protocol/configuration.md`, section 3 (a tier's
`description`), 13.2 (*A setting's words*), 13.4 (kind 9's stock), 13.10 (a
stock way's headline, the `ways` read); `temper/docs/design/client/README.md`,
4.6 and 5.13.

## 1. Numbers and bounds

- **Kind 9, a charter's words:** the deployment's scope (0); its number a
  charter's, 1 to jig's `tasks.charters`; its value a `CharterWords`, its `line`.
- **Kind 10, a project's words:** a project's scope; number 1 alone; its value a
  `ProjectWords`, its `headline` and its `lede`.
- **Bounds:** a `line` and a `headline` 1 to 120 bytes, a `lede` 1 to 480, a
  tier's `description` 1 to 120; each UTF-8 holding no `char` for which
  `char::is_control` is true (so no line break, no tab).
- Kind 8 is ui-reaches' (a project's classes), landing beside this plan: no task
  here uses 8.

## 3. The forge's value (`words-value`, `temper/crates/temper-engine-domain-forge`)

In `src/settings.rs`:

```rust
pub const LINE_BYTES: u32 = 120; // a charter's words' line
pub const HEADLINE_BYTES: u32 = 120; // a project's words' headline
pub const LEDE_BYTES: u32 = 480; // a project's words' lede

/// A project's words, kind 10 (protocol/configuration.md, 13.2).
#[derive(Clone, PartialEq, Eq, Hash, Debug)]
pub struct ProjectWords {
    pub headline: Box<[u8]>,
    pub lede: Box<[u8]>,
}

/// Words saved with a setting fit `most`: 1 to `most` bytes of UTF-8 holding no
/// control character (protocol/configuration.md, 13.2).
#[must_use]
pub fn words_fit(words: &[u8], most: u32) -> bool
```

- `Kind::CharterWords` numbered 9 and `Kind::ProjectWords` numbered 10, after
  `ProjectWays`, in `number` and `of`.
- `Value::CharterWords(Box<[u8]>)` and `Value::ProjectWords(ProjectWords)`, after
  `Ways`.
- `Refusal::Words`, its `words` "words that are empty, past their bound, not
  UTF-8 or holding a control character".
- `judge`: kind 9 at scope 0 alone (else `Scope`), its number 1 to
  `bounds.charters` (else `Number`), a `Value::CharterWords` whose line
  `words_fit(.., LINE_BYTES)` (else `Words`); kind 10 at a project's scope alone,
  number 1 alone, a `Value::ProjectWords` whose headline fits `HEADLINE_BYTES`
  and lede `LEDE_BYTES` (else `Words`); a removal or a reset of either good; any
  other value `Value`.
- In `src/kinds.rs`, beside `project_questions`:

## 4. Declared, read and proposed (`words-kind`)

- **Names:** `charter-words`, its `number` the charter's, no `project`; and
  `project-words`, its `project`, no number (number 1 implied), as
  `project-ways` is. In a settings file and a run's `settings`, `see` and
  `propose_settings`.
- **Values in a settings file:** a charter's words are a JSON string, the line;
  a project's words `{"headline": STRING, "lede": STRING}`, both members
  required.
- **Declared** (`temper-service/src/stock/kinds.rs`): kind 9 `KindScope::Deployment`,
  its `value_bytes` `size(&Value::CharterWords(..))` of a 120-byte line, its
  `per_scope` jig's `tasks.charters` as the engine's limits give it, `charters`
  0; kind 10 `KindScope::Project`, its `value_bytes` the size of a
  `ProjectWords` at 120 and 480 bytes, `per_scope` 1, `charters` 0.
- **The wire's entrances:** `settings_kind` takes 9 and 10.
- **`see`:** `settings charter-words N` and `settings project-words`.
