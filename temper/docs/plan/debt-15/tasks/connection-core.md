# A new crate holds one connection's core

## Goal

`skein-fake-forge-http` exists, with `connection` as `notes/contracts.md`, 2 has it, and step tests of its own. No server
reads it yet: `forgejo-on-core` and `github-on-core` move them onto it.

Acceptance:

- The crate is in the workspace (`members` and the workspace's dependencies, as `skein-fake-forge-github` is), in
  `.config/gate/wasm32.txt`, and has the step profile.
- `connection`'s public surface is the contract's, in its names.
- The crate's tests pass and each of `up`, `turn`, `request`, `respond` and `close` has one that bites.
- No code in `skein-fake-forge-protocol` or `skein-fake-forge-github` changed.
- Size: about 170 production lines, about 250 of tests.

## Inputs

- `temper/docs/plan/debt-15/notes/common.md`
- `temper/docs/plan/debt-15/notes/contracts.md`, 1 and 2
- `skein/docs/design/fake-forge.md`, 12; `skein/docs/foundation/programming-model.md`, 10
- `tools/lints/design.md`, 2.1 and `--set`
- What's copied: `P/server.rs`' `State`, `up`'s gate, `http_down`, the head's snapshot in `entrance`, `http::Event::Reply`,
  `room` and `respond`'s tail; `G/server.rs`' same pieces. Read both before you write: where they differ, the contract
  says which holds.

## Owns and may change

- **Owns:** the new crate.
- **May change:** root `Cargo.toml` (`skein/crates/skein-fake-forge-http` wherever `skein/crates/skein-fake-forge-github`
  stands, and its workspace dependency line), `Cargo.lock`, `.config/gate/wasm32.txt` (one line beside the fake's others).
- **Not to touch:** either server. They keep their own copy until their tasks.
- **Options:**
  - The root `Cargo.toml` says it's generated by the assembly, and no generator is in the repository: edit it by hand.
  - If the contract's `Connection` needs one more private field, add it. A change to a public signature is a question.
  - `is_settled` and `has_work` are what each `Server`'s are of the common fields: where the two differ, the common
    part goes here and the server adds its own in its task.

## Steps

1. The crate: `Cargo.toml` (description "What the fake forge's HTTP layers share"), `src/lib.rs`, `src/connection.rs`,
   `src/tests.rs`, `src/tests/connection.rs`. `python3 -I -B tools/lints/lints.py --set step
   skein/crates/skein-fake-forge-http/Cargo.toml`, taking the name from `tools/lints/design.md`.
2. `connection`, as the contract.
3. Tests, in `src/tests/connection.rs`, driving a `Connection` with bytes on one `Env`:

   ```rust
   fn a_head_is_kept_with_its_credential_blanked();
   fn up_is_refused_before_start_and_after_close();
   fn the_first_bytes_turn_reading_into_receiving();
   fn a_reply_streams_within_the_send_limit_and_finishes();
   fn an_empty_body_finishes_and_a_bodyless_status_does_not();
   fn a_failed_reply_stream_is_a_failed_turn();
   fn close_drops_the_reply_and_both_queues();
   ```

4. `cargo fmt`; `heavy cargo clippy -p skein-fake-forge-http --all-targets -- -D warnings`; `heavy cargo nextest run -p
   skein-fake-forge-http`; the citations check. Commit and push.
