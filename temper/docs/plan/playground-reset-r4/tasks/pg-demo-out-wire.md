## Goal

The demo leaves the client wire (draft
`temper/docs/design/draft/playground-reset.md`, 6.3, "What goes"): the
request `start_demo`, the read `demo`, `DemoAnswer` and `DemoState`
are gone from `client-wire-v1.schema` (in place, `CLIENT_VERSION` 11),
with the service's arms that decode and answer them. The client has
sent neither since `pg-client-read`; the playground's start and read
are `start_playground` and `Read::playground`.

Acceptance:

- the schema names no `start_demo`, `Read::demo`, `DemoAnswer` or
  `DemoState`; `Read` is `{ playground }`; `lib.rs`'s `demo_answer` and
  `decode_demo_answer` are gone; the generated code and goldens are
  regenerated by the drift test's write mode, the removed records'
  goldens deleted;
- the service decodes no `StartDemo` from the wire: its `to_root` arm
  and the key carry it took for `StartDemo` (`clients.rs`) now serve
  `StartPlayground` alone (`pg-start` gave `StartPlayground` the
  carry; here `StartDemo`'s goes); the read answers
  `Read::playground` alone;
- no temper code or test names the removed wire items; every temper
  package that named them builds its tests and passes its focused tests.
