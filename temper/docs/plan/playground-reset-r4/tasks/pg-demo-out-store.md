stay until the playground part's task that removes the demo erases them
from the schema, in version 11 still; their tags are then left
unused"): `Demo`, `DemoState`, `DemoClose`, `DemoRemoveAccount`,

  are), decodes neither, and `keys::largest` still bounds every key;

  variants; `STORE_VERSION` stays 11;

  focused tests; the drift and store tests of `temper-codecs-world`
  pass, `fuzzy` for the store's fuzzy test alone.
