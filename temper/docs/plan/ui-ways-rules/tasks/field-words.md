# A rule's pattern says it may name a branch of each repository

## Goal

`temper/docs/design/draft/stock-ways.md`, 3.1 and 3.2, in the words a
person and Ask temper read: the settings form's field words for a landing
rule's pattern say that a project's rule may begin at `branch` and then
covers that branch in each of the project's repositories; the help page
`landing-rule` says each project's rule 1 is the stock's, that removing or
resetting it brings the stock back, and that the deployment's rules add to
a project's; and the settings file and `propose_settings` carry a pattern
of either form, as rows of their existing tests. No behaviour changes:
the forge's admission of the form is `forge-rules`', and the binary's
stock is `project-stock`'s.

Acceptance:

- The `pattern` field of `landing-rule` (`fields/jig.rs`, the `Field` with
  `shape: Shape::LandingRule, name: b"pattern"`) says both forms: `forge`,
  a forge's host, the repository's owner and name, then `branch` and its
  segments; or, for a project's rule, `branch` and its segments alone,
  covering that branch in each of the project's repositories. The
  `landing-pattern` form's line may say the same in fewer words. Words
  within the field's bounds, the form's own tests green.
- `temper/docs/help/reference/settings-fields.md` regenerated by its test's
  writer (below), never by hand.
- `temper/docs/help/landing-rule.md`: the paragraph starting `The binary
  supplies one deployment rule` says instead what
  `temper/docs/design/protocol/configuration.md`, 13.3 and 13.4 now say:
  each project's rule 1, the stock's, covers every branch of each of its
  repositories, enforcing CI passed, up to date, and gate 1, an agent's
  review, blocking and fresh at a clean head, with no approvals; a
  project's owners may change it, and removed or reset it is the stock's
  again; the deployment has no rule of its own until its owners add one,
  and its rules add to every project's. Its pattern paragraph says a
  project's rule may begin at `branch` (hex `6272616e6368`), covering that
  branch in each of the project's repositories, those adopted later among
  them, and that the deployment's begin at `forge`. Within the page's
  bound; a fact lost is a red.
- A row of `temper_kinds_round_trip_from_file_to_shown_json`
  (`settings_file_tests.rs`): a project's landing rule whose pattern is
  `["6272616e6368"]` with an exact `6d61696e` round-trips from file to

  (`temper-engine-smith/src/tests/settings.rs`): a proposal item of kind 2
