Look request (temporary): the TUI's launchpad, its commands and a chat

Hand-made frames of what follows the TUI's first screen, drawn from
draft/tui.md, section 3, at 80 by 24 in the dark theme
(client/tui/journeys/launchpad/README.md). Their glyphs and words are
the browser's chat terminal's (client/README.md, 3.5, "A chat's
terminal"), and the chat reads as draft/chat-reading.md says.

- 01: after sign-in, the TUI opens on a launchpad instead of
  "Connected to HOST:PORT as PARTY" and "Projects: ...". A framed header
  with the project and the deployment's address, the status agent's
  brief in words, where you left off, what needs you, the composer
  ("❯ Ask temper") at the foot, and the quiet line: "/ for commands",
  what needs you, the model and effort, and the link's state.
- 02: "/" typed in the composer lists the commands the launchpad
  offers, in the browser terminal's order and words, the first one
  selected, in the quiet line's place. /font is the browser's alone, and
  /exit closes temper.
- 03: a question typed in the composer, then Enter, opens a new chat.
  The browser terminal's top line ("✦ Hello / TITLE T2"), the person's
  words on a band after "❯", the run of calls as one line in words with
  its time under "▸", the agent's answer after "✦" still growing (whole,
  blocks drawn once, its end a small block after the last word), the
  running line "✦ working" with no clock, and the box taking a reply.
- 04: the same chat later. The answer committed in place without
  moving, the person's reply, and the agent's confirmation under the box
  as a list, as the terminal draws /close's: its question as the head,
  the recommended choice first and selected, "Tell it something else"
  last, in the quiet line's place.

States the frames don't show:
- Before the first call or word: the three dots. While a call runs,
  the run's line counts what has ended and names the call being made.
- No project: the launchpad says so, and the composer still asks temper.
- Nothing needs you: "Needs you" is left out, and so is the count on
  the quiet line.
- Offline or signed out: the quiet line's right end, as the first
  screen's status line says it today.
- The light theme, by the same tokens.
- A narrow terminal: the header's address goes first, then the model.

Replaces: the first screen's two lines, and "q to quit". Once the
composer has the focus, q types, and Ctrl+C or /exit closes.

Not the look's: the status agent's brief, where you left off, what
needs you, the chats, the commands and the model are engine facts and
features past the first pass (draft/tui.md, section 3). These frames
ask for them; building them is a plan's.
