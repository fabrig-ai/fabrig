# The client wire carries the approvals document and a proposal's `owned_approval`

## Goal

A change waiting on a person's review on the forge will show under Needs you, from a forge-family document of the party's own scope, `approvals` (`temper/docs/design/client/README.md`, 4.6). And a proposal's items read will say whether one of its landing rules has an enforcing approval on an owned Forgejo, for its card's sentence (same, 5.13). This task adds both to the wire's schema, in place, with no version bump, and nothing else: the engine and the client fill and read them in later tasks (`approval-read`, `approval-client`).

Acceptance:

1. `schema/client-wire-v1.schema` holds `ApprovalYours`, `ApprovalRow` and `Approvals` exactly as `temper/docs/plan/ui-forge-identity/notes/contracts.md`, *The approval read*, *The wire*, writes them; `Watch` ends in `approvals`; `ProposalItemsAnswer` ends in `owned_approval: bool`. `family temper-client-wire 16` and `CLIENT_VERSION` stay as they are.
2. The generated code and the goldens are regenerated by their tool, never by hand; `src/lib.rs` has `approvals` and `decode_approvals` beside `changes_panel` and `decode_changes_panel`, in their form.
3. One new test in `src/tests.rs` round-trips the document and its watch (Steps, 3) and bites; the proposal-items round trip carries `owned_approval: true`.
4. Every package that matches `Watch` or builds `ProposalItemsAnswerParts` compiles, with the compiler-forced edits below alone; `heavy cargo nextest run -p temper-client-protocol-wire` and the drift test pass; clippy clean for `temper-client-protocol-wire`.
