# ui-forge-identity: what every task holds to

- **Focused and fuzzy alone (the user, 11-06).** Never `tools/tiers/run`, a `<tier>-<tree>` profile, `scripts/browser.sh`, a walk, a render check, a throwaway driver or a live run, even where a test's name invites it. A new story for the browser, process or live tier is written and compiled (`heavy cargo check -p PKG --tests`), never run: say so in the report. `temper-client-journeys again` is generation: a task whose change leaves a recording stale runs it, and retries "a key mark caught a moving page" up to three times, each on a fresh fake box.
- **Tests stay cheap (temper/tiers, 11-06).** A new case of one behaviour is a row of an existing table test (`skein/docs/foundation/testing-strategy.md`, 8); a new test is only one your spec sketches. temper's focused and fuzzy suites go to 30 and 120 CPU seconds in the gate. No new browser test: that tier is at its cap of 12.
- **Decide, don't ask.** A stored format, a protocol or a public interface may change in place, with no compatibility and no version bump, before v0.1.0. A small edit just outside your list (a re-export, a `mod` line, a golden, a call site the compiler names) is made and named in the report.
- **Generated files your change makes stale are yours,** regenerated by their own tool, never by hand: temper's codecs and goldens by `SKEIN_CODEGEN_WRITE=1 heavy cargo nextest run -p temper-codecs-world --test suite -E 'test(drift::)'` (the command the drift test prints), `docs/help/reference/*`, prints, the gallery's goldens and `names.txt` (kept sorted). On a catch-up, regenerate rather than hand-merge.
- **`docs/design/` stays closed** but for the status entries your spec names: the design is already written. `docs/help/` is closed but for the pages your spec names, and a page your change pushes past its byte bound, which you shorten losing no fact; never raise a bound.
- **jig names no system:** nothing in `jig/` says forge, repository, pull, review or box (`jig/AGENTS.md`).
- **`temper.css`:** a new rule goes under its area's marker comment, `/* ==== ui: AREA ==== */`, never at the file's end; a replaced rule is removed where it stands.
- **Reach for the mock, not the old page:** `temper/docs/design/client/greenfield/` is the target; an old test walking a part the mock removes changes with it.
- **`forge_route.rs` is shared:** temper/migration's `goal-delivery` and `call-room` write `temper/crates/temper-engine-domain/src/route/forge_route.rs` and the stock beside it. Keep your hunks there small and in the functions your spec names; whichever lands second catches up.
- **A test's oracle or helper that your change's correct behaviour trips is yours to repair,** keeping what it checks for everything else, and named in the report.
- **Before calling a focused or fuzzy failure "outside the change",** run it once on the pristine base. If the base passes and your branch fails, it's yours.
