## 3. jig-protocol-transcript: the projection (task `withhold`)

```rust
/// What a turn shows its task's requester alone (domain/engine.md, 10.1).
#[derive(Clone, Debug, PartialEq, Eq, Hash)]
pub struct Private {
    /// The brief section's title as runs' briefs render it; empty withholds no section.
    pub title: Box<[u8]>,
    /// The tools whose calls are withheld, by name.
    pub tools: List<Box<[u8]>>,
}
impl Private {
    /// Nothing withheld.
    pub fn none() -> Self;
}
/// The turn's bytes with its private parts withheld, re-encoded as the current version.
pub fn withhold(limits: &Limits, bytes: &[u8], private: &Private) -> Result<Box<[u8]>, Undecodable>;
/// The one line a withheld part becomes.
pub const WITHHELD: &str = "Withheld: shown to its person alone.";
```

- **Sections:** in the turn's `system` text and in every `Text` block of every
  message, a line exactly `## {title}` keeps its place; the lines after it
  that are empty, `None kept.` or a run of ASCII digits then `. ` are
  replaced by one empty line, `WITHHELD`, and one empty line, put after the
  heading even where no line matched; the first other line, or the text's
  end, ends it. A text so changed loses its `replay`.
- **Calls:** a `Call` whose `name` is in `tools` keeps its `id` and `name`, its
  `input` becomes `{}` and its `replay` none; the `ToolResult` whose `id` is
  that call's becomes `Said { text: WITHHELD, error }`, its `error` kept, for
  any `Returned`.
- Nothing else changes; a turn with nothing to withhold comes back with the
  same blocks (its bytes may differ only by the version 6 upgrade).
