## Goal

Print "What temper remembers about you" in the gallery's named states, by
day and by night, so that its look is compared at a laptop's and a phone's
width after landing (`temper/docs/design/client/README.md`, 5.17, *Its
tests*; `temper/docs/design/client/browser.md`, section 7). The gallery
draws what the view draws: no view changes here.

Acceptance:

- Samples, as `temper/docs/plan/ui-ask-m4/notes/contracts.md`, section 6
  names them, `remembered-none`, `remembered-three`, `remembered-full`,
  `remembered-changing` and `remembered-refused`, at `Address::Remembered`,
  each its domain made from the gallery's document answers
  (`src/worlds.rs`): the person's party scope's `Notes` page answered with
  none, three lines (two a chat kept, one the person changed), twelve
  lines of up to 120 bytes, three with the second's field open on edited
  words, and three with a moved refusal under the second; and each by
  night, `night-` before its name, made as `night-limits` is
  (`src/samples.rs:1670`).
- `names.txt` regenerated by its test (`SKEIN_CODEGEN_WRITE=1`,
  `tests::names_are_the_promised_ones`), its order the samples'; `HOLDS`
  (`src/tests.rs`) an expectation for each new sample: its heading "What
  temper remembers about you" and its rows' lines, the field's words, the
  refusal's line, the band's tag, as the gallery's other samples hold them.
- One existing sample that prints the person's menu open (`src/tests.rs`,
  near `:5146`) holds its three items.
- Delete `temper/docs/design/client/README.status.md`'s **Not yet** entry
  `5.17, *Its tests*`.
