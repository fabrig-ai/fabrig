## Goal

Give jig's notes a party's scope, `Party { party }`, a person's own in no
project, through every type that carries a scope, the store, the people
child, the clients' wire and their cursors, with the notes child's two
bounds for it; and have every path that meets it refuse it, so that the
feature tasks after `core-surface` admit it one path at a time
(`jig/docs/design/domain/engine.md`, 10.1, *The scope*).

Acceptance:

- `jig_domain_notes::Scope::Party { party: u64 }` and its mirrors exist as
  `temper/docs/plan/ui-ask-m4/notes/contracts.md`, section 1 gives them,
  with their doc comments; `jig_domain_authority::NoteScope` is unchanged.
- The store's notes and people families and the clients' wire and cursor
  schemas carry the variant, edited in place; their generated code and
  goldens regenerated by their tool, a `party` golden for each scope
  family beside the others (`enum_scope_party_*`, `enum_note_scope_party_*`);
  the store's key orders a party's scope after every project's scopes, tag 4,
  and a project's intervals (`jig-protocol-store/src/intervals.rs`) still
  cover tags 1 to 3 alone, so a project's deletion leaves a party's scope.
- `jig_domain_notes::Limits` has `party_entries` and `party_bytes` as the
  note gives them, named `notes.party_entries` and `notes.party_bytes` in
  `jig-protocol-config` (`names.rs`, `overrides.rs`), both profiles 12 and
  120, and `valid` checks them as the note says.
- Every exhaustive match the variant forces handles it as the note's
  section 1, *Until the feature tasks*, says: the core's run admission
  (`routing/mod.rs`, `authorize_tool`'s scope check and `note_readable`)
  refuses it beyond authority naming the scope; the core's reads and acts
  (`reads/vocabulary.rs`, `reads/rows.rs`, `reads/person.rs`, `reads.rs`,
  `acts.rs`, `admission/roles.rs`, `documents/project_note.rs`) answer it
  unknown or refuse it as unknown; a project's listings and deletion
  (`drop_project.rs`, `reads/rows.rs`' project listing) leave it out; the
  notes child's validity (`domain.rs`) admits it as a scope; jig's
  client view labels it "Yours" (`tree/sections/project/notes.rs`).
- Round trips: a party's scope is a row of the store codec's scope tests
  (`jig-protocol-store/src/tests/codec.rs`), of its key ordering and project
  intervals (`tests/keys.rs`, `tests/intervals.rs`), of the client link's
  read translation (`jig-client-protocol-link/src/tests/reads.rs`) and of the
  clients' cursor (`jig-protocol-engine-clients/src/tests/reads.rs`); and
  one row of jig's core world's existing refusal table, or a new story in
  `jig/tests/core/tests/suite/stories.rs` beside the chat scope's, shows a
  run's `NamedAction::Note` in a party's scope refused beyond authority,
  and a party's `ScopedNotes` read of a party's scope answered unknown, with
  the store unchanged.
