## Goal

Lay the core's whole surface for a party's scope, so that four feature
tasks can fill one module each beside one another: the named action
`NoteDelete` and its call part, the brief's `preferences` kind and budget,
the private names in the core's and the clients' settings, the withheld
turn carriers and their projection on the clients' wire, and four stub
modules wired at their call sites, each doing what
`temper/docs/plan/ui-ask-m4/notes/contracts.md`, section 1's refusals do
(`jig/docs/design/domain/engine.md`, 9, 10.1 and 7.3).

Acceptance:

- Everything of `notes/contracts.md`, section 2 exists as it says, with its
  doc comments: `NamedAction::NoteDelete` (its tool kind `ToolKind::Note {
  scope }`), `CallPart::NoteDeleted` in the store's core family schema in
  place (code and goldens regenerated by `notes/common.md`'s command),
  `brief::Core::Preferences` named `preferences`,
  `CoreBriefBudgets.preferences`, the core's section count one more,
  `Settings.private_tools` in the core and `Settings.private` in the
  clients' component, both empty by default (`jig-protocol-config`'s
  `core.rs` and `clients.rs` constructors), and the two withheld carriers.
- **Translation:** the clients' component, given a non-empty `private`,
  writes a `PageTurn::Withheld` or an `EndedValue::WithheldTurn` onto the
  wire as `jig_protocol_transcript::withhold` returns it, and a carried
  turn unchanged (`jig-protocol-engine-clients`' `translate/activity.rs`
  `page_turn` and `archive`, `translate/objects.rs` `document`, the
  settings passed from `component.rs` through `reads.rs` and
  `translate/pages/documents.rs`); a row of its page tests
  (`src/tests/pages.rs`) shows a withheld activity turn projected, and one
  of its read tests (`src/tests/reads.rs`) a withheld turn read.
- **The stub modules,** each with a module doc citing engine.md 10.1 and
  naming the task that fills it, wired once at every call site below, so
  that no feature task edits a shared file but for a call site's line:
  - `routing/party.rs` (`party-runs`): the scope check of a party's scope
    for a run's `Note`, `NoteLines` and recall (`routing/mod.rs`,
    `authorize_tool`'s `ToolKind::Note` arm and `note_readable`, where
    `party-scope` refused it), and `NoteDelete`'s routing from
    `routing/run/calls.rs`' `named_action`, replay and call record
    included; the stub refuses every `NoteDelete`, whatever its scope,
    beyond authority naming the scope, and `party-scope`'s refusals stand.
    The success path is laid ready: `routing/effect/note.rs`' `tag_notes`
    answers a run's deletion as `CallPart::NoteDeleted { name }` through
    `call_decision`, reached from the notes child's `Deleted` answer, so
    that `party-runs` only admits.
  - `reads/party.rs` (`party-reads`): one predicate, whether a reading or
    acting party may see a party's scope, called where `party-scope`
    refused a party's scope in the core's reads and acts
    (`reads/vocabulary.rs` `row_visible`, `reads/rows.rs` `scope_allowed`,
    `acts.rs` `note`, `admission/roles.rs` `note_authorized`,
    `documents/project_note.rs` `note_from_entry`, `routing/effect/note.rs`
    `edit_note_request`); the stub answers no.
  - `routing/run/preferences.rs` (`party-brief`): the section's gather from
    `routing/run/preparation.rs`' `start_brief`, its plan from `plan_brief`,
    its indexed answer from `routing/effect/note.rs` (a `NoteRoute` of its
    own, or a discriminator on `Brief`, beside `Work::BriefNotes` in
    `routing/mod.rs`), and its text from `runs/brief_text.rs`; the stub
    gathers and plans nothing.
  - `reads/withheld.rs` (`withheld`): whether a turn is carried withheld,
    called where `reads/rows.rs` answers a turn (`object`, the `Turn`
    answer, `page_row`'s activity branch), `reads/activity.rs`' `row`, and
    `reads/person.rs`' answers; and whether a live call's input reaches a
    follower, called at `routing/view/routes.rs`' `made_outputs` and
    `documents/small.rs`' `run` snapshot; the stub answers carried and
    reaches.
- **The story files** `party_runs.rs`, `party_reads.rs`, `party_brief.rs` and
  `withheld.rs` in `jig/tests/core/tests/suite/`, each its module doc alone,
  and their `mod` lines in `main.rs`, in that order after the last.
- **Its own rows:** a `NoteDelete` of a goal's scope refused beyond
  authority with the store unchanged, a row of the core's existing scoped
  run admission tests (`src/tests/admission/goal_of.rs`); `CallPart::NoteDeleted`
  a row of the store's core codec round trips; `Core::Preferences` a row of
  `jig-domain-brief`'s planned-section tests, never planned in this task.
- **temper's arms,** pre-approved across the tree boundary, each edited
  only for these shapes: `temper-engine-domain/src/store.rs` (`CallAnswer`,
  `core_part` and its reverse, byte accounting: `NoteDeleted` as the other
  parts, placed apart from `remember-surface`'s types after `Documented`),
  `src/route.rs:557`, `src/route/forge_route.rs:5386`, `src/limits.rs`
  (`BriefBudgets.preferences`, mapped as `notes` is); `temper-service/src/limits.rs`
  (the section count and `preferences` 2,048 in both profiles);
  `temper-engine-smith/src/lib.rs` (`PREFERENCES_TITLE`), `src/start.rs`
  (its title arm), `src/see/person/runs.rs` and any other `see` renderer
  (a withheld carrier rendered as its carried form, a comment naming
  `see-withheld`, which projects it).
- `heavy cargo nextest run -p temper-service --lib -E 'test(/stock_brief|ask_bound|limits/)'`
  passes: the stock briefs fit with the new budget.
