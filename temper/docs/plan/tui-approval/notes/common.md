# tui-approval: what every task holds to

- **Focused and fuzzy alone (`AGENTS.md`, 5; the user, 11-06).** Never `tools/tiers/run`, a `<tier>-<tree>` profile, `scripts/browser.sh`, a browser, process or live tier, one of their stories, a walk, a render check or a throwaway driver, even where a test's name invites it. A new story for a slow tier is written and compiled (`heavy cargo check -p PKG --tests`), not run, and the report says so.
- **The contract is `temper/docs/plan/tui-approval/notes/contracts.md`.** Its names, fields, routes and answers are binding. A change it seems to need is a question; a detail it leaves open is yours, named in the report.
- **A sign-in's tests carry the plan:** for each refusal your spec lists, commit its failing test first, then the code that makes it pass, so the branch's history shows it red; the report names what each failed on.
- **No secret in a print or a log.** The approval's secret and the credential a take draws are `skein_lib::Secret` wherever they are held, whose `Debug` gives only a length. No `Debug` of a record, message or state that holds one may show its bytes: where a type holding encoded bytes derives `Debug` (a `Message`'s body), nothing new logs it. Tests use fake bytes (`[7; 32]`, a fixed seed), never a real secret.
- **Tests stay cheap.** A new case of one behaviour is a row of an existing table test (`skein/docs/foundation/testing-strategy.md`, 8); a story goes beside its siblings' fixture. Budgets and timeouts are the user's, never raised. Name your new tests' times in the report.
- **A compiler-forced arm or field** your change makes in another crate (a match on `Signing`, a literal `Settings`, a pattern on `FromTransport::Exchange`) is yours, in any tree, edited only for that: the nearest existing behaviour, a `browser: false`, an `approval: false`. List each in the report.
- **Generated files** your change makes stale (the wire's `generated/v19.rs` and `golden/`, prints, goldens, `docs/help/reference/*`, counted baselines) are yours to regenerate with their own tool, never by hand.
- **A test's oracle or helper that your change's correct behaviour trips is yours to repair,** keeping what it checks for everything else, and named in the report.
- **Before calling a focused or fuzzy failure "outside the change",** run it once on the pristine base. If the base passes and your branch fails, it's yours.
- **`docs/design/` stays closed:** the design sections are written; what the code taught you goes in the report.
- **Overlaps:** `per-wake` is in flight in jig (`jig-protocol-config`, `jig-domain-people`, `jig-client-domain`'s settings, the wire's schema); `tui-connected` adds the TUI's crates and edits `temper-client-domain`; `look-round-17` edits `temper-client-view`'s terminal pages and `temper.css`. Keep your hunks to what your spec names; never reformat or move what you don't change.
