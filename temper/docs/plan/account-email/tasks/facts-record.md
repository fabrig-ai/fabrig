# An account's facts have a record, a key and a codec

## Goal

What a provider says of an account gets a record of its own: the types and the registry in `jig-domain-accounts`, the key and codec in `jig-protocol-store`, restored at start and erased with the account. Nothing writes one yet but a restore, and no client reads it yet.

Acceptance:

- `jig-domain-accounts` exports section 3 of the contracts note, exactly.
- `accounts::encode` and `decode` round-trip `Stored::Facts` at its bounds: 24 facts, a 255-byte text, a 32-byte label, each value shape. A row of `accounts_records()`, so that `each_accounts_record_reads_back_at_its_bounds` holds it.
- `a_share_past_whole_does_not_decode`: a share, and a window's `used`, of 10001 is refused; 10000 reads. A row or a test beside the codec's.
- A kind, origin and window state no build registers (say 999) round-trips unchanged: the same table.
- `Key::Facts { account: 7 }` writes tag 5 and reads back: `src/tests/keys.rs`'s table. A key of the accounts' family keeps its place in the walk: `RestoreRange::Accounts` gives three intervals, facts last, and `src/tests/intervals.rs`'s pinning of `RestoreRange::interval` to `intervals::cursor` holds it.
- `jig/tests/accounts`, in `records_are_linked_after_loading_in_any_order`'s table or beside it: a facts row loaded before its account is the account's (`Domain::account_facts`), and one whose account never comes is dropped by the link and refuses nothing.
- `jig/tests/accounts`, in `changes.rs`: removing an account that holds facts erases `Key::Facts` in the same decision; removing one with none pushes the requests it pushes today.
- A table in `jig/tests/accounts` over `audience`, `registered` and `shown_to`: kinds 1 to 7 as `accounts.md`, 3.7 has them; kinds 0 and 8, origins 0 and 7, and window states 0 and 4 not registered; a holder's kind hidden from a reader who isn't its holder, an account's kind shown.
- The store's and the world's heap checks hold: `jig/tests/store-codecs/tests/suite/memory.rs`'s longest rows include a full facts row, and the accounts world's memory test counts the new map. Your report gives each bound before and after.
- The focused and fuzzy tests of `jig-domain-accounts`, `jig-protocol-store`, the accounts world, `jig-store-codecs-world` and `jig/tests/store` pass, and `heavy cargo check --workspace --tests`.
