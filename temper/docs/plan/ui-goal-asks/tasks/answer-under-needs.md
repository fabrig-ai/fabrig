## Goal

A person task a goal asks with today draws under Needs you as its title, its spec and "Answer in words", and that act sends nothing. Have a person task in words read as a goal's question form draws: its context and choices, no extra step, and answering it there or by "Reply…" answers the task, which wakes the goal with what they said.

Acceptance:

1. Under Needs you, on Home and in a goal's band, a person task in words (`notes/contracts.md`) reads as a question: the question line, its context, "What the agent saw", its choices with the recommended one, "Reply…" and "Later", its kind "a question". Words in no such form, or cut, fall back as a question's do: the first paragraph, the rest as context, "Reply…" alone.
2. Its mono line's TITLE is "Task N", N its `Requester::Task`, linking there. Another requester keeps its own title and link.
3. Choosing or replying holds the answer as a question's is held, with its Undo and toast, then sends `AnswerOn::Person { task, revision, answer: PersonAnswer::Words(words) }`, the revision being what the card showed when they answered. Its answered line titles itself by the person task's own title. A refused answer reopens with the engine's words, as a question's does.
4. On the terminal's inbox, "Answer" on a person task in words sends the same ask with the card's words.
5. A role's person task, a verdict's and an approval's draw and act exactly as today.
6. A story in the client world has a goal started from the sheet ask, its person answer under Needs you, and the goal carry on with those words. This task's **Not yet** entry in `temper/docs/design/client/README.status.md` is removed.
