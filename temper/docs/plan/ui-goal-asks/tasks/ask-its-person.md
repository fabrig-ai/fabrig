## Goal

A goal started from the sheet or from Goals has a person as its requester and no way to reach them: `message` reaches tasks alone, and the stock authority refuses a person executor at three places. Give it the person executor, narrowed as far as jig allows, and have its paragraph, `decided`'s answer and the `message` refusal's hint say to use it. A goal a chat calls keeps asking its chat.

Acceptance:

1. The stock `rules.ceiling` and each project's `policy.ceiling` name `Executor::Role(0)`, `Role(1)` and `Role(2)` after their existing kinds; each of roles 0, 1 and 2 names its own role alone among role kinds; role 3 still names no kinds.
2. A goal a person started may delegate a person task addressed to them, with exactly the JSON of `notes/contracts.md`. A person task addressed to another role, or to a person with no role in the project, is refused with `missing executor permission` and makes no task.
3. Their answer in words ends that task and the goal's next run has it as a result message carrying those words.
4. A goal a chat calls holds no `Executor::Role` kind in its authority, whatever its chat's holds.
5. `decided`'s Ask, the refusal's hint and the goal's paragraph read exactly as below; the question line form's sentence still ends the paragraph.
6. `temper/docs/help/goal.md` says so within its bound. This task's **Not yet** entry in `temper/docs/design/domain/agent.status.md` is removed.
