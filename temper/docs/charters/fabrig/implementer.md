# fabrig's checks

- **What you run,** where the lines above say: `cargo clippy -p PKG --all-targets -- -D warnings` on what you wrote, while errors or lints last; a new or failing test alone, `tools/tiers/test -p PKG --test BIN -E 'test(=NAME)'` or `--lib`, adding `--profile fuzzy` only for a fuzzy test: it is nextest with each test's instructions counted, and once main's counts are in your checkout it ends with a `budgets:` line for each layer the run's tests are in, saying how far a change may grow that layer; growth past it refuses the landing, and the goal's close refuses it too, or warns where it is near (`tools/tiers/budgets.md`, 8; `tools/pre-pr/design.md`, 4.1); a repository tool's own Python tests, for a tool you changed, run directly with `python3 -I -B`. These lines win over `AGENTS.md`'s on testing. A run that selects no test exits 4. Read a failure's log before anything reruns; no unchanged rerun, except once after exit 137.
- **When done,** once: `cargo fmt`; for each tree the branch touches, `(cd <tree> && python3 -I -B scripts/old_forms.py)` from the tree's root, with `--write` only to lower a count for what you removed: old-form counts only fall, and a raised count is red; then `deliver`. Its checks, `.temper/pre-pr`, build nothing: formatting, old-form counts, citations, outward names and the budgets file, so clippy and the tests above are yours before it. The rest runs later: the goal's close runs the focused tests its selection takes from what the goal changed and judges their budgets (`tools/pre-pr/design.md`, 1), and the landing's gate runs clippy and both suites.
- **Edits:** a scoped `#[expect(clippy::<lint>, reason = "...")]` on an item you wrote or changed, where reshaping it isn't your task. Generated files include goldens, prints, `docs/help/reference` and counted baselines. `docs/design/`, drafts and `docs/help/` aren't yours, but a help page your change or merge pushes past its byte bound may be condensed, losing no fact and keeping the bound.
- **A red you didn't cause:** a failure your check's summary names inherited passes. Otherwise ask, quoting the failure's key and the base the summary names; once it is recorded, your next check passes it.
- **An assertion you add** takes no test of its own, since no test triggers one (`skein/docs/foundation/programming-model.md`, 10.4): pin what it guards through the outcomes around it.
- **A shared helper that runs seeds, samples commits or judges generated mutations** counts each one it checks with `skein_checks::count` (`skein/docs/design/testing/checks.md`) and lists its file in `tests/boundary/tests/check_counts.txt`; a loop inside one test doesn't count. The gate compares a binary's counts with main's (`tools/tiers/budgets.md`, 13).
- **A message is only a question.** Your requester refuses anything else: progress, a plan, an acknowledgement or a hand-off in the `words` form. Progress goes in your report at `finish`. A sibling change can't hear you: what it needs from you is the code you push, named in your report.
- **A landing repair's failure** is the gate run its words name: the change's own cancelled checks say nothing about it. Run each failing test its words or CI section name, alone, in the profile it failed in. If you can't read which tests failed, ask; never conclude there's nothing to repair.
- **Another change must land first:** ask your goal, in your question, to add it to your change's `after`; your answer then waits for its landing. Never keep a question open, or call `wait` with nothing asked, to stay alive.
- Where the design or your spec makes you write the same thing twice, or a type with no behaviour of its own, say so: as a question when the simpler form crosses your change's edge, otherwise under `## Simpler` in your report, naming the place and the simpler form.
