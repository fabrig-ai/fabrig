# temper's store

Provisional, 2026-10-05, revised 2026-11-02. What temper keeps in jig's
store: its families (the forge's records under the application's tag),
keys, records, store version and restart ranges. jig's store
(jig's `protocol/store/README.md`), `jig-protocol-store`, is skein's key-value
store in the engine's process, with transcripts as files and secrets in
a second store. temper's engine-only crate is `temper-protocol-store`
(`components.md`). Open questions: `store.md`, section 7.

## 1. In one page

- **jig's store, temper's families.** The engine's protocol layer owns
  jig's store component; temper adds records beside jig's under the
  application's tag, in the same commits (jig's
  `protocol/store/README.md`, section 7).
- **One family per forge record.** Each variant of the forge connector's
  `Stored` is a family, a keyed adoption's or forge outcome, each
  forge's writer, each forge itself, the last forge number given, the
  playground's row and a person's dismissals among them. Nothing
  else of temper's is in the main store: no index or secrets; its forges'
  credentials are in jig's secrets' store (credentials.md, 2.1).
  The core's, people's and tasks' records are jig's.
- **Keys sort as the forge's keys do:** a family byte in the variants'
  order, then the key's fields by jig's key rule; a property test holds
  each encoder to the derived order (section 3).
- **Records from a schema,** generated by skein's generator, translated
  to and from the domain's by total functions; `STORE_VERSION` names
  their encodings (`store-versions.md`, section 1).
- **All live, but the landing history.** The forge's restart step
  restores every family of temper's below byte 128 as one interval, the
  root's `Range::Forge`; the landing history's families, bytes 128 and
  above, are loaded on demand alone (`store-restart.md`, section 1;
  `../domain/landing-history.md`, 4).
- **The core's ranges are jig's, filtered.** The root's core ranges are
  asked as jig's coarser ones and filtered, and its census is jig's walk,
  taken as it comes (`store-restart.md`, section 1).
- **Sized from limits.** The largest key and record follow from the
  forge's limits, and jig's component checks them with its own at start
  (`store-versions.md`, section 1).
- **The real store in temper's worlds,** over skein-kv's in-memory mode,
  and on the simulator's crashing disk in the one-process world
  (section 6).
- **jig's, cited, not restated:**
  - transcripts: one file per task, written before their commit (jig's
    `protocol/store/transcripts.md`);
  - secrets: a second store, holding temper's forges' credentials under
    temper's tag (credentials.md, 2.1) and the values clients' requests
    carry, kept by jig until their run ends (credentials.md, 2.2); both
    written and read by the engine's protocol layer, never by this
    crate (jig's `protocol/store/secrets.md`);
  - opening: the format row and the header checked before the root
    starts (jig's `protocol/store/opening.md`);
  - fill: reported with each commit, new work refused above the
    high-water mark (jig's `protocol/store/growth.md`, 1.2);
  - retention: the core's ended work shrunk past horizons (jig's
    `protocol/store/growth.md`, 1.1); temper's families have none (`store.md`, section 7).

## 3. Keys

- **Under the application's tag,** 2, which jig's component prefixes and
  never lets temper's bytes leave (jig's `protocol/store/keys.md`, 1.2).
  temper's own bytes start with the family's byte (`store.md`, section 2), then the
  key's fields.
- **By jig's rule** (jig's `protocol/store/keys.md`, 1.1), written with
  skein-kv's key writer: every number as eight bytes, narrower ones
  widened; a commit as its 32 bytes, a byte string; a branch as the list
  of its segments. So:
  - `Name` is the forge, the repository, then its `What`: 0 for the
    repository, 1 and the branch's segments, 2 and a pull request's
    number, 3 and an issue's;
  - `Topic` is its variant (0 `Landings`, 1 `Ci`, 2 `Pull`, 3
    `Participation`), then the repository, then the branch, the commit
    or the number; or 4 `Findings`, then the project
    (`../domain/forge/topics.md`, 1.1);
  - `Finding` is the project, then the number;
  - `Making` is the forge, then the org and the name, each one byte
    string;
  - the client's key is its variant (0 `Live`, 1 `Repository`), then its
    resource or repository, a resource's `What` tagged as `Name`'s is,
    its branch one byte string;
  - `Dismissed` is the party, then its fact: 0 `ToBegin` and the project; 1
    `GoalFrom`, the project, the chat, the goal and when; 2 `KeepAsking`,
    the project, its subject, 0 and the kind's place in
    `../domain/forge/attention.md`, 6's order or 1 and the set's number, and when.
  - `ProjectFacts` is the project; `Looked` and `PersonBrief` are the
    party, then the project.
  - `Away` is the party, then the project; `Decided` is the project,
    then the number.
- **Each family's byte has a name** for the store's census, in a table
  of `temper-protocol-store` that the engine hands jig's component with
  its owner set (jig's `protocol/store/growth.md`, 1.3): `temper.` and the
  family's name in lower case, its words joined by `-` (`temper.changes`,
  `temper.landing-history`), one per family byte `store.md`, section 2 names. A test
  holds the table to the families' bytes: every byte written has one
  name, and no two names repeat.
- **Written out, not generated:** each family has a small encoder and
  decoder in `temper-protocol-store`. A property test holds each encoder
  to the order `Key` derives, on generated keys of every family, and
  each decoder to its encoder; a decoder refuses a tag it does not know,
  a number past its field's width, and trailing bytes.
- **A key and its record agree.** A row's value holds the whole record,
  its key's fields among them; the routing checks that a decoded
  record's key encodes to the row's key, and a row where they differ is
  one that does not decode, which stops the engine (jig's
  `protocol/store/loads.md`, 1).

## 6. Testing

Within the suites' budgets (`../testing/tiers.md`, section 1):

- **Step tests** in `temper-protocol-store`: each family's key encoder
  against the derived order, and its decoder against its encoder; the
  interval of `Range::Forge` against `Range::contains` over generated
  keys; each codec both ways, with built values; each translation both
  ways, a domain value equal to itself after a round trip; the schema's
  bounds at their limit and one past; the sizes against the forge's
  limits.
- **Fuzz targets** for each family's decoder and for the key decoder.
- **The real store in the domain worlds,** as jig's `protocol/store/testing.md`,
  section 1 has it: temper's engine worlds run jig's component over
  skein-kv's in-memory mode; `temper-protocol-store` encodes temper's
  families. Every story runs real keys, codecs and interval with faults:
  commits held, delayed, one failed to stop the engine, and a crash
  keeping what reached the store.
- **The one-process world** (`tests/one-process`, `../testing/tiers.md`, 1.5):
  the store on skein's crashing disk, the process killed and restarted
  at drawn moments; after each restart the forge's records are those of
  the last acknowledged commit, or of that and one whole uncertain one,
  never part of one.
- **The store's own world** is jig's (jig's `protocol/store/testing.md`, section
  1); temper adds none.

## 7. Open questions

- **Links for several members** (section 2, family 25): one optional link
  belongs to a box's one person. A deployment with several members needs
  a family and evidence of each account (`../draft/forge-identity.md`,
  section 12). Default: the forge's one link and adoption's party seeds;
  no party merge or compatibility migration.
- **`Landed` grows with history.** A merge temper made is kept for
  own-change news, and no row is erased; once the forge's `landings`
  limit is reached, later merges are not recorded, and news of them reads
  as another's landing. Default: bounded by the forge's limits, no
  retention. A row could go once no live goal's subscription could hear
  of its merge.
- **A party's dismissals outlive it.** jig erases no party: a member
  taken out of the configuration stays a party, and a role lost leaves
  the party's rows. So `Dismissed`, `Looked`, `Away` and a party's
  `Decided` rows are erased with their project alone, and a row can outlive its member's use for it, within
  the family's bound:
  a person given the role back finds it still dismissed. Default:
  nothing, until jig erases a party, when its connector would
  need telling, a change of jig's (jig's `domain/connectors.md`, section
  2).
- **Indexes for the forge's client parts,** once panels are served
  (`../client/forge/family.md`): a family of small rows per view, as jig's
  indexes are (jig's `protocol/store/loads.md`, 4). Default: none until
  they are served.
