# welcome-playground

On a fresh `temper box --fake-backends`: the box's printed sign-in link
opened, the welcome drawn, "Try the playground" pressed.

- `01.html`: the welcome as it was when "Try the playground" was
  pressed. Its "Try the playground" leads to `02.html`.
- `02.html`: the chats page in the Demo project, the playground
  started, the project rail around it and the playground banner's one
  line at its pane's foot, as it was when the
  recording ended.

The rail names Demo, the recording's project, with its switcher,
Home, Needs you, Chats and Settings. The page remains Chats, with its
composer and suggested requests; it is not the project's Home.
On a phone the switcher stays at the top and Sections at the foot.
