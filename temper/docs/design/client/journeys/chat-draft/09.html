Away mode

Draft, 2026-11-05, with the user.

Purpose

Away mode lets the person step away from ongoing work. Agents keep working within the authority already granted. They leave a clear account of progress for the person's return.

The person chooses when to leave and when to return. Away mode does not grant extra authority.

Added by T1 just now

When the person returns

Show the work completed while the person was away. Keep the decisions that need the person in one place. Make each question name the goal and the choice it needs.

The person can answer questions before starting more work. The conversation and its document remain available.

Open questions

  • Where should the person switch away mode on?
  • Should it apply to one project or every project?
  • How should urgent questions be shown on return?

These choices remain for the person to settle.
