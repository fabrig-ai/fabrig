# Telling you

The person's own page of where temper pushes what needs them
(`../../draft/what-reaches-you.md`, section 9;
`../../domain/forge/attention.md`, section 7): this browser turned on or
off, and the devices pushes go to. It draws what the engine holds, with
no placeholder. Mail and the digest have no row: they are not built
(`../../domain/forge/attention.md`, 7.4).

- **Its address,** `Told`, at `/told`, of no project: the person's menu
  (`../view.md`, 3) and, on a phone, the `Accounts` tab's last row
  (`settings.md`, 1) open it. It takes the rail's frame and the column of
  Deployment's tabs, 720 wide, with no kicker and no tabs, under its own
  level-1 heading, "Telling you", and its lede, "When something needs
  you, temper pushes to the devices you turn this on for: how many things
  wait, and in which project, never what they say." No entry of the rail
  is current on it.
- **Its reads:** the deployment's read's `push`, which the client holds
  from its start (`../forge/records.md`); the `devices` subject, watched
  while the page is open (`../forge/records.md`); and this browser's
  state, which the shell reports (`../web/README.md`, 2.7): whether it
  can be pushed to, its notification permission, and its subscription's
  endpoint, if it holds one. Until each answers, the page keeps what it
  last knew (`README.md`).
- **With push off,** the deployment's read's `push` none: a quiet band,
  as Remembered's with none, the tag "Not set up", and "This deployment
  doesn't push. Its owner turns push on in its configuration." Nothing
  else.
- **This browser,** the first row: "This browser", in small type its
  state's words, and at its right its act:
  - *can't be pushed to,* no service worker or no push manager: "This
    browser can't be pushed to.", and, where its user agent names an
    iPhone or an iPad, "Add temper to the Home Screen, then open it from
    there and turn this on." No act.
  - *blocked,* its permission denied: "Notifications are blocked for
    this site in the browser's settings." No act.
  - *off,* no subscription, or one whose endpoint no row of `devices`
    holds: "Off", and "Turn on". A press asks the browser's permission,
    subscribes with the deployment's key, and sends `AddDevice` with the
    subscription's endpoint and keys and the label the client makes from
    its user agent: "BROWSER on SYSTEM", BROWSER one of Safari, Firefox,
    Chrome, Edge, and SYSTEM one of iPhone, iPad, Android, Mac, Windows,
    Linux, or "This browser" where either is unknown. "Turning on…"
    while it waits.
  - *on,* its subscription's endpoint a row's: "On since DAY", the
    row's `at` by `../view.md`, 6's day's rule, and "Turn off", which
    sends `RemoveDevice` of that row and, answered, drops the browser's
    subscription.
  - *A refusal* says why under the row, in a line: `off`, the band
    above; `service`, "This browser's push service isn't one this
    deployment sends to."; `too_many`, "You have 8 devices: remove one
    below first."; `malformed`, "This browser gave a subscription temper
    can't use."; `person`, "Only a person is pushed to."; the permission
    refused at its prompt, the blocked words.
- **Your devices,** under the heading "Your devices", a row each of
  `devices`, in number order: its label; under it, in small type, its
  push service's host and when it was added, "fcm.googleapis.com ·
  Today · Thursday 22 October"; "This browser" after the label where the
  row is this browser's; and "Remove" at its right, which sends
  `RemoveDevice`. With none: "No device is pushed to yet."
- **Its look** is Remembered's (`remembered.md`, *Its look*): rows of
  Deployment's, each a hairline under it; at 760 px and under, an act
  under its words, at least 44 pixels tall to a press.
- **The terminal** has no such page, and `Told` falls back to its
  launchpad, as `Remembered` does: being pushed to is a browser's.
- **Its tests:** the domain's reads and acts, each request as sent and
  each answer's page, as rows of `temper-client-domain`'s page tests,
  this browser's states given as the shell reports them; the view's rows
  and refusals as `temper-client-view`'s; the person's menu with its four
  items; the label's rule over user agents, a table; and the gallery's
  states (`../web/gallery.md`), each by day and by night.
