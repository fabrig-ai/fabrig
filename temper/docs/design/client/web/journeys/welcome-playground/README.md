# welcome-playground

A version-2 `walk` in one process, on the playground's composition,
signed in at `/welcome` by its printed link: no `temper box`
(`../../../journey-walks-world.md`, 1, and `../../../journey-walks.md`, 6). It sees and marks the welcome,
presses "Try the playground", then waits for the playground's banner
and "New chat" before marking again. The banner alone can arrive
before the project does.

- `01.html`: the welcome under its top bar, with temper's mark, Steps,
  the live link region and the night switch, no person or Sign out.
  The two cards offer "Set up temper" and "Try the playground"; the
  temper box paragraph follows their note. "Try the playground" leads
  to `02.html`.
- `02.html`: New chat in Demo, with the playground's one-line banner
  above main. The rail names `playground/widget` and offers Home,
  Needs you, Work, Ask temper and How work runs, then New change.
  Its foot has Set up temper, Live, Deployment ("a forge is setting
  up"), Owner's menu and the night switch. Away and the New draft or
  change menu are not drawn at this mark.

New chat shows Ready, `playground/widget`, the composer, and the three
suggested requests: "Add CSV export to widget", "Add a dark theme to
widget" and "Fix widget's empty search state". The model is `fake`
under Playground; no plan-or-key choices or Chat/Split/Draft switch
are drawn here. The composer hint is "Enter sends · Shift+Enter for a
new line". On a phone the rail and tab bar are hidden; the back link
returns to Chats.
