# welcome-playground

On a fresh `temper box --fake-backends`: the box's printed sign-in link
opened, the welcome drawn under its top bar, "Try the playground" pressed.

- `01.html`: the welcome as it was when "Try the playground" was
  pressed. Its "Try the playground" leads to `02.html`.
- `02.html`: `NewChat` in the Demo project, the playground started,
  its banner's one line above its main, as it was when the recording
  ended.

The rail names Demo, the recording's project, with its switcher,
Home, Needs you, Work, Ask temper and How work runs, with the New draft or
change menu below and Deployment and the night switch at its foot. New chat holds the composer and
the playground's suggested requests.
On a phone the rail and tab bar are hidden; the back link returns to Chats.
