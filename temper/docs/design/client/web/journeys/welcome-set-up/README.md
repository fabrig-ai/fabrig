# welcome-set-up

A version-2 `walk` in one process, on the playground's composition,
signed in at `/welcome` by its printed link. The world's box's peer
plays the owned Forgejo's set-up on the world's time: no `temper box`
(`../../../journey-walks-world.md`, 1.4, and `../../../journey-walks.md`, 6). The walk presses "Set up
temper", then Forgejo's "Run on this machine" and its sheet's own
"Run on this machine", leaving "A sample project" chosen.

Every page's top bar holds temper's mark, Steps, the live link region
and the night switch last, no person or Sign out.

- `01.html`: the welcome, with "Set up temper" and "Try the playground"
  cards. The temper box paragraph follows their note. The walk waits
  for "Set up temper", not just the heading, before marking; its link
  leads to `02.html`.
- `02.html`: "Connect a forge", with Forgejo and GitHub choices.
  Forgejo's "Run on this machine" link leads to `03.html`.
- `03.html`: "Local forge", a sheet over those choices.
  "A sample project" is selected, with no fields or name shown; its
  repository is `work/hello`. Its "Run on this machine" button leads
  to `04.html`.
- `04.html`: the Model step, "Connect a model", with the world's
  "A fake-provider API key" card and "Use an API key" link. The
  "Forge and model" pane names Forgejo on this machine, `work/hello`
  and "Setting up: fetching Forgejo". Its meter reads 25%; "Each step"
  has "Fetching Forgejo · 25%" under way.
- `05.html`: the same Model step, its pane saying "Setting up:
  preparing" with a busy meter. "Each step" has "Fetched Forgejo"
  done and "Preparing Forgejo" under way.
- `06.html`: the Model step, its pane saying "Setting up: starting".
  "Each step" has fetching and preparing done, "Starting Forgejo"
  under way.
- `07.html`: the Model step, its pane saying "Forgejo on this machine ·
  connected as temper · work/hello made". The steps and meter have
  gone; Model still says "none yet".

The forge's pane sits beside the model choices from 1280 px, below
otherwise. Pages `04.html` to `07.html` have no acted control: the
peer's reports and the engine's own work advance the set-up, and the
printed pages lead on by their neighbours.
