# The whole story's mockups: the web and the phone

Drawn 2026-11-08 with the user, for `../../../draft/whole-story.md`,
section 9, and `../../../draft/surfaces.md`, whose pages they draw. Plain
pages with one stylesheet, `whole-story.css`: open one from a checkout,
with no server. `index.html` is the shape, and links every page and
frame.

**They are the target** where they differ from the greenfield mock, which
stays the target where these draw nothing (`../../../draft/surfaces.md`,
section 13; `../../greenfield/README.md`).

- **At scale, with standing roles:** `home.html`, `needs.html`,
  `work.html`, `team.html` and `how.html`; on a phone, `phone-home.html`,
  `phone-need.html` and `phone-tell.html`. A Sunday morning on one
  deployment with four projects: vsr, vsr-rs, cowsql and fabrig.
- **`pair/`:** one person, one project, You approve.
- **`team/`:** three people, one project, Agents review with sign-offs by
  path.
- **The terminal's frames** are `../../tui/whole-story/`.

Names, numbers and questions in them are made up. Their words, parts,
order and look bind; their figures don't. Where a draft and a page
disagree on a mechanism, the draft wins and the page is redrawn.
