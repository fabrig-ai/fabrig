Direct in the terminal. Decide and know on the web.

A person does three things with temper. Agents and code do the rest. The web says where things stand and what needs you, on a laptop or a phone. The terminal is where you work with agents, one thing at a time. Both show the same objects in the same words.

Why

  • Work is goals, changes and the chats that started them, in one list. The project's way sets what it holds: changes at Pair, goals and drafts at scale.
  • Team is new: each area's owner, you or a standing role. Findings, reds and questions go to the owner.
  • Chats leave the rail. A conversation lives with what it's about, a goal's or a role's, and is had in the terminal. Ask temper keeps its place.
  • Settings splits: How work runs is the project's process, changed by proposal; Deployment keeps accounts, forges, workers and members.

Three kinds of agent session

Calls

One job, one run, done: producers, reviewers, foremen, surveys. Code writes their brief.

You meet what they made, never them.

Conversations

You are there: a design session, Ask temper, talking with a role. They last as long as you stay.

Each ends in something kept: a draft, a proposal, a rule.

Standing roles

They own an area for the project's life. Each wake is a short session from their rules, their Now and what woke them; they write their Now when it ends.

A budget per week, a report on Team. The goal's coordinator is one, bounded by its goal.
