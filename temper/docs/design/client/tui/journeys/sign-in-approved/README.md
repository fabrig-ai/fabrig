# sign-in-approved

A version-2 `walk` in one process, on the playground's composition,
with both shipped clients: no box or terminal
(`../../../journey-walks.md`, 4 and 6). The browser starts signed in
at `/welcome`; the TUI starts signed out. The walk sees and marks its
approval frame, approves the address it asked to browse in that browser,
then sees and marks the no-project launchpad.

- `01.html` and `01.txt`: the framed sign-in to `127.0.0.1:10001`,
  "A tab opened in your browser", the code beside "then press Approve."
  and the same code in `http://127.0.0.1:10001/terminal?code=CODE`.
  Below are the ten-minute expiry, the instruction to paste temper's
  printed link and press Enter, and the Link field with its example
  link's secret elided. The foot says Signed out and Ctrl+C to quit.
- `02.html` and `02.txt`: temper's mark and build `0.0.0 (000000000000)`,
  the service address, "No project yet", and "Setting temper up makes
  your first project: open http://127.0.0.1:10001 in a browser."
  The empty composer offers `/ for commands`; the foot says Live.

Both frames are 80 columns by 24 rows, in the dark scheme a terminal
that answers no scheme draws. Approval is a browser act, not a TUI
key: neither page has a key link; the pages lead on by their neighbours.
The world's fixed facts make the approval code reproducible, with no
recording's port or clocks. The writer prints the walk and removes its
old recording after landing.
