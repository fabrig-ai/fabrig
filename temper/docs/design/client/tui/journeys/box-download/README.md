# box-download

Hand-made pages, for the look request `tui-box-download`. No walk reaches
the box's front, since a walk's TUI is not in front
(`../../../journey-walks.md`, 3, *Its start*), and the TUI has no gallery.
They are drawn from `temper-client-tui`'s goldens
`welcome-fetching-80x24-dark.txt`, `welcome-150x39-dark.txt` and
`starting-fetching-80x24-dark.txt`, and from a person's recording of a
release build's first `temper box`, at 150 by 39 in the dark scheme,
whose saved frame stood at 15.4 of 24.1 MB, 17.6 s into the download.
Their values are written in by hand, and no journey's print will equal
them.

- `01.html` and `01.txt` the request, at 150 by 39: the recording's
  moment. The lead says the link comes once the box starts, and the
  link's place holds "Downloading temper-box, once for this build", its
  bar, "15.4 of 24.1 MB" and "about 10 s left". The quiet line's right
  is empty.
- `02.html` and `02.txt` the request, at 80 by 24: the same moment, with
  ", once for this build" left out for room.
- `03.html` and `03.txt` the request: every byte in. The link's place
  says "Starting the box…", and the quiet line's right holds the box's
  last log line, as today.
- `04.html` and `04.txt` the request: a box set up before, on the
  starting screen, its box step under way with the same bar.

All four are in the dark scheme. They lead on by their neighbours, with
no key link.
