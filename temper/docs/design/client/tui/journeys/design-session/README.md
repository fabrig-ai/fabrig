# design-session

On a fresh `temper box --fake-backends`: the browser signed in by the
box's printed link, the setup following `project-landing` to Hello with
its home repository, then `temper` started at the box's address. The
browser approves the terminal. `/draft Plan a farewell line for hello.`
opens a design session; the fake drafter writes `farewell-line.md` and
asks where the line should go. Ctrl-P cycles half, all, folded and half;
"1" sends the recommended location. The drafter settles Open questions,
and `/goal` starts a goal by the project's first way, Task by task.

Each number has an `.html` page and a `.txt` frame:

- `01.html` and `01.txt`: Hello's launchpad, its Start commands and quiet
  line, marked by Alt+Shift+M. Home's facts have not answered yet, so
  the framed header has no headline.
- `02.html` and `02.txt`: the opening `/draft` words in the launchpad's
  box, before Enter sends them.
- `03.html` and `03.txt`: the session at half, the first draft's sections
  marked new, and the drafter's three choices under the box.
- `04.html` and `04.txt`: that half pane before Ctrl-P widens it.
- `05.html` and `05.txt`: the draft at all, from its head, the choices
  still under the box.
- `06.html` and `06.txt`: that all pane before Ctrl-P folds it.
- `07.html` and `07.txt`: folded, the conversation alone, with the
  choices under the box.
- `08.html` and `08.txt`: folded before Ctrl-P opens the half pane again.
- `09.html` and `09.txt`: half again with "1" typed, before Enter sends
  "At the end of README.md" as the person's words.
- `10.html` and `10.txt`: the second reply says nothing blocks the work;
  the half pane begins at Open questions, the only section marked new,
  now settling the farewell line's location at the end of README.md.
- `11.html` and `11.txt`: `/goal` typed, its command offered under the
  box, before Enter opens the start sheet.
- `12.html` and `12.txt`: "Start a goal from farewell-line.md. How should
  it run?", Task by task first and active, then Plan, tasks, land whole

- `13.html` and `13.txt`: that prompt before Enter takes Task by task.
- `14.html` and `14.txt`: the returned chat with its goal linked, the
  revised section still marked new. The walk checks `/goal` is no longer

- `15.html` and `15.txt`: that chat at the recording's end. The quiet
  line's goal part is left out at 80 columns, as `pages.md`'s narrow rule
  says (`../../pages.md`, *A design session*).

The rule above the box names Hello, the chat's shortened title, T2 and
`127.0.0.1:PORT`, not "box": this TUI reads no engine's link file
(`../../journeys.md`, 8.2). The quiet line uses the fake box's GPT model
and high effort, rather than the illustrative mockup's Opus and xhigh.
The pane has its filename alone and marks whole Markdown sections; the
choices also stand under the box, and the quiet line says what the next
Ctrl-P does, as `../../pages.md`, *A design session*, specifies.
