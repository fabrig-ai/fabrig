# The whole story's mockups: the terminal

Drawn 2026-11-08 with the user, for `../../../draft/whole-story.md`,
section 9, and `../../../draft/surfaces.md`, section 11, which revises
`../../../draft/tui.md`, section 3. Each page is one frame of the TUI,
110 columns by 32 lines in the dark theme, drawn with the web's
stylesheet, `../../web/whole-story/whole-story.css`.

- **At scale, with standing roles:** `launchpad.html`,
  `design-session.html` (a design session with the draft in the side
  pane) and `role.html` (talking with a standing role).
- **`pair/launchpad.html`** and **`team/launchpad.html`:** the
  launchpad at the spectrum's other two points.

Their data is made up. The frames are the target for the screens' parts,
words and order; the TUI's colours stay the terminal's own
(`../../../draft/tui.status.md`).
