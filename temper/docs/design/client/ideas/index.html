temper, driving work from drafts: ideas

The first increment is ideas 1 to 3, with away mode (4) next. Each page also works at phone width: narrow the window, or use the browser's device view.

The first increment

  1. Draft session: a long session on the laptop, a chat writing a draft, the draft beside it.
  2. Start a goal: Start goal on the draft; how it runs comes from the project's ways of working.
  3. Goal: its agent's status line and report first, then tasks, landing, decisions; steering is a message.
  4. Home: since you last looked, Waiting on you, each goal's status line. Inbox and Board merge into it.
  5. Waiting on you: the phone's check-in, choices as buttons, answered in a row.

Next

  • Back from away: decided for you, each with Overturn; what always waits still waited.

Flexibility

  • A small project: the same home with chats only; each part appears when the project uses it.
  • How work runs: per project: the parts shown, its ways of working, who writes status, what waits on you, the away limits, notifications.

What moved, compared with today

  • The navigation is per project: Home, Waiting, Goals, Chats, Drafts, Changes. Forges, Accounts and Builders move to the project's settings, under the person's name.
  • The side pane keeps chats and adds drafts.
  • New pieces: the away switch in the banner, agent-written text with a byline (✻), questions as cards with numbered choices.
