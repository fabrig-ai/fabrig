Away mode

Draft, 2026-11-03, with the user.

1. In one page

  • A setting, per project or for all: "I'm away" until a time, or until turned off.
  • While away, agents decide what they would ask about, within the kinds the project allows (section 3).
  • Each such decision shows in Waiting on you as decided, with an Overturn while it is cheap (section 4).
  • Coming back opens on what was decided (section 5).

3. What agents may decide

  • Implementation choices within a task's spec: on.
  • Retries and splitting a failing task: on.
  • A design document's wording, not its decisions: on.
  • Adding a dependency: off.
  • Always wait, fixed: budgets, sign-ins, machines, what is hard to reverse.

4. Decided for you

Each decision keeps the question, the choice taken and why, in the goal's decisions and in Waiting on you, marked decided…

Open questions

  1. How long can a decision be overturned after its change landed?
