Decided by goal 8650's coordinator, 2026-11-19.
  - 5.5: "Enter is the screen's one key, with no field" is, at a name, a focused "Ask again" button under the lines that Enter or Space presses, standing once the approval has ended or wasn't asked; while an ask is in flight or a typed code's ask waits there is no button and no key does anything (`../client/tui/pages/sign-in.md`, 3). skein's UI kit has no region that acts on a key (skein's `ui/tree.md`, 2.1), and its vocabulary isn't grown for this (draft-4, 2026-11-19).
