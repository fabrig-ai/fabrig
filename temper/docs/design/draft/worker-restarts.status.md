# worker-restarts: status

2026-11-24. Where the design lags or departs from [worker-restarts.md](worker-restarts.md).

Section 10's redeploy tool is the deployments' operator's, outside this repository, as the draft has it (the user, 2026-11-20): fabrig ships none. What it runs on a worker's host is temper's: the engine's request that the worker drain, `temper ctl DIR worker drain WORKER`, and the wait for it (`../client/ctl/workers.md`), the worker's stop, a drain within its stop bound (`../domain/worker/README.md`, section 2), and its start.

- **Design lags:** none.
- **Design departs:**
  - 7: a workspace a crash left is saved at its take-up, by the next start of its run's workstream on that worker, before its agent starts, not at the worker's start: the worker holds no git identity until an assignment grants one (`../protocol/credentials-git-identities.md`, section 1), and the engine sends that attempt back at once (jig's `domain/engine/executors.md`, 6.5). A workspace no such start takes up is never saved, and its crash costs what its last save left out, as before (`../domain/worker/checkouts.md`, *A workspace kept for its run*). The goal's designer, 2026-11-24.
  - 7: a crash's workspace is taken up by any start of its workstream whose repositories its mark names, not only a saved start as a drained one is: the engine learns of no save from a crashed attempt, so the next one may be a branch start. Its take-up's save makes it a saved start of that save's commit (same section). The goal's designer, 2026-11-24.
  - 7: the worker finds a crash's workspaces by a mark each hold with a saved-work destination writes before its run starts, `held`, kept current and removed at its release, in the kept mark's file at `temper-kept 2`; a hold with no saved-work destination writes none, so its workspace still goes to the trash (same section). The goal's designer, 2026-11-24.
  - 7: of the calls a crash cuts, only a command and a `job` wait tell a start, an `interrupted` result part with no time and no output, which their end's supersedes, so the next activation reads them interrupted after an unknown time. Any other workspace tool a crash cut with no part runs again, since it ends in moments, and checks, saves, host calls and sub-agents resume by their own rules (smith's `domain/turn-parts.md`, sections 3 and 5). No wire, part kind or stored version moves. The goal's designer, 2026-11-24.
  - 9, test 4: held in the three worlds by layer, as tests 1 to 3 are: the agent's half in `smith/tests/run/` (a resume from the parts a crash left), the worker's in `temper/tests/worker/domain/` (the workspace kept at the next start and saved at its take-up) and the engine's in `jig/tests/channel/` (a worker killed with its agent, its attempt suspended at its new process's hello, no try spent, resumed on the same worker with a real agent). The goal's designer, 2026-11-24.
  - 3: the answer committed on arrival is a part, a new kind on jig's workers' wire from version 22 (`part`, with `part kept` and `part busy`, jig's `protocol/channel/records.md`, 5), beside the turn, which is still told and committed whole once its last call has answered; each call's answer is a part too. smith's turn record moves to version 8 only for the `interrupted` outcome (smith's `protocol/transcript.md`, section 6). Parts cross only where the agreed version has them, and a worker on an older link drains at the turn's end, as before; so no floor moves. A worker of the new build meeting an engine of the old one closes its link, its turns' reverse not total, and waits for its engine to go first, as `redeploys.md`, 7.2, orders a redeploy. The goal's designer, 2026-11-21.
  - 3: the grace before the calls are cut is the worker's `graces.drain_grace_ms`, 10 seconds when absent, beside the drain's bound, carried to the agent in its drain (jig's `domain/hosts/hub.md`, 7; smith's `protocol/channel.md`, section 3). The goal's designer, 2026-11-21.
  - 4: a kept workspace is matched to the resumed attempt by its workstream and its saved commit, not by the attempt, which the assignment does not name: a saved start of that workstream at the commit its save made takes it up; any other makes it again (`../domain/worker/checkouts.md`, *A workspace kept for its run*). The checks' results directory is `.git/smith-check` in each repository's tree, named by the worker in smith's start and passed to the check as `SMITH_CHECK_RESULTS` (smith's `domain/run-delivery.md`, 1.1). The goal's designer, 2026-11-21.
  - 6: a host call is asked anew under the resumed activation's name only when no host decided it; one decided in any earlier activation is in the start's answered calls under its own name, whether a drain settled its relay or the engine settled it after losing the worker, and an engine with an unsettled decided effect places the run again only once it settles; a call the cut left unstarted runs on resume rather than going back as interrupted, since it never ran (the goal's coordinator, 2026-11-22).
  - 9, tests 1 to 3: each is held in the three worlds by its layer: the agent's half in `smith/tests/run/`, the worker's (parts relayed, the workspace and results directory kept and taken up) in `temper/tests/worker/domain/`, and the engine's (the answer committed, no try spent, the parts handed back on the same worker) in `jig/tests/channel/`, whose worker is jig's and keeps no workspace. The goal's designer, 2026-11-21.
  - 4: the window a resumed attempt waits for its worker is the restart wait itself, `fleet.restart_wait` (`jig/docs/design/domain/engine/executors.md`, 6.4), with no setting of its own defaulting to it: one figure serves both, as `redeploys.md`, 12, has the restart wait serve every worker. The goal's designer, 2026-11-20.
  - 7: a task whose previous attempt was suspended by its worker coming back as a new process has its next such end counted lost, spending a try (`jig/docs/design/domain/engine/executors.md`, 6.1), so that a run that brings its worker down is not tried for ever. The goal's designer, 2026-11-20.
  - 8: `link.ping_ms` and `link.silence_ms` wait for a worker's next start, though they are timeouts, and so does the engine's own copy of every time bound but the link's (`../protocol/configuration/limit-settings.md`, 5.2 and 11). Ping and silence make the stop bound a worker's hello states, which the engine checks against its grace and holds runs by (`redeploys.md`, 5.3), so a running link can't take them without a new opening; the engine's children copy their own time bounds at construction. The draft's aim, a restart only where one is needed, holds: every time bound a worker reads but those two is live. Decided by the goal's design, 2026-11-20.
  - 9, test 7: in `temper-worker-service`'s own tests, not `temper/tests/worker/domain/`, since a worker's figures reach its service, which the domain world below it doesn't hold (`../protocol/configuration/figures.md`, 9). Decided by the goal's design, 2026-11-20.
  - 9: test 6, a signal to an agent while its host lives, is in smith's agent-process world (`smith/tests/agent-process/`), where the agent's signals are tested, rather than `smith/tests/run/`; and test 8, with real processes, is a story of temper's process tier (`temper/bin/temper-box/tests/process.rs`), whose stories need real signals, so a plan writes and compiles it and it runs after landing (`AGENTS.md`, section 5). The designer, 2026-11-20.
  - 2: the ctl verbs are `worker pause`, `worker drain` and `worker resume` (`../client/ctl/workers.md`), and a pause is the engine's placement as well as the worker's, so a worker of an older build is kept from new work though it cannot drain (jig's `domain/engine/fleet.md`). The designer, 2026-11-20.
- **Open:** none.
