# cli: status

2026-11-12. Where the design lags or departs from [cli.md](cli.md).

- **Design lags:**
  - 5: `../release.md`, sections 1, 2 and 5, give a release one file, the whole product, saved as `temper`.
- **Design departs:**
  - 3.5: `skein/docs/foundation/cli.md`, 6, also disallows `skein_shell::arguments` and `skein_shell::environment` outside a binary's capture, since they read the process's for any caller. fabrig/ui's reading, 2026-11-09, in C1's cut.
  - 3.6: `skein/docs/foundation/cli.md`, 7's example wraps the help's long lines at 80 columns, as 3.6's rule asks, where the draft's example runs past it. fabrig/ui's reading, 2026-11-09, in C1's cut.
  - 4.1: `temper` has an internal command the draft's table doesn't list, `internal fetch-box`: `temper box` runs the finding and the download of `temper-box` in a child of its own executable, its lines on a pipe, so the TUI draws while it runs and nothing of the fetch shares the TUI's loop (`../client/box-binary.md`, 4). C3's designer's reading, 2026-11-11.
  - 5: `temper box` hands the box five descriptors, not two: beside the link's and the lines' pipes, fd 0 a pipe `temper` holds, whose end stops the box, so that `temper`'s quit and its death stop it on Linux and macOS alike with no parent-death signal, and fds 1 and 2 two pipes `temper` reads into one tail for what the box says before its log is open (`../protocol/setup/box.md`, 6). The box runs in a process group of its own, as skein's spawn makes every child's, so it stops by its life pipe alone, not by a signal to `temper`'s group (`../client/tui/box.md`, *The box's start*). C3's designer's reading, 2026-11-11; the two pipes and the own group C3's coordinator's, 2026-11-11, since skein's spawn hands no descriptor twice and spawns no child in its parent's group.
  - 5: `temper box` with `--echo steps` runs the box in its own place in a terminal too, since the steps' echo is the terminal's (`../client/box-binary.md`, 5). C3's designer's reading, 2026-11-11.
  - 5.2: a failed download stays on the TUI's screen until the person closes it, then `temper` prints why and the `curl` line, exit 1, so the screen's words can be read (`../client/tui/box.md`). The `curl` line puts the file beside `temper`, where the first look finds it, since a cached file without its sum is fetched again (`../client/box-binary.md`, 3). C3's designer's reading, 2026-11-11.
  - 6: the playground's page is `temper-playground-page`, and the shipped page's wasm start moves out of `temper-client-shell-web` into `temper-client-web-page`, the bundle's entry, since a module has one start and the playground's crate links the shell (`../client/web/README.md`, 2.1). C5's designer's reading, 2026-11-12.
  - 6: the page's internal settings are on when their value is `1`, `internal-small-window` and `internal-loopback-navigation` honoured when the page's host is `127.0.0.1` or `[::1]`, and taken out of the address at start, so that no route or recording sees them and a reload is a load without them (`../client/web/README.md`, 2.1). C5's designer's reading, 2026-11-12.
  - 6: `internal-record` is honoured on any origin, and the page records too by its `temper-record` meta, which `--record` asks of the deployment, where this section honours every internal setting on a loopback origin alone (`record-anywhere.md`, sections 2 and 3; `../client/web/README.md`, 2.1 and 2.2). The user's, 2026-11-12.
  - 6: the records' reading at start, `temper-protocol-records-forge`, is linked by the engine's service, so by `temper-engine` and `temper-box`, and not by `temper-worker`, whose service reads no forge's records: the binary that needs a crate depends on it (3.2; `../protocol/components.md`). C5's designer's reading, 2026-11-12.

To reverse: `ctl` leaves `temper` for `temper-box` alone, and a deployment's people and agents have no `ctl` without the whole product.

For the user: that draft's words, and the deployment's setting.

For the user: those drafts' words.
