# Trying temper, in frames

The mockups of `../trying-temper.md`, section 8: the try, step by step,
the browser and the terminal side by side. A first pass, drawn by hand
on 2026-11-19 (the user: "let you give a first stab to the mockups").

- **`index.html`:** the journey, its eleven steps in order, each with
  its browser pages and its terminal frames and what each shows.
- **The switches:** each change of the draft's section 4 has one, at the
  top of `index.html`. On, a page draws the change; off, what temper
  does today. A page opened alone takes them from its address,
  `w08-chat.html#off=46,48` drawing today's chat and today's card.
- **The browser's pages,** `wNN-*.html`, one per frame, in the greenfield
  mock's look (`../../client/web/greenfield/mock.css`), with what the try
  adds to it in `trying.css`. Today's pages are the client's as its
  documents say (`../../client/pages/welcome.md`,
  `../../client/pages/home.md`, `../../client/view.md`, 5), not prints.
- **The terminal's frames,** in `index.html` itself, at 80 columns in
  the TUI's light theme (`../../client/tui/journeys/theme.css`),
  illustrative as `../../client/tui/box.md`'s frames are.
- **Not drawn:** Anthropic's and Claude Code's own screens, Forgejo's
  pages, GitHub's way (`../trying-temper.md`, 6) and the playground
  (`../trying-temper.md`, 7).

Open `index.html` from a checkout; it needs no server.
