## 2. Order

- **Main stays redeployable to dev.fabrig.ai at every landing** (the user, 2026-11-27): a project without `.temper/ways.toml` runs exactly as it does today, a turn without `share` packs as today's does, and stored values and wires follow the upgrades rule, each build talking to the one before it (`../protocol/wires.md`). A by-hand step dev.fabrig.ai needs for a landing is named in the goal's result under `## Dev`, with the setting or file it changes, and done by ops with the redeploy that carries it; the aim is none. fabrig adopts its own ways only by the user's step below, so until then dev.fabrig.ai runs its work as today whatever has landed.

- **Without the web or the TUI first** (the user, 2026-11-27): W1 to W9 and W11 to W13 change no page, and run while the user's UX pass goes on. Clients are rebuilt for the wire's new fields, as at any release, and an older client reloads. W10 and W14 come after the pass.
- W1, W2, W3, then W4: the core. Then W5 to W8, each needing W4 alone, W6 also the speeds milestone. Then W9, its entries released as their needs land. W12 and W13, the landing, last. W11, Ask temper's review after an upgrade, is left out for now: a project's files change only by its own changes meanwhile.
- **fabrig's own ways** are a step of the user's, not a goal's: once W4 and W9 have landed and dev.fabrig.ai runs them, fabrig picks its ways and writes them, with its charters whole from today's stock and fabrig's overlay words, by a change like any project's. dev.fabrig.ai's lean goal becomes fabrig's own way then, and its own lines stay its deployment overlay.
- Pair is [pair.md](pair.md)'s, built by its own goal after W4. Until it lands, the stock and the gallery offer Hand it over and Milestone, and a chat keeps its template.
- The hooks are [hooks.md](hooks.md)'s own goals, already running.
