# forge-reads-targeted: status

2026-11-11. Where the design lags or departs from [forge-reads-targeted.md](forge-reads-targeted.md).

Items 1 and 5 are designed, with item 6's text for them: every containment reader reads the ancestry range (`../domain/forge/keeping-up.md`, 3, and `../domain/forge/branches.md`, 2; skein's `forge/client.md`, sections 4 and 7), a comparison reads names and counts alone (skein's `forge/README.md`, 3.2 and 3.3), and item 3's narrow lists are designed (`PullFor` head/base filters, `Item` since, and branch names bounded by bytes alone; skein's `forge/README.md`, 3.7, and `forge/forgejo.md`, 1.2; `skein/docs/design/forge/github.md`, 4.1), runs are resolved once per call and a short first status page supplies `Pull`'s combined state (skein's `forge/forgejo.md`, 1.1 and 1.2), and a `TooLarge` names its bound and size (skein's `forge/README.md`, 3.4; `../domain/forge/change/repairs.md`; `../domain/agent/tools.md`). Item 2 is designed, with item 6's text for it: each commit's CI read once in a gather, `Pull`'s `ci` and `PullFor` with none, a commit's hint reading no pull request, and a checking change's CI on the runner's events (`../domain/forge/keeping-up.md`, 2 and 3; skein's `forge/README.md`, 3.2, `forge/forgejo.md`, 1.1 and 1.2, `forge/client.md`, 4). Item 4 is designed for Forgejo, with item 6's text for it: review, remark and pull-file pages sized for real rows, read on past a cut with `skip` (skein's `forge/README.md`, 3.2 and 3.3, `forge/forgejo.md`, 1.1 to 1.3, `forge/client.md`, 4; `../domain/agent/tools.md`).

- **Design lags:**
  - Item 1: goal overlap reads a pull request's files, both sides, for their paths alone (`../domain/forge/README.md`, 5.3); a pull request's paths alone wait for contents on demand (skein's `forge/README.md`, section 10).
  - Item 4: skein's `forge/github.md`, 3 sizes GitHub's issue, review and pull-file pages for the largest row each listing may hold; Forgejo's are sized for real rows.
