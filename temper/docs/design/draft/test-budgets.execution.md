# test-budgets: execution

2026-11-16. How [test-budgets.md](test-budgets.md) gets carried out: in small steps, each landing on its own before the next in its stream starts from main, so no branch grows large or old. It designs nothing: each milestone writes its own design from the draft as it runs, and lands whole. Plans use the focused and fuzzy suites alone. The gate holds nothing until its runner, outside this repository, reads what B3 and B4 write, and the delivery check nothing until B6 lands and the builders that run goals keep main's ledgers (section 2).

## 2. Order

- **Each milestone is a goal,** started once the user approves it.
- **No priority mark:** budgets aren't one of v0.1.0's critical pieces. Its plans start when places are free and marked work isn't about to arrive.
- **B7 first** (the user, 11-17): a hundredth's noise holds nearly every change red in a layer over at main, so B7 starts at once, ahead of other work. Judging each selection as it stands, without main's ledger, while it ran made every selected layer over at main the change's red, so the gate kept the ledger.
- **Back to red** (the user, 11-18 12:4xZ). The gate warns on budgets until B8 has landed and the same-work backlog's lines stand (`test-budgets.md`, 6). Meanwhile the drafter reads each gate's would-be reds: a flagged test past its limit that its change didn't cause takes a line, the same day, by a mechanical change of `tools/tiers/budgets.toml` alone; a new test past its reference and a layer's growth are the change's own. After ten gates in a row, both suites, with no flagged test past its limit, the gate goes back to red, and the delivery check follows at the next worker restart the user confirms. B9 and B10 run beside it, from the start.
- **One at a time within a stream,** each from main once the one before it has landed. B5 runs beside the budgets stream.
- **The gate's runner, outside this repository,** follows B3, B4 and B5: it keeps the ledger beside its reports, passes it and the gate's configuration to its runs, runs a run's flagged tests alone at the base and judges again with their figures (`tools/tiers/references.md`, 5), makes red cases of what they judge red, and reads its cgroup's stalls for the wall (`test-budgets.md`, 7). Until it does, B2 to B4's verdicts print and hold nothing. For B6, after each landing it copies main's ledger for each suite to each builder that runs goals, into the directory `TIERS_LEDGER` names there, set as the builders' deployment sets `TIERS_HOLD` (`test-budgets.md`, 3); until then every delivery check warns.
- **For B8, the gate's runner** runs B8's check on the candidate and its base, as it runs the citations check, and makes a red case of each refusal.
- **The figures after landing:** each layer's figure, budget and ideal, from the gate's report, read by whoever runs the CI builder. No plan's.
