# codec-shape: status

2026-11-21. Where the design lags or departs from [codec-shape.md](codec-shape.md).

- **Design lags:**
  - 4.3 and 4.4: the wires' translation stays written by hand. `skein/docs/design/data/codec/maps.md`, 9, defers their maps files, one per end, until their versions freeze; `layouts-both-ways.md`'s L5 follows that freeze (`layouts-both-ways.execution.md`, 1).
  - 6.1: the `.layouts` lists, the patches' tables and the kept texts stay while versions move (`skein/docs/design/data/codec/command.md`, 1 and 2), until `layouts-both-ways.md`'s goals remove them.
- **Design departs:**
  - 3.1: generated code carries two scoped expectations, each emitted only where its lint fires: `too_many_lines` and `struct_excessive_bools` (`skein/docs/design/data/codec/golden.md`, section 1, "Generated files"). `skein-codec`'s helpers carry scoped expectations where a module says why, as `skein-lib`'s do. The repository's rule is a scoped `#[expect]` with a reason, never `#[allow]` (`AGENTS.md`, 2), so the draft's "no allowances" holds as no `allow`. Decided by goal K6's designer, 2026-11-21.
  - 5.1: no family's drift test checks its schema's constructs. The classifier's whole range is the list: its matches over the `Type`, `Shape` and `Held` tables have no rest arm, and the generator's tests hold each construct listed once. A schema can use no unlisted construct the classifier can name, so a family check catches nothing more. A new table variant fails skein-codegen's build until listed, then the pin's check until `every-v3` holds it. A construct the grammar gains other than as a table variant needs the list's edit by hand either way. Decided by goal K6's designer, 2026-11-21, on the drafter's word after K3's review.
  - 4.2: the maps file says more than the domain's type, renames and converters: where a variant's payload sits in the domain (its fields inline, or one value), a record that stands for its one field, the domain's container or box where it isn't the record's (`slice`, `boxed`: the generator reads no domain type, and a conversion to its own type fails the workspace's lints), the paths of the records, the by-hand module and the context, all in the schema's order (`skein/docs/design/data/codec/maps.md`, 2 and 3). The survey's exhaustive count found these shapes in every owner. Decided by goal K5's designer, 2026-11-21.
  - 4.3: generated code also calls the owner's translation of a `by hand` declaration, passing the family's context, so that a parent that only copies stays generated above a child of the owner's shape (`skein/docs/design/data/codec/maps.md`, 4.2). Decided by goal K5's designer, 2026-11-21.
  - 4.2: a family whose every record is the owner's shape, jig's registry and secrets, keeps no maps file, and its translation stays by hand. Decided by goal K5's designer, 2026-11-21.
  - 3.2: encoding checks a list by its length, the count that becomes bytes, where today's constructors check its capacity: capacity bounds a value's heap in memory, and a value past its limits may now exist there. Decided by goal K3's designer, 2026-11-18.
  - 3.2: a lean module names each path with a constant of its `paths` module, beside the names' table, so hand-written code and tests name a refusal's field as they named a `Path` variant, and a renamed field fails their build. Decided by goal K3's designer, 2026-11-18.
  - 3.3: the other families followed with the line criterion met and compile not shown slower. Lines: 18,090, 42% of the survey's 42,813. Compile, on builder elapsed time (CPU was not measurable through the build wrapper): the tasks crate and clippy about 35% faster beyond their spreads, the two store builds' spreads overlapping both ways, and one store unit 5.5% slower beyond its spread in the no-schema case (3.09 s to 3.26 s). Decode, timed alone, lean against sealed on the tasks rows: within about 10% on release (successful full rows about 21% faster), the test-profile gap is addressed with dev/test package optimization (level 2, tasks level 1 to keep its compile bound) and concrete debug-only lean root decode entries, leaving release's helpers and default entry unchanged. The paired layer-duration proxy medians fall from 30.969 to 28.165 s for jig protocol, 66.724 to 58.263 s for jig simulated, and 14.598 to 13.151 s for smith simulated (still 0.151 s above its 13 s budget); these are not gate CPU measurements, and smith's families are still sealed in this measurement's base. Both compile medians stay within their base's spread and K3's lean spread. K4's decode-only and the dev/test layer figures are beside K3's in `temper/docs/development/codec-shape-k3-figures.md`, 6 and 7. The drafter read compile as not slower and had decoding timed alone before the other families followed, 2026-11-19.
  - 5.2: a root whose encoded bound at the ceilings passes the memory test's 64 MiB cap is decoded at its largest value under lower limits, its encoded and decoded bounds at the ceilings pinned as literals beside it in its owner's `largest_memory.rs`: 16 roots of jig's client wire (the largest, `ReviewItem`, 2.5×10⁹ bytes), 7 of jig's store (tasks' `Row`, 4.2×10⁸), 2 of jig's worker wire, 6 of smith's families and 7 of temper's (its store's `Row`, 2.8×10⁹). Every other root is decoded at the ceilings. Decided by goal K3's designer, 2026-11-18.
  - 5.2: the values a family's tests decode are built by `skein-codegen`'s fixed rule from the schema's text (`fixed_values` and `largest`), not by `skein-codec`'s generic values, which build a value only through a patch's machine (`skein/docs/design/data/codec/golden.md`, 1, and `skein/docs/design/data/codec/run-time.md`, 2). The goal K1's designer, 2026-11-18.
  - 5.2: a family keeps the fuzzing tests it has; one with none gains none, its bytes being its kinds', which `every` pins. The goal K1's designer, 2026-11-18.
  - 5.1: the completeness check lists the grammar's constructs and no shape of the output: lean code groups no booleans and splits no function, so neither has a construct to pin. Decided by the drafter, 2026-11-19.
  - 5.1: `every` is completed as a third version, `every-v3.schema`, since its second is frozen by the landed patch that names it (`skein/docs/design/data/codec/command.md`, 2); its first two versions stay for the patch story. The goal K1's designer, 2026-11-18.
  - 5.3: `jig-client-view`'s rendered views stay under its `golden/rendered/`, snapshots of their own subject, which 5.3 leaves aside; its codec goldens beside them go. The goal K1's designer, 2026-11-18.
  - 6.1: a `fixed N` field's value stays `[u8; N]`: N is the field's width, a value's shape that only that field's change touches, not a count of what the schema holds. Every other array whose length counts the schema's items goes. Decided by goal K2's designer, 2026-11-18.
  - 6.1: skein's codec fixtures outside `src/generated/` (`skein/tests/codec/src/every/`, `lints/`, `scalars/`, `secrets/`) keep files named by version, since each holds several versions of one family side by side for its stories (`skein/docs/design/data/codec/command.md`, 1). Decided by goal K2's designer, 2026-11-18.
  - 6.2: the merge job runs the generator's command on a family's schema texts (`temper/docs/design/protocol/tooling.md`, 4.1), not the family's drift test: every drift test's world builds the codec crates whose files are left in conflict, so it cannot build until they are settled, while the command builds `skein-codegen` alone. The drift test checks the result at delivery. Decided by goal K2's designer, 2026-11-18.
- **Open:** none.
