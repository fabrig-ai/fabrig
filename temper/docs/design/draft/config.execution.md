## 2. Order

- **Eager.** N1, N3, N5 and N7 start at once, each its own goal, in parallel. N2 and N8 start once N1 lands, N4 once N3 lands, and N6 once N2, N4, N5 and N7 have.
- **dev.fabrig.ai moves at its operators' pace.** The steps of several landings are taken together at its next redeploy, in their landings' order, each redeploy on the user's yes.
- **No priority mark.** v0.1.0's critical pieces are the migration and UI with looks; these plans start when places are free and marked work isn't about to arrive.
- **N6 last,** so that the file's format changes once, for the keys left.
