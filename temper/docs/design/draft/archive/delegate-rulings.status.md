# delegate-rulings: status

2026-11-17. Where the design lags or departs from [delegate-rulings.md](delegate-rulings.md).

Designed: 1 in `../../domain/forge/change/questions.md`, 1 (*Its answers, kept for later work*, *Its amendments, kept for later work* and *What every delegate is told*), `../../domain/forge/change/README.md`, 1, `../../domain/forge/change/repairs.md` (the preservation words and a resolution's instructions) and `../../domain/forge/change/gates.md`, 1 (a review's words and the review charter's line), with the stock implementer's line; 2 in `../../domain/forge/change/questions.md`, 1 (*Its requester's words and amendments*) and `../../domain/forge/change/gates.md`, 1 (*Valid at a spec revision*); 3 in those documents and their status files. Section 1's *Today* predates the block a review, repair and resolution already ended with (`What this change is asked now`, 2026-11-15): the design grows that block into the context the draft asks for.

- **Design lags:** none.
- **Design departs:**
  - 1: the context is read from the change task (its words and spec revision) and the change's own record, which keeps its 16 newest answers, as it did, and now its 16 newest amendments' reasons, each with its spec revision, rather than from the core's retained history through a bounded read. The step that makes a delegate reads no history: jig loads a task's history only for a read a party or a run makes, event by event, and the record outlives the inbox and a restart as the draft asks. Two amendments that reach the change's inbox before one step merge there into the newer (jig's `domain/tasks/messages.md`, 2), so the older reason is not kept; the spec revision kept holds both amendments' words. The goal's designer, 2026-11-17.
  - 1: the context is required within the delegate's spec words, the brief's task section, which jig's plan keeps as required and the charter's fit cuts last, rather than a section of its own that `finish_brief_plan` plans: the draft names `change_delegate` as what gives it, and a section of its own would add a gathered source and a budget for text the task section already carries whole at the stock's bounds. The goal's designer, 2026-11-17.
  - 1: rulings past the context's room are counted, `[N older rulings left out]`, with no read that gives them, where a cut spec says where it is read whole (`read_task` on the change); the record keeps 16 of each, so only a spec bound smaller than they are reaches it. The goal's designer, 2026-11-17.
  - 1: a producer's words are the change's, kept whole (`../../domain/forge/change/README.md`, 1: a change is never refused or held for the length of its words), so a change whose words fill its producer's spec bound gives that producer no context. Every other delegate is held rather than made without its context's fixed lines. The goal's designer, 2026-11-17.
  - 2: a live producer is amended only where its words before their context change, as before, so `producer_amendment.rs` passes with its stories as they are, its expected spec words now ending with the producer's context; a live repair or resolution is amended by every amendment, its context holding the reason. The goal's designer, 2026-11-17.
  - 2: a review's verdict counts only at jig's spec revision, which moves with any amendment of the spec or of the task's policy (its dependencies, its authority, its wake), so an amendment of a change's dependencies alone asks its reviews again. Telling an amendment of the words from one of the policy alone would need the change's former words, which no step keeps. The goal's designer, 2026-11-17.
- **Open:** none.
