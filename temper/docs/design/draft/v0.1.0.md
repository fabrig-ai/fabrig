# v0.1.0

Draft, 2026-10-26, from sessions with the user; its top rung is the
reference case of 2026-11-02, the TUI's first pass joined its parts
on 2026-11-07, the whole story on 2026-11-08, and the command line the same day. How temper gets to v0.1.0: the bar it
must pass, how that bar is climbed, the parts it needs, and their
order. Each part that is product has a draft of its own, which this one
points to; this one holds only the path. Nothing here is adopted.

## 1. In one page

- **v0.1.0 is temper's first public release:** a person downloads one
  binary, runs `temper box` with their model, ChatGPT or Claude, and
  their forge, a Forgejo the box owns or GitHub, tells temper how they
  work, and temper runs that work. Its aim is the reference case
  (`reference-case.md`): a goal from a person's draft, produced and
  landed on temper the way fabrig develops itself
  (`reference-code-production.md`, `archive/reference-landing.md`). It may land
  a little narrower, close to it, with the rest in v0.2.0.
- **Its bar is a ladder of stories on real backends** (section 2),
  from the playground's requests up to the reference case, each walked
  through `temper ctl`: ChatGPT with an owned Forgejo first, then
  GitHub and Claude. Real backends lead; fakes hold what the real runs
  find.
- **A rung is an acceptance story** (section 3): what a person does and
  what must hold (`../../development/acceptance.md`). The same story,
  unchanged, is the acceptance.
- **QA after landing runs on real backends too** (section 3).
- **Every finding is weighed against the happy path** (section 4): one
  on v0.1.0's happy path, the ladder, is fixed with a test in the lowest
  world that reproduces it; the rest waits until v0.1.0 is out.
- **The parts** (section 5) each have their design elsewhere: the
  reference drafts, configuring, upgrades, the playground in the browser,
  public reading, documentation for agents, the UI, the TUI's first
  pass, the whole story, the command line, the site.
- **The whole story** (`whole-story.md`, 11-08): temper as a person's
  one front end, at every point of the spectrum: its pages on the web,
  the phone and the terminal, standing roles, what reaches a person,
  and work that follows another project. All of it is in v0.1.0, with
  two stories beside the ladder, E on a box and V, the vsr pilot.
- **Left out** (section 6): `temper open`'s line client, models and forges
  beyond the four, our own development on dev.fabrig.ai, TLS between
  hosts, hearth.
- **The reference case** (`reference-case.md`, 11-02) is the top
  rung's story, frozen for v0.1.0 (section 7).
- **The order** is section 8; what done means, section 9.

## 2. The ladder

### 2.1 The rungs

| Rung | Story |
|---|---|
| 0, the ground | a box whose owned Forgejo is set up through `ctl`; the held ChatGPT account moved in, one chat turn, the account moved out |
| A, the playground | the playground's three suggested requests (`../protocol/setup/playground.md`) on a copy of `playground/widget` on the box's forge: each answered, and "Add CSV export to widget" delegated, pushed, passed by CI and an agent's review, and landed |
| B0, the green light | A, with a question the delegate asks and the person's answer (`reference-case.md`, steps 9 and 18) |
| B1, the repair | B0, with a gate the first push fails, and the change's repair (`reference-case.md`, step 23) |
| B2, two delegates | B1, with an advisor's report and two delegates, one depending on the other (`reference-case.md`, steps 2, 8 and 10) |

| W, the browser | A and B walked through the web client instead of `ctl`, once the user has taken their pages |

- **B0 to B2 are on the playground's repository,** each adding one
  mechanism of the reference case, cited in the table. A widens the
  playground's walk to its suggested requests, so that the playground's scripts and
  the live runs tell one story, and a live transcript can become a
  script.
- **A rung opens when the one below passes on real backends.** Its fake
  twin follows from its passing run's transcripts
  (`../testing/real-loop.md`, the ladder's twins) rather than coming
  first: the fake model says only what it is scripted to, so on fakes a
  story stops at its plumbing.
- **temper through `ctl`, the forge through its API,** as every pass
  of QA after landing does (`../../development/qa.md`, sections 2, 4
  and 6): a finding is temper not saying where to look, the two
  accounts disagreeing, or temper stale past its bound; reading the
  forge is none.

### 2.2 The backends

- **The first pair is ChatGPT with an owned Forgejo:** every rung
  climbs on it first.
- **GitHub and Claude follow a rung behind.** A rung that passes on the
  first pair is walked again with GitHub for the forge, connected and
  then managed (`github.md`), and with Claude for the model, Claude
  Code on a person's own login (`../protocol/llm.md`). Each walk's
  failures are findings like any other.
- **C runs with both plans,** Claude's and ChatGPT's, as the case has
  them.
- **One holder per login:** a subscription's login refreshes, so it has
  one holder at a time, moved into each run's box and exported back
  before the box stops. Live runs on one login go one at a time.

## 3. Climbing a rung

- **A story per rung,** in `../../development/acceptance.md`: what a
  person does and what must hold, on the rung's backends, with the
  login's move in and out.
- **The story is driven through `temper ctl`,** as
  `../../development/qa.md` says, from a fresh box, changing nothing
  but its own deployment and repository.
- **Its fixes make it pass:** the rung's work ends when the same story,
  unchanged, passes. It fixes what the story needs, and leaves a finding
  outside it to section 4.
- **A rerun that passes is the rung's pass,** in one sitting. A rung that fails after passing has a finding,
  never a retry.
- **Then it holds:** the passing story becomes a live-tier story, as
  the owned-forge landing did (`../testing/live-tier.md`), run before
  the release and whenever a change touches it; its fake twin goes into
  the worlds.
- **QA after landing** climbs `../../development/qa.md`'s rungs (its
  section 3) on real backends too: fakes, a real model, GitHub, one
  live run at a time on a login.

## 4. Findings

- **Each names its reproduction** from a fresh box, what was expected
  and what was seen.
- **v0.1.0's happy path is the ladder:** its stories, and each part's
  bar in section 5, used as intended, on the four backends. A security
  hole, lost data and a flake that fails a gate count as on it.
- **A finding on the happy path is fixed before the release;** any
  other waits until v0.1.0 is out.
- **A rung's own findings** are on the path by definition.
- **Its fix carries a test in the lowest world that reproduces it,**
  with the real run's transcript as a fake model's script where the
  model's turn matters (`../testing/real-loop.md`); then the rung is walked again.
- **The tool's friction counts:** a place where a story stalls, is
  guessed at or needs the source to get on is a finding against
  `temper ctl` or the help, since people's agents drive temper the same
  way.

## 7. The reference case

- **`reference-case.md`** (11-02, from sessions with the user) walks
  one small goal from a draft to cleared through the reference's code
  production and landing: five acts, 28 steps, one repository, condensed
  from a real run of `archive/playground-reset.md`. It is the top rung's story.
- **On real backends:** both plans, as the case has them; the workers
  on one machine; real time, where fakes scale it down. The person's
  words are a script, typed through `temper ctl`.
- **On a real-world deployment,** the user's word of 2026-11-04: a bare
  engine whose first run the person configures, not `temper box`, which
  stays the quick try of one forge and one model. Rung D walks on it too.
- **Frozen for v0.1.0:** the process keeps changing, and a moving top
  rung would never pass. Later changes wait for the next edition.
- **The aim is the whole case.** v0.1.0 may stop a little short of it,
  leaving to v0.2.0 the acts whose gaps are not closed in time; which,
  is decided as C climbs.
