# tier-controls: a tier held by a person, bisected reds, verdicts on the forge, and a stall that says why

A project's tiers after landing (`../domain/after-landing.md`) are how it learns what a landing broke that its gates don't run. Four gaps make them hard to run for long: a tier can't be paused without leaving its landings cleared by a run that tested nothing, a red over a long range is probed landing by landing, a verdict is seen nowhere on the forge, and a change or landing whose CI never reports is held with no reason from its pending jobs. This request closes them, from the user's word of 2026-11-14. **Run, unless marked read.**

## What's wanted, ranked. Drop from the bottom if it passes four tasks, and say what you dropped

1. **A tier held and released by a person.** `temper ctl` acts that hold a repository's tier and release it, beside the release after runner faults that `../domain/after-landing.md`, section 3 has. While held, the tier starts no run; a run in flight is let finish and judged, or dropped as a cancelled run is; its landings stay verifying, and its bound counts, so past it they end landed and uncleared (`../domain/after-landing.md`, section 6). A release starts the next run at the branch's newest tip, as a move would. The hold says who held it and why, in the tier's words, and survives a restart. Stories in the forge world: a hold with a run in flight; a hold past the bound; a release covering the landings made meanwhile; a restart while held.
2. **Bisection over a red range,** as `../domain/after-landing.md`, 5.2, step 4 says: the failing tests probed at the middle landing whose key changed, then the half that holds the boundary, until one landing owns each failure, in at most `ceil(log2(n))` probes for n such landings. Its status's departure (linear probes) closes. Stories: a range of eight landings, one breaking, found in three probes; two failures owned by two landings; a landing whose key equals the one before it skipped.
3. **Verdicts on the forge,** as `../domain/after-landing.md`, section 9 says: the status `temper/verdict/<tier>` on the run's tip, success, failure with the blamed tests counted, or pending while the tier is held, linked to the run; and one comment per tier verdict on each member's pull request, "cleared by run N", or "red: tests X, Y" and, once one lands, "repaired by #M". Keyed as temper's other comments. Stories: a green run's status and comments; a red's, then its repair's.
4. **A stall's reasons,** as `ci-on-forgejo-17.md`, section 3 says: before a change or a landing is held because its CI or its gate never reported, it reads each pending job and names it in the hold, `waiting` for a runner with its labels, `blocked`, or `running` at its step. A tier's run held past its bound says the same. Stories: a job no runner takes, its labels named; one blocked; one running past the stall.

## Not in this plan

A turn landing that is due and never runs (`turn-first-landing.md`, section 1). What a project's tiers or gates run. Batches. Webhooks.

**Tests stay cheap:** a new case of one behaviour is a row of an existing table test. Failing tests first. **A plan runs the focused and fuzzy suites alone and is complete when it lands (`AGENTS.md`, 5).** If a fix needs a field no store keeps today, the store's version moves and its patch comes with it: upgrades are supported (`redeploys.md`). `walk = false`.
