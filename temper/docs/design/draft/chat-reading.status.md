# chat-reading: status

2026-11-08. Where the design lags or departs from [chat-reading.md](chat-reading.md).

- **Design lags:** 2, *Opened*, and 5, *No "Older"* and *"Latest ↓"*, in the TUI: `../client/tui/pages/chat.md`, 1, draws a run's line without its rows, and reads only the window a chat's page opens with, since skein's screen reports no log edge yet; its section 11 defers both, with "Latest ↓", to temper/tui. PgUp and PgDn page the current window and end following resumes when the person reaches its end; neither loads another page.
- **Design departs:**
  - 5: *Keys, in the terminal:* `../client/pages/chat/conversation.md`, 2: the terminal reads lines, never raw keys. `up` and `down`, or the escape sequences PgUp and PgDn make, followed by Enter, read earlier or later lines and print the whole window again. The plan `chat-reading-c` decided, under its request's "take the nearest thing".
  - 5: *Keys, from anywhere on the page:* skein's `ui/web/README.md`, section 5: a component holding the focus keeps PgUp and PgDn as well, beside the menu or dialog the section excepts, since a component's keys are its own. The plan `chat-reading-c` decided.
  - 5: *The same rule opens a chat:* `../client/pages/chat/conversation.md`, 2, *Opening history*: in a browser it holds, the log's measure reading on from where the opening's reads stop. A shell with no view, the terminal or the TUI, measures nothing, so the opening's reads stand in for the screen: they read earlier until the history's end or until the lines held weigh a screen and a half of an 80 by 24 terminal, 2,880 bytes by jig's weight of a line, and in the terminal `up` reads the next. An empty page with an older cursor reads on, so that it isn't stranded. The plan `chat-reading-c` decided, under its request's "take the nearest thing"; `red-82-chat-opening` took the stand-in from one page to that weight.
  - 5: *The newest are never lost:* jig's `client/domain/client/conversations.md`: the lines that go are those furthest from the page just read, not from where the person reads, since a thread measures no view. A window holds far more than a screen, so what goes lies out of view either way; seen by reading. The plan `chat-reading-c` decided.
  - 5: *"Latest ↓":* `../client/pages/chat/conversation.md`, 2: on an agent's page, the person sending does not go to the end, where the section has it: its log has no end request, which a chat's page alone counts. A press there reaches the end a page at a time, the log following, where a chat's reads the end at once. The plan `chat-reading-c` cut an agent's page out of the pill, `chat-reading-b` landed one on it, and the close took what was built. No reason is recorded for sending.
  - 5: *A window bounded by size:* `../client/view.md`, 4, and `../client/pages/chat/conversation.md`, 2: `lines` stays for jig's reference UI and tools, whose trees are priced by rows. temper sets it to `document_bytes / LINE_ROOM`, 16,384, past what its bytes hold, so it never bounds the conversation first. The plan `chat-reading-c` decided, taking the draft's alternative.
  - 5: *A window bounded by size,* in the browser tier alone: `../client/web/testing.md`, 3: the tier's bundle holds the window to 32,768 bytes, a thirty-second of `document_bytes`, and its `long_text` and `turn_text` with it, so that the long reading story passes three windows and recovers both ends. The unanchored position story stays within one window. A person's bundle keeps the whole bound; its manual check remains separate (`../client/web/README.md`, section 3). The repair's cost at that window is unmeasured (`../client/web/testing.status.md`, 3). The plan `browser-budget` decided, from its request's second lever, and `browser-reading` took the window to its `long_text` floor.
  - 5: *In every browser:* `../client/pages/chat/conversation.md`, 2: the browser tier runs Chromium alone, including the position story with `overflow-anchor: none`, the nearest a run comes to Safari. Firefox and Safari themselves are read, not run; the user reads the position staying put on a real chat. The plan `chat-reading-c` decided, under its request's "take the nearest thing".
  - 2: *Its time:* `../client/pages/README.md`: a committed turn says only when it ended, so a run is measured from the line before its first turn, which a run of one turn has too; at a window's head or for a turn's second run, from its first turn's end, often no time at all. It holds the model's writing of any text that opened the turn. Storing a turn's start would change a stored format and a protocol (`../client/README.md`, section 5). The plan `chat-reading-b` decided, under its request's "take the nearest thing".
  - 2: *A failed call:* `../client/pages/README.md`: "1 failed" stands in the line's description, before the time, not after a comma in its name, so the time takes the signal's colour with it, where this section has ", 1 failed" alone in that colour: skein's summary holds one name and one description. While a run is out its description is the call under way, so the line says no "1 failed" until it ends, where the draft has the same line add it; no reason is recorded. The plan `look-round-6` decided.
  - 2: *A run's end:* `../client/pages/README.md`: a document's write ends a run too, and any other drawn line: its line stays where it falls, so a run can't span it without moving calls past it. The plan `look-round-6` decided.
  - 2: *A row a call:* jig's `client/domain/view.md`, section 9: jig's reference UI keeps a folded line a call. It knows no tool's words, and a chat's line is temper's view. The plan `chat-reading-b` decided.
  - 2: *What stays apart:* `../client/pages/task.md`, 2: on an agent's page a `delegate` call is a row in a run and counts in it, since a delegation's card is the chat's. The plan `look-round-6` decided.
  - 2: *The terminal:* `../client/pages/README.md`: a print has no opening, so the terminal prints a run's rows under its line whatever its state, a row a call, where the draft has them opened: there a run is more than one short line. No reason is recorded. The plan `look-round-6` decided.
  - 3: *Drawn once:* `skein/docs/design/ui/markdown.md`, and `../client/pages/README.md`: the page reads the whole text again at each delivery, and a closed block's nodes come out the same, so it stays on the page unchanged. Nothing keeps a block read, in the browser or the TUI (`../client/tui/pages/README.md`). A delivery that makes the view stale rebuilds and compares the whole semantic tree, while unchanged builds are gated (`../client/pages/chat/conversation.md`, 2). Retaining parsed blocks remains a new mechanism, deferred in that document's section 11. The plan `chat-reading-a` decided under its request's "take the nearest thing"; `browser-budget` found no justified small fix. Its whole-bound samples show 27.0–38.4 ms of main-thread tasks per delivery, not a Markdown-only cost (`temper/docs/development/browser-budget.md`). The draft's read-once requirement remains unmet.
  - 3: *Whole:* an answer past the engine's retained bytes (`views.text_bytes`, 65,536) arrives whole only to a page following it from its start; a page opened later shows the retained end until the turn commits. One delivery's wire field stops at the same 65,536, so holding more needs a chunked delivery (`../client/README.md`, section 5). The plan `chat-reading-a` decided, likewise. A gap between suffix snapshots also leaves a thread's held prefix incomplete, even below `long_text`, until a full-origin snapshot or commitment replaces it (jig's `client/domain/client/conversations.md`). The plan `ui-pgup` keeps that prefix rather than replacing the words being read with a cut tail; asking for missing provisional bytes remains deferred in jig's `client/domain/client/README.md`, section 15.
  - 4: *The order changes:* jig's `client/domain/client/README.md`, section 11: an outcome's line comes before inputs, not after them. A note's input can fill the budget, and the chat's document line and "Show" read that call's outcome. An outcome's line is bounded by `text`, so it costs the input little. The plan `chat-reading-a` decided, under its request's "an outcome's first line breaks a card".
  - 4: *A delegate's card:* jig's `client/domain/client/conversations.md`: every result's summary stays whole, priced last, not only those a card reads: the transcript knows no tool's words, and a whole summary is what the agent's page, the tools and a brief read. The plan `chat-reading-a` decided, likewise.
  - 4: *Whatever their results weigh:* jig's `client/domain/client/README.md`, section 13: a turn past `document_bytes` on the wire (1,048,576 bytes) reads as too large, with no calls' names. Reading it needs a page past the client's document bound (`../client/README.md`, section 5). The plan `chat-reading-a` decided, under its request's "take the nearest thing".
