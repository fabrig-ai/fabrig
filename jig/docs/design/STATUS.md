# Status

2026-11-07. Where jig's code lags or departs from its design, from the
status files below. Agents in a host's own process are the widest gap:
no root composes `smith-inline-agent`, so the engine's own slots, a
hub's agent capability and the tools a worker begins with their calls
are not in jig (`README.md`, `domain/README.md`, `domain/engine.md`,
`domain/hosts.md`, `domain/root.md`, `protocol/README.md`,
`protocol/channel.md`, `protocol/llm.md`, `protocol/service.md`). TLS
between hosts and contained process trees for workers' agents are not
built (the protocol documents), nor the store's retention, rehearsal and
going back (`protocol/store.md`, `protocol/client.md`). An
application's own kinds of setting, a project's policy in a change of
settings and a settings revision's `Restore` are built, but a proposal
of settings waits for the deployment's owners whatever it names, so a
project's owner alone reads no row of it, and the
core world's restore stories are ignored (`domain/engine.md`,
`domain/connectors.md`). A run's fresh settings proposal replaces its own
pending one atomically, preserving it on refusal; at the deployment
proposal bound, refusal remains the default (`domain/tasks.md`, section 9;
`domain/engine.md`, 17.6). Numbered model settings, catalogue-derived
charter versions and endpoint bounds at hello are built; provider-list reads and per-account
narrowing remain deferred (`domain/engine.md`, 16 and 17.11).
Standard admits batches of 32, charters of 262,144 bytes and outcomes of
131,072; a change body's bound follows its contract's words. Message
rendering room remains deferred (`domain/hosts.md`, sections 8 and 12;
`protocol/configuration.md`, section 6).
A start does not check an application's declared
kinds against its limits, and a project's deletion as a whole has no price
against `journal.writes` (`domain/engine.md`).
An ended settings acceptance reaches its retained decision with the
running-work choice. An automatically replaced task proposal has no
retained decision and answers unknown; retaining one and naming its
decider remain deferred (`client/domain/contract.md`, 3.2, 4 and 11).
Of the acts, `Hold`, `Move` under another task, `Succeed`, successors,
markers, a drain and a conversation's questions lag (`domain/people.md`,
`domain/tasks.md`, `domain/engine.md`, `client/domain/contract.md`); of
the client, a committed turn's objects,
`me`'s amendment bound, a service's root, the one-shot
mode and the drawing family (`client/domain/`); and `ops` standalone, with its
service and its connectors' protocols (`examples.md`). `RemoveProject`
is offered in no project's document (`client/domain/contract.md`).
Public lines carry a goal's spend alone, no budget, and distinct endings
(`domain/people.md`, `client/domain/contract.md`). Titles retain the user's
publication decision, with temper's warning in its help and completed
answer; an acknowledgement before publication is deferred
(`domain/people.md`, 13.3). Public reading's remaining
departure is the public index's memory fit check (`protocol/configuration.md`);
ended goals across a restart or before publication are deferred
(`domain/people.md`, section 12). The test root is not in `ops`'s
shape, which the user deferred, 2026-11-06, and temper keeps its own message words and
its admission's yes or no, where the user's request of 2026-11-05 has it
use jig's (`domain/root.md`, `domain/hosts.md`).
A task given no LLM choice takes the first model it can use at its run's
check, but no walk on record has a real box run on it (`domain/accounts.md`).
Its template preview can differ from its task's next-run model across a worker
restart with mixed program and direct accounts (`client/domain/contract.md`,
`domain/engine.md`).
A transcript's load refused for want of a slot counts as a failure, where
room should pause its task (`domain/engine.md`). A note's range names the
revision it read `recalled`, where the `note-parts` request of 2026-11-05 has
`revision` (`domain/engine.md`). Four of the worlds' files name a fixture again,
where the `debt-24` request of 2026-11-05 has each named once. The core's
observer updates keyed evidence from durable commit rows and keeps complete
ordered snapshots, with whole rebuilding for cold reads and bulk changes.
Views are made only for readers; further observation-cost work and proof
that the focused suite fits 30 CPU s remain
(`domain/testing.md`, sections 5, 9 and 10). Typed core row names and shared
setup avoid repeated formatting and construction; protocol wires skip idle
polls and parse only new bytes, and due agents check holds and credentials
before joint account and host selection. The seven store sweeps run in fuzzy
with the focused cuts' checks; the focused stray story selects a directory
creation where `domain/testing.md`, 9 requires its first removal. Whole-suite savings and
fuzzy's fit remain unproved (`domain/testing.md`, sections 7, 9 and 10;
`protocol/store.md`, section 14). The channel's root checks at every
step cover its fuzzy stories; the other stories can miss transient grant
values between arrival and end checks (`protocol/channel.md`, sections 12
and 14).
A Finite report can deliver when its policy and workspace permit it,
keeping its report contract; a procedure on its requester's resource can
step beside that requester's writing run (`domain/hosts.md`, section 8;
`domain/connectors.md`, section 11). The shell authority of a run without
delivery still departs (`domain/hosts.md`).
The client's link exposes a watch's Busy refusal through retries and clears
it on recovery; distinguishing its capacity cause remains deferred
(`client/domain/client.md`, section 18).
Printed-link sign-ins carry used, expired and unknown reasons through the
client; malformed browser secrets take that same exchange. The history of
eight digests is memory-only, so forgotten links are unknown; longer history and
fallback to a live cookie remain deferred (`protocol/client.md`, sections
8 and 15; `client/domain/client.md`, sections 12 and 18).
Decoded turns retain model and effort outside the text budget, and a
thread retains its last named completion and says whether announced
activity has been read. A run with no readable named turn can leave an
older model; latest-run metadata independent of turns is deferred
(`client/domain/client.md`, sections 10 and 18).

`client/domain/contract.md`, `client/domain/testing.md`, `client/domain/view.md`, `domain/hosts.md`, `domain/people.md`, `domain/root.md`, `domain/testing.md`,
`protocol/README.md`, `protocol/browser.md`, `protocol/client.md`, `protocol/configuration.md`, `protocol/service.md`, `protocol/store.md`). `domain/core.md`,
`domain/providers.md` and `client/domain/README.md` have no gap of their
