# Status

2026-11-09. Where jig's code lags or departs from its design, from the
status files below. Browser-approved sign-in is built through the wire,
clients' component, HTTP mapping, configuration and linked and unlinked
clients. Only the secret's take makes the approving browser's party's
sign-in; bearers cannot read or decide approvals. An admitted take's
reservation can outlive expiry (`protocol/client.status.md`, section 8).
Approval between hosts and listing sign-ins remain deferred
(`protocol/client.md`, section 15). Both profiles hold returning
redirects of 16,384 bytes and a browser's request head of 24,593;
configuration refuses a driver that holds less than its parts. An
over-long listener answer fails its own sign-in at once with a specific
page; carrying its length and bound beyond `SignInAgain` to the
application's page remains deferred (`protocol/configuration.md`,
sections 6 and 9). The deployment's sole-owner read is
built, including
during restore, answering none with no owner, several or an unreadable
roster (`domain/people.md`, section 4). Agents in a host's own process
are the widest gap:
no root composes `smith-inline-agent`, so the engine's own slots, a
hub's agent capability and the tools a worker begins with their calls
are not in jig (`README.md`, `domain/README.md`, `domain/engine.md`,
`domain/hosts.md`, `domain/root.md`, `protocol/README.md`,
`protocol/channel.md`, `protocol/llm.md`, `protocol/service.md`). TLS
between hosts and contained process trees for workers' agents are not
built (the protocol documents). The reference test service still aborts
workers without first cancelling their runs; temper's service implements
that ordering (`protocol/service.md`, section 10). An orderly engine stop
settles its in-process agent claims as interrupted before draining,
keeping their committed work and spending no try; crash recovery still
settles claims as lost or not started (`domain/engine.md`, sections 6
and 6.1; `domain/tasks.md`, 5.2). A worker stopped in another process
still spends an agent try on its Shutdown cancel; changing that remains
deferred (`protocol/service.md`, section 9). A worker's link says why
it was lost, on each side, its silence counts bytes, and a closing
connection is aborted at the stall deadline, so a worker lost fifty
times each way is taken back each time; the fifty-loss row leaves out
a full cap and the worker's refusal (`protocol/channel.md`, 9, 10.1,
10.2 and 12; `domain/hosts.md`, 6.6). Each side's link takes its
stream's room; the authenticated opening refuses unequal `io.output`
values and names both. A resumed start's committed calls are fitted to
the engine's room first, the newest answers whole and the older cut to
words naming their bytes, a start still past it fails its run with its bytes and the
room, the link and its pings going on, and a side whose other frames,
or the engine's smallest resumed start, could pass it is not made;
making a start past it by its other parts run is an open question
(`protocol/channel.md`, 5.2, 9, 11 and 14). The store's retention,
rehearsal and going back are not built (`protocol/store.md`,
`protocol/client.md`). A start whose store fails a link refuses,
naming the record and what it links to, and no route writes a link to
a person the core does not hold; the core world, killed at any file
operation of a decision and again during its restart, starts from a
whole one, but a snapshot racing commits and several commits in flight
are proven in the store's world alone (`domain/engine.md`, 5.1 and 6;
`domain/accounts.md`; `protocol/store.md`, 14). An
application's own kinds of setting, a project's policy in a change of
settings and a settings revision's `Restore` are built, but a proposal
of settings waits for the deployment's owners whatever it names, so a
project's owner alone reads no row of it, and the
core world's restore stories are ignored; a setting reset to a default
its connector neither reports nor names as dropped keeps its state at the
next start (`domain/engine.md`, `domain/connectors.md`). A run's fresh settings proposal replaces its own
pending one atomically, preserving it on refusal; at the deployment
proposal bound, refusal remains the default (`domain/tasks.md`, section 9;
`domain/engine.md`, 17.6). Derived run-wait refusals carry admitted hosts'
bound shortfalls and required/offered figures through the v19 client
wire, including a standing hello's figures and every charter-bound
shortfall's charter (`domain/engine.md`, section 8;
`client/domain/contract.md`, 5.1; `protocol/client.md`, section 3).
