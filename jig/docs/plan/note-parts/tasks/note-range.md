# `note` takes a line range, in jig

## Goal

A run revises a note by a line range, so that a body past what one call holds can be built in parts. jig's machine, core, store and tool
hold it, every refusal says what to do next, and a revision stores the whole result.

Acceptance:

- `jig_domain_notes::LineRange`, `splice`, `Refusal::PastEnd` and `PastBound`, `Event::WriteLines`, `new_refusal` and
  `core::NamedAction::NoteLines`, as `notes/contracts.md` has them, `splice` beside `line_change` in `lines.rs`.
- `core-v6.schema`'s two refusals, regenerated by the schema's own tool, with goldens by their drift test (`rg -n SKEIN_CODEGEN_WRITE
  jig/crates/jig-protocol-store jig/tests` shows it).
- `jig-protocol-tools`: `note`'s schema in `declarations.rs` gains `first` and `remove`; `notes.rs` decodes the range form by the
  contract's rules, to `NamedAction::NoteLines`; `json.rs`' list of names gains `first` and `remove`, in its order; `render.rs` says the
  contract's words.
- The tests below, each having bitten.
- `jig/docs/design/domain/engine.status.md`: the sentence on `note`'s line range goes, that sentence alone.

## Inputs

- `jig/docs/plan/note-parts/notes/common.md`, `jig/docs/plan/note-parts/notes/contracts.md`.
- `jig/docs/design/domain/engine.md`, section 10: *Whole, or by a line range*, *Stored whole*, *Refusals say what to do next*, *Its answer
  says what changed*, *A body's bound*.

## Owns and may change

Owns, as `files` lists them.

May also change:

- **jig's readers,** each only for what the compiler forces: every exhaustive match on `notes::Refusal`, `notes::Event` or
  `core::NamedAction` that `heavy cargo check --tests` shows, `..` or a new arm, in `jig/crates/**`, `jig/tests/**` and `jig/testing/**`.
  `Event::Write` and `NamedAction::Note` keep their fields, so their literals don't change. `jig/tests/notes/src/referee.rs`, `Intent`:
  `WriteLines` is a write with `recalled: Some(_)`.
- **temper's one forced place,** `temper/crates/temper-engine-smith/src/answers.rs`, `CallAnswer::NoteRefused`'s arm: it says the
  contract's three refusals, `Moved` included, as a required field's lines. `note_written` and temper's decoder are `note-tool`'s: stay out
  of them. Its unit tests stay as they are.

Options, each pre-approved:

- **`splice`'s scan** is yours, in the strict subset: one pass, no index. `line_change`'s `front` and `count` may be shared.
- **The fuzzy notes world:** if `jig/tests/notes/tests/suite/fuzzy/simulation.rs` takes `WriteLines` as one more arm of its generator, add it;
  if it would need a model of bodies it doesn't have, leave it and say so.
- **A change of meaning:** `render.rs`' words for `Moved` and for a written note change. Run `jig-protocol-transcript`'s and
  `jig-protocol-tools`' tests. If another test reads the old words, change its literal.

## Steps

1. **`lines.rs`,** with its tests beside `line_change`'s in `jig-domain-notes/src/tests.rs`: every row of the contract's table, as
   `a_range_replaces_its_lines`, `a_range_inserts_when_it_removes_nothing`, `an_append_after_a_final_newline_keeps_the_texts_ending`,
   `an_append_to_a_body_with_no_final_newline_joins_no_lines`, `text_with_no_final_newline_joins_no_lines_before_what_follows`,
   `removing_the_last_lines_keeps_the_rest`, `a_blank_line_counts_as_a_line`, `an_empty_body_takes_an_append_and_nothing_else`,
   `first_zero_and_ranges_past_the_end_say_the_lines`, `a_range_on_u32_max_does_not_overflow`,
   `a_result_at_the_bound_fits_and_one_past_says_its_bytes`; and a small generated check,
   `a_range_matches_a_list_of_lines`, against a model of a body as a list of lines, its result's `line_change` counts included.
2. **The machine,** `domain.rs`, tests in `tests.rs`: `a_range_write_saves_the_whole_body_at_the_next_revision`,
   `a_range_answers_as_line_change_does`, `a_stale_range_says_the_revision`, `a_range_on_another_scope_says_the_revision`,
   `a_range_on_no_entry_is_missing`, `a_refused_range_saves_nothing`, `a_range_keeps_references_unless_it_names_some`,
   `a_range_writes_the_description_it_names`, `a_range_at_the_last_revision_is_exhausted`, `a_body_past_its_bound_says_so_in_either_form`.
3. **The core and the store:** `NoteLines` through `routing/run/calls.rs`, `named_tool_kind` and `refusals.rs`; `routing/effect/note.rs`'s
   arms. `jig-protocol-store`'s fixtures take `NoteRefused` of `Moved`, `PastEnd` and `PastBound`.
4. **The worlds:**
   - `jig/tests/notes/tests/suite/lines.rs`, a new `mod` in its `main.rs`: `a_stale_revision_says_the_current_one`,
     `a_range_past_the_end_says_the_line_count`, `a_result_past_the_bound_says_so`, each recalling the note after to show it as it was;
     `a_note_built_in_parts_recalls_whole`, and `parts_survive_a_restart` on the shape of `restart.rs`.
   - `jig/tests/core/tests/stories/note_room.rs`, `a_note_built_in_parts_is_recalled_and_read_whole`: `NamedAction::Note`, then
     `NamedAction::NoteLines`, each under `core.call_answer_bytes`, to a body at least twice one call's text; `Recall` whole;
     `reads::Read::ScopedNotes` whole; the revisions in order.
5. **The tool,** tests in `jig-protocol-tools/src/tests.rs`: `a_line_range_decodes_to_note_lines`, `a_line_range_needs_its_revision`,
   `first_and_remove_come_together`, `first_zero_is_out_of_range`, `a_note_without_first_decodes_as_today`, and the words,
   `each_note_refusal_says_what_to_do_next`.
6. `heavy cargo nextest run -p jig-domain-notes -p jig-protocol-tools -p jig-protocol-store -p jig-protocol-transcript`; the worlds
   by name, `rg -n '^name' jig/tests/notes/Cargo.toml jig/tests/core/Cargo.toml` naming their packages; `heavy cargo nextest run -p
   jig-domain-core`; `heavy cargo check --tests -p temper-engine-smith -p temper-engine-domain -p temper-service`.
7. **Check,** the implementer's text, then push.
