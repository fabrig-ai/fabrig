# v0.1.0

Draft, 2026-10-26, from a session with the user. What temper v0.1.0
is, how we will know it is done, and the order of the work that gets
it there. It replaces the split between the reference case on fakes
first and real backends opportunistically with one bar on real
backends, and takes in the parts of `first-release.md` the release
needs. Nothing here is adopted.

## 1. In one page

- **v0.1.0 is temper's first public release:** a person downloads one
  binary, runs `temper box` with their model, ChatGPT or Claude, and
  their forge, a Forgejo the box owns or GitHub, tells temper how they
  work, and temper runs that work: chats delegate changes, which pass
  CI and review and land.
- **Its aim is the updated reference case:** temper's own development
  run on temper. It may land a little narrower, close to it, with the
  rest in v0.2.0 (section 8).
- **Its bar is a ladder of stories on real backends** (section 2),
  from the playground's requests up to the reference case, each walked
  through `temper ctl`: ChatGPT with an owned Forgejo first, then
  GitHub and Claude. Real backends lead; fakes hold what the real runs
  find.
- **A rung is climbed as a focused QA story** (section 3): a brief a QA
  session walks, a report of what it found, an owner who fixes it, and
  the same brief, rerun unchanged, as the acceptance.
- **Findings go one way** (section 4): to the owner of what each names,
  fixed with a test in the lowest world that reproduces it.
- **Four tracks** (section 5): the ladder, and configuring by talking,
  are the agents'; the UI and the site are the user's. QA after landing
  moves to real backends now.
- **The release itself** needs the store's disk format frozen, the
  download and the guide (section 6).
- **Left out** (section 7): the terminal client, the playground in the
  browser, public reading, TLS between hosts, hearth.
- **The reference case is updated once,** to the process of 10-26, and
  frozen for v0.1.0 (section 8).
- **The order** is section 9; what done means, section 10.

## 2. The ladder

- **B0 to B2 are `qa-live.md`'s rungs.** A widens that draft's rung A
  to the playground's requests, so that the playground's scripts and
  the live runs tell one story, and a live transcript can become a
  script.
- **A rung opens when the one below passes on real backends.** Its fake
  twin follows from its passing run's transcripts (`qa-live.md`, 8)
  rather than coming first: the fake model says only what it is
  scripted to, so on fakes a story stops at its plumbing.
- **Through `ctl` alone** (`qa-live.md`, 4.4): what the tool can't show
  is a finding, never a reason to read the forge or the store instead.

### 2.2 The backends

- **The first pair is ChatGPT with an owned Forgejo:** every rung
  climbs on it first.
- **GitHub and Claude follow a rung behind.** A rung that passes on the
  first pair is walked again with GitHub for the forge, connected and
  then managed (`github.md`), and with Claude for the model, Claude
  Code on a person's own login (`../protocol/llm.md`). Each walk's
  failures are findings like any other.
- **C runs with both plans,** Claude's and ChatGPT's, as the case has
  them, on the forge temper's own development uses.
- **One holder per login:** a subscription's login refreshes, so it has
  one holder at a time, moved into each run's box and exported back
  before the box stops. Live runs on one login go one at a time.

## 3. Climbing a rung

- **A brief per rung,** in the shape of the focused QA stories of
  10-26: the scenario, the backends, the held login's move in and out,
  and a report with a verdict, the walk with its times, how hard the
  tool was to drive, the tooling it lacked, ranked, and each finding
  with its reproduction from a fresh box.
- **A QA session walks it,** as agents' `roles/qa.md` says, changing
  nothing but its scratch.
- **An owner makes it pass:** a mission per rung, or per two small
  ones, whose end is the same brief, rerun unchanged, passing. It fixes
  what the story needs, or hands a finding to the owner of what it
  names (section 4).
- **A rerun that passes is the rung's pass.** A seed fixes the lab's
  draws, not a real model's sampling, and the upper rungs last hours,
  so `qa-live.md`'s three seeds give way to one sitting. A rung that
  fails after passing has a finding, never a retry.
- **Then it holds:** the passing story becomes a live-tier story, as
  the owned-forge landing did (`../domain/testing.md`, 2.7), or a lab
  experiment, run before the release and whenever a change touches
  it; its fake twin goes into the worlds.

## 4. Findings

- **Each names its reproduction** from a fresh box, what was expected
  and what was seen.
- **It goes to the owner of what it names,** through the queue QA after
  landing already uses (agents' `docs/design/qa-flow.md`, 6): the
  rung's owner fixes what is its own, and the director routes the rest.
- **Its fix carries a test in the lowest world that reproduces it,**
  with the real run's transcript as a fake model's script where the
  model's turn matters (`qa-live.md`, 8); then the rung is walked again.
- **A ladder's finding is on v0.1.0's path** by definition; a corner the
  ladder never reaches waits.
- **The tool's friction counts:** where the QA session stalled, guessed
  or read source to get on is a finding against `temper ctl` or the
  guide, since people's agents drive temper the same way.

## 5. The tracks

### 5.1 The ladder (agents)

- **First:** it is the bar, and its findings decide much of the rest.
- **QA after landing moves to real backends now** (agents'
  `qa-flow.md`), beside the ladder: its passes take the held login in
  turn with the ladder's runs, one live run at a time. If that
  saturates the login, the director says so to the user.

### 5.2 Configuring by talking (agents)

- **`configuring.md`, whole.** Built or landing on 10-26: settings in the
  store and their revisions; `ctl`'s settings, proposals and decisions;
  temper's stock defaults; the documentation in the binary. Left: the

  the golden experiment, which becomes rung D.
- **The golden reads the updated case's process** (section 8), not the
  roles as `temper-on-temper.md` read them, so that C and D test one
  process.
- **The configuring pages** wait for the user's mockups.

### 5.3 The UI (the user)

- **The ladder says which pages v0.1.0 needs:** those its stories pass

- **A rung that passes through `ctl` is recorded as a journey** on the

- **W follows** the user's word on those pages.

### 5.4 The site (the user)

- `first-release.md`, section 3: static; the vision, temper with an

## 6. The release

- **The baseline at v0.1.0 is the store's disk format** (the user,
  10-26): its files and their framing, which every later version reads.
  What the records in it say, the configuration and everything else, a
  later version upgrades by itself as it starts, between versions, by
  `upgrades.md`'s patches. What `upgrades.md` designs beyond that is not
  v0.1.0's bar; its designer says which parts that leaves.
- **The download:** `temper box` as one Linux binary, built at the tag,
  with its checksum, among the forge's releases; macOS as far as
  `macos.md`'s quick try reaches, and marked so.
- **The guide** (`../../guide/`, `temper help`) walks rung A as the
  ladder does, on each pair of backends, and says what v0.1.0 leaves
  out.
- **dev.fabrig.ai is no bar:** it runs as it does, reset at will.

## 7. Left out

- **The terminal client** (`temper open`): the web client is v0.1.0's.
- **Models and forges beyond the four:** ChatGPT and Claude, Forgejo
  and GitHub.
- **From `first-release.md`:** the playground in the browser (8),
  public reading (5.3), temper's own development on dev.fabrig.ai
  (5.2).
- **TLS between hosts,** clusters across machines, and hearth.

## 8. The reference case, updated

- **Why:** written on 10-21, it tells that day's process. The process
  changed on 10-24 and again on 10-26:
  - designers between the director and the owners, one draft each,
    designed a part at a time;
  - QA after landing: a commit's `QA:` line, a pass on a cleared
    `main`, its findings routed to the owner of what they name;
  - tiers that run after landing and clear `main`;
  - landings across repositories as one set;
  - the user's waits as issues on the forge;
  - missions paused and resumed on the user's word, and parked for a
    machine's redeploy.
- **Mostly mechanics temper has:** a designer is one more level of
  delegation; a QA pass, a procedure woken by a landing's news; a wait
  on the user, the inbox or a forge issue. The update maps each, and
  names what is new.
- **On real backends:** both plans, as the case has them; the workers
  on one machine; real time, where fakes scale it down.
- **Frozen for v0.1.0** at the process of 10-26: it keeps changing, and
  a moving top rung would never pass. Later changes wait for the next
  case.
- **The aim is the whole case.** v0.1.0 may stop a little short of it,
  leaving to v0.2.0 the acts whose gaps are not closed in time; which,
  is decided as C climbs.
- **The user's draft,** updated in a session with them, as it was
  written; designers then take its deltas.

## 9. The order
