temper moves its pins from jig `d91a89c4`, skein `b5d3a2c3` and smith
`dfc0fe57` to jig's base, `a3b53546`, and jig's own pins of skein,
`f12f29d4`, and smith, `29fa97c9` (decisions.md, 4), and repairs what
they break: skein-ui took jig's tree, its fake person and its text

(`client.md`, 2; `protocol.md`, 3 and 5), and the terminal prints and

## Choices this plan makes

- **temper 01 pins jig's base, `a3b53546`,** not jig's `origin/main`:
  its Needs name no jig capability, and jig 01's renames, which may land
  meanwhile, are temper 02's.
- **The repin is one increment** (01.1): the pins move together (jig's
  crates at `a3b53546` need skein `f12f29d4`'s `skein-ui`), and it
  measured 146 counted lines, under the README's 200-line bound on a
  repin's repairs.
- **The printer's repair in 01.1 is the smallest that compiles:** its
  four `match node.role` gain skein-ui's fifteen new roles (88 of the 146
  lines), and 01.3 removes them with `render.rs`. 01.1 then lands with
  the goldens changed by jig's pages alone, and 01.3 with the goldens
  changed by skein's renderer alone.
- **01.3 takes the renderer and the command reader** of `skein-ui-text`
  (`ui-text.md`, 3, its first two bullets), which is what
  `findings/temper.md`, 3 counts (`render.rs` and `command.rs`, 741
  lines). The terminal's own loop, `terminal::Loop`, keeps its line
  buffer, its output and its print schedule; skein's
  `skein_ui_text::Terminal` (`ui-text.md`, 3, the third bullet; temper's
  `client.md`, 6.2), which owns them on skein's io, is not adopted here
  (Questions).
- **The status region** of the first line is jig's link part,
  `jig_view::regions::JigRegion::Link`, whose node already carries
  "Behind: waiting for current facts" while the page is behind
  (`crates/jig-view/src/frame.rs:15-22` at `a3b53546`); the printer's
  `Frame.behind` goes.
