# temper-13.s1 The one-process world's cost, at its cause

Added by ruling 112 (the designer's answer of 2026-10-19, point 5): the
one-process world is 40% of the worlds' sum below the domain. Temper
13.4 waits for it.

Scope: why the one-process world's three slowest stories take their
time, and a fix at that cause in the code or the test (README, Rules,
"No optimized test builds"): in `tests/one-process/tests/one_process.rs`,
`an_adopted_repository_gets_its_change_pushed_through_git` (0.865 s),
`a_signal_mid_run_drains_and_the_next_start_settles_the_claims` (0.247
s) and `a_person_signs_in_by_the_printed_link_and_a_chat_is_answered`
(0.243 s), from 08.2's measure.
Needs: nothing; it starts at once.
Budget: ten instrumented runs and 60 minutes; past it, raise it with
each run's finding.
Files: `tests/one-process/src/`, `tests/one-process/tests/one_process.rs`,
`testing/temper-fake-git/src/` (the README's Hotspots exception, ruling
112). The forge kit's temper-05.2 also changes `tests/one-process`: the
later landing rebases.
Shape:
- Measure by instrumentation, not `perf`: it is not installed here and
  `kernel.perf_event_paranoid` is 3. Per story, count `Outcome.iterations`
  and `procs.len()`, and sum `std::time::Instant` spans over the
  answer's candidates: `Referee::observe` (`src/referee.rs`, after each
  turn, over every process), the referee's per-commit `rows.clone_from`
  and `snapshot`, and fake git's per-invocation `Checkout` and tree
  clones (`answer.rs`, `program.rs`). Remove the diagnostics before the
  commit.
- Fix the largest share found, then the next while the budget lasts.
Test shape: no new test; each one-process and fake-git test asserts what
it asserts now.
Proof: each story's time before and after, idle, the median of three, in
the report with the cause found: `sh -c 'export
NEXTEST_DOUBLE_SPAWN=0; for i in 1 2 3; do cargo nextest run -p
temper-one-process-world --profile measure -j 1 >one-process-$i.log
2>&1; done'`; and `-p temper-fake-git` passes.
Size: at most ~150 lines changed.
Don't: touch Cargo's `[profile.*]`, the nextest profiles or a build
setting; move, cut or merge a test: the fix is at the cause (Scope);
drop or skip a referee check to save time: a check made cheaper checks
the same.
Removes: nothing.
